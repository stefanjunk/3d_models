# Model / Person / Property Release — Counsel Checklist

Use qualified local counsel, especially for minors, biometrics, medical/sensitive contexts, and international use.

- Identity and age/capacity; guardian authority
- Photographer/scanner/operator and copyright ownership
- Date, location, objects/persons/data captured
- 2D photo, video, voice, 3D scan, avatar, height map and biometric scope
- AI input, synthetic modification, model training and output use
- Product design, physical merchandise, digital file, advertising and portfolio use
- Commercial channels, territory, duration, sublicensing and contractors
- Name/likeness/endorsement rules and sensitive-use exclusions
- Property/location/museum/event access authority
- Depicted art, trademarks, designs and confidential material
- Compensation and additional approvals
- Privacy notice, lawful basis, data minimization, transfers, retention, security
- Withdrawal/objection/deletion handling and limits after lawful distribution
- Contact, governing law, signatures, guardian/witness if required
- Evidence ID/hash and restricted internal storage
