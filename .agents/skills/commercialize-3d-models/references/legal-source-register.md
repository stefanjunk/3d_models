## 1. How to Use This Register

For each relied-on source, record in the project:

- official title and issuing body;
- URL/identifier;
- publication, amendment, effective, and retrieval dates;
- language/version;
- relevant article/section;
- one-sentence release decision supported;
- evidence snapshot/hash;
- reviewer;
- next review trigger.

Do not cite a summary page when the operative legal text or signed contract is available. Use Commission/agency guidance to interpret scope, but distinguish it from binding law.

## 13. Release-Time Update Protocol

Within 30 days before launch, and again on any material delay:

1. Open every source supporting a required decision.
2. Check amendments, corrigenda, effective dates, national transposition, regulator guidance, and court decisions.
3. Re-open exact provider/tool/marketplace/item terms while signed in to the correct business plan.
4. Save/hash new evidence.
5. Redline against the previously approved version.
6. Re-run AI actor/content, product, consumer, export, and license classifications.
7. Obtain renewed approval for a material change.

Also re-check on:

- new market/channel;
- new product category or intended use;
- tool/model/plan/plugin upgrade;
- source/component revision;
- new AI disclosure guidance;
- safety or IP incident;
- contract/insurance renewal;
- any statutory deadline listed above.
