# Safety and physical validation

Mesh validation checks topology, orientation, volume, build-plane position, and file completeness. It does not establish strength or life.

For production use, measure insertion force, breakaway friction, running friction, initial play, retention force, static failure load, and wear after repeated cycles. Repeat after temperature and humidity conditioning. Record printer, nozzle, material lot, drying, slicer version, orientation, line width, flow, cooling, and layer height.

Reject samples with whitening, root cracks, delamination, progressive play, jamming, heat creep, or uncontrolled release. Printed mechanisms are not approved for persons, lifting, vehicle steering/braking, guards, medical use, or other safety functions by inclusion in this library.
