# Select FDM Mechanism

Load `fdm-mechanical-sample-library` and analyze the user's requirement for degrees of freedom, permanent versus reusable connection, motion range, load class, material, printer, hardware allowance, desired play, and assembly direction.

Search the local catalog, compare at least two mechanism families and every relevant tolerance variant, then recommend exact sample IDs and an integration and test workflow.

Do not claim physical strength without a test plan.
