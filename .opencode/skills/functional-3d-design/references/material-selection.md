# FDM Material Selection

## Default Order

1. **PLA:** economical, stiff, dimensionally convenient. Use for static indoor
   parts without elevated temperature, impact, creep, or repeated flexure.
2. **PETG:** primary functional default when more ductility, impact tolerance,
   or occasional flexing is needed. Check creep under sustained load.
3. **ABS/ASA:** require a justified temperature, outdoor, or environmental
   need. Warn about enclosure, warping, fumes, and customer process variation.
4. **TPU:** use only for compliant, damping, grip, tire, or sealing functions.
   Specify hardness and deformation limits.
5. **PA/CF:** use only for justified stiffness, wear, temperature, or structural
   demand. Require drying, abrasive-resistant nozzle, process control, and
   conservative customer instructions.

Do not upgrade a simple static product to PA/CF or ASA merely because it is
functional. Material cost and customer printability are design constraints.
