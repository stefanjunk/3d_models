# Research Notes: German Filament Prices

**Research date:** 2026-08-01

## Comparison Rules
- Prices include German VAT and exclude shipping.
- Normalized price is calculated as item price divided by net filament weight in kilograms.
- Only offers shown as purchasable or in stock at retrieval time qualify for the cheapest-offer field.
- Conditional coupons, subscriptions, business-only net prices, and mandatory bulk purchases are excluded.
- Marketplace listings require enough product and seller information to verify material, weight, and price.
- A product qualifies as high speed only when the manufacturer or listing explicitly markets it as high speed, rapid, or hyper-speed filament.
- TPU must explicitly state Shore 95A hardness.

## Validation Notes

- Prices are point-in-time observations from 2026-08-01 and may change quickly, especially public Amazon sales.
- Amazon pages were set to delivery in Berlin, Germany. Amazon states that marketplace prices for EU delivery are final prices as indicated by the seller; checkout shows the order total including tax.
- Categories are mutually exclusive for this comparison: standard PLA excludes PLA+ and explicit high-speed products; conventional PLA+ excludes explicit high-speed products; standard PETG excludes explicit high-speed products.
- High-speed qualification required explicit listing language such as high speed, rapid, hyper, super speedy, or a directly stated fast-printing claim.
- All winners were checked for a live buy box, stock, product weight, and German delivery. Shipping was excluded throughout.
- Range values describe the directly validated sample, not a guarantee of the entire German market's minimum and maximum.
- Direct retailer pages were preferred. Search snippets, stale price-comparison pages, ambiguous weights, unavailable variants, and blocked pages were not used to establish winners.
- Main exclusions included refills, required multi-roll bundles, quantity-tier prices, Prime-only prices, coupons, subscriptions, business-only net prices, and used products.
- Retrieval issues: one BerryBase URL returned 404 and was replaced by its live canonical product URL; one PETG research run returned no result and was rerun; several search and comparison sites blocked automated retrieval.
