# Concept prompt — Revision 6

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: revision-6 approval sheet for a printable product design
Primary request: Redesign the supplied dark PETG honeycomb hair clip as a noticeably longer and more strongly arched large-hair-volume variant, replacing the old flexure end with a true captured print-in-place rotational hinge.
Input images: Image 1 is the approved revision-4 appearance and honeycomb style reference. Image 2 is the current revision-5 CAD overview and functional layout reference.
Scene/backdrop: dark neutral product-visualization studio backdrop
Subject: an open, metal-free two-member hair clip with an upper armored bow, a lower comb member, opposing blunt comb teeth, a captured rotation hinge at one end, and an integral press-release snap closure at the other
Style/medium: polished dark-anthracite 3D product concept render, matte-to-satin PETG, crisp faceted edges
Composition/framing: one coherent approval sheet without text labels; a large three-quarter overview, a clean side/profile view emphasizing the increased length and arch, a top/armor view, and a magnified hinge inset that clearly shows the captured vertical pivot, knuckles, pin caps and print-in-place separation gap
Materials/textures: smooth printed PETG with a subtle layer-neutral finish; raised honeycomb armor plates separated by deep, clear grooves
Constraints:
- Depict the large preset at approximately 85 mm overall length and 12 mm crown rise; it must look visibly longer and more curved than the references, with more enclosed space for hair.
- The upper armor uses exactly three staggered rows of substantially larger, uniformly oriented hexagons across the clip width.
- Along the clip length, use as many whole cells as fit naturally; preserve the mirrored high-low whole-cell edge contour over both hinge and closure zones.
- Use a coherent real honeycomb lattice with 0.8 mm-looking grooves; no rotated cell family and no rectangular armor.
- Show a real two-part captured print-in-place rotational hinge at the hinge end: vertical axis, interleaved rounded/faceted knuckles, integrated captive pin with top and bottom retention caps, and a visible narrow separation gap between moving members.
- Show the clip in an open pose that makes the rotational relationship understandable; the hinge is not a bendable strap or C-flexure.
- Keep the opposite-end press-release snap closure, lower comb rail, blunt teeth, and skin-safe contact edges visibly unobstructed.
- No standalone decorative end blocks; complete honeycomb cells continue over the structural hinge and latch zones without covering the moving gap or release access.
Avoid: flexure hinge, living hinge, bendable strap, metal pin, exposed loose hardware, screw, spring, rectangular armor, square tiles, more or fewer than three transverse armor rows, rotated hexagon families, cropped cells on the visible outer side, isolated end caps, logos, text, watermark, dimensions, arrows, extra objects

This image communicates appearance, relative scale, and mechanism intent only. Exact dimensions, clearances, motion range, and printability remain controlled and validated by design-spec.yaml and the later production CAD.
