# Business idea and scope

## Two customer problems

Consumer customers want personalized, attractive objects that solve spatial or practical problems without requiring them to understand CAD, slicers or 3D printers. Maker customers want a broader range of configurable, inspectable and self-printable models without the uncertainty and poor versioning of uncontrolled file repositories.

## Two-brand solution

`metriMade` creates **premium personalized products that look intentional and integrate useful function into living and office spaces**. A metriMade product should do at least one of the following:

- fit a specific available width, depth, height, gap, drawer, shelf, device, or object;
- organize a defined set of belongings better than a generic container;
- combine decoration with concealed or integrated storage;
- let the buyer choose meaningful dimensions, labels, names, colors, or modules;
- make an otherwise unusable small space practical without permanent construction.

For `metriMade`, 3D printing is an enabling manufacturing method, not the main customer promise. The customer buys an accessible, aesthetic outcome.

`metriCreate` exposes the broader 3D-product system: technical and experimental products, allowed parameters, revision information, digital-model downloads and optional printed manufacture. Its thematic scope is intentionally open. Examples may include maker tools, mechanisms, drone-related models, original figures, practical systems and utilities. Product legality, third-party rights, safety classification and evidence gates still decide whether any specific item may be sold.

## Initial focus and broader vision

The first `metriMade` category remains intentionally narrow:

- exact-fit drawer organizers;
- shelf-fit bins and modular compact storage;
- personalized desk and entryway organizers;
- decorative storage pieces and labeled holders;
- light-duty, freestanding products that avoid structural, electrical, food-contact, child-safety, or wall-mount claims.

The first products should fit a common 220 × 220 × 250 mm printer as separate parts, use ordinary PLA or PETG, need little or no support, and be verifiable with simple dimensional and use tests. These same releases can appear in `metriCreate`; only the consumer-appropriate subset appears in `metriMade`.

`metriCreate` is not permanently limited to this category. It expands one qualified release at a time into other product areas, including complex hobby and technical products, without making “anything printable” equivalent to “ready for sale.”

## Delivery modes

The long-term offer has three coordinated customer modes:

1. **metriMade As-Is:** the consumer selects a curated approved variant and receives a professionally printed, inspected product.
2. **metriCreate Configure & Order:** the customer changes only validated parameters, freezes a configuration and orders the resulting printed product.
3. **metriCreate Download & Print:** the customer buys a licensed, versioned manufacturing file plus instructions and support terms.

A future configuration flow can collect dimensions and generate a variant, but it is not part of the first transactional MVP. The first releases are fixed, validated sizes or a small set of manually approved variants.

## Boundaries

The first release is not:

- an open marketplace for third-party files;
- an automatically published library of every local model;
- a structural furniture, wall-mount, footwear, toy, food-contact, water-treatment, or electrical-products shop;
- a promise that every parameter combination is safe or printable;
- a global physical-fulfillment operation;
- a multi-format bundle unless the publishing system and release manifest explicitly support every promised file.

Those categories can enter `metriCreate` later after category-specific rights, test, regulatory, insurance and operating evidence exists. “No thematic restriction” does not remove those gates.

## Defensible advantage to build

The advantage is a reusable measured-fit system rather than isolated meshes:

- guided measurement and error prevention;
- parameter rules and printability constraints;
- consistent interfaces, modules, and aesthetic language;
- documented test profiles and revision traceability;
- one controlled catalog with a premium `metriMade` subset and a broader `metriCreate` view;
- one model source that supports both licensed files and controlled production.

The moat grows when each release improves reusable sizing rules, fit coupons, test evidence, and customer measurement data.
