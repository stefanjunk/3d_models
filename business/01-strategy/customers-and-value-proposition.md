# Customers and value proposition

## Two customer groups

### metriMade customer

The metriMade customer lives or works with limited space, values a tidy and attractive result, and prefers guided choices over technical controls. This customer wants a finished `as-is` product and may optionally enter advanced configuration without needing to understand CAD or slicing.

### metriCreate customer

The metriCreate customer is a hobby technologist, maker, tinkerer or curious advanced buyer who wants to inspect options, change meaningful parameters, download a released 3D model for self-printing, or order the configured result. The customer accepts more technical information and a broader, less lifestyle-curated catalog.

The first transactional MVP serves the metriCreate digital-download customer. metriMade `as-is` checkout follows printed-fulfillment qualification.

## Jobs to be done

| Job | Current frustration | Brand outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Use an awkward drawer | generic trays leave gaps or do not hold the actual tools | modular layout fitted to stated dimensions and use |
| Use a narrow shelf | standard bins waste depth or block access | bins sized to the available envelope |
| Keep a small desk calm | separate trays and cables create clutter | one compact, intentional organizer system |
| Give a useful personal object | personalized decor often has no function | name/form personalization with storage or organization value |
| Buy without technical decisions | 3D-print stores expose materials, slicer jargon and too many weak choices | `metriMade`: guided premium selection and finished product |
| Adapt an appealing product | mass-market variants do not fit, but CAD is inaccessible | `metriMade` handoff to the exact `metriCreate` configuration |
| Print at home confidently | marketplace files have uncertain orientation and fit | `metriCreate`: versioned file, tested profile, dimensions, instructions and support boundaries |
| Explore technical or playful products | lifestyle shops are narrow and file repositories are uncontrolled | `metriCreate`: broad but release-gated catalog with parameter and revision transparency |

## Value proposition

**metriMade:** For people who want personalized products that make homes and offices more beautiful and useful, metriMade offers a curated, guided selection of premium smart decor and space-saving objects, available as finished products with optional advanced customization.

**metriCreate:** For makers and curious advanced customers, metriCreate offers a broad catalog of configurable, versioned 3D products that can be ordered as printed objects or downloaded for self-printing, subject to product-specific release gates.

## Purchase confidence requirements

Each product page must answer:

- What exact problem and space does this revision fit?
- What must the customer measure, and with what allowed error?
- What printer volume, material, nozzle, and skill level are required?
- Which files are actually included?
- What was physically tested and what was not?
- Which uses are excluded?
- How does a customer obtain help, a correction, a refund, or withdrawal support?

`metriMade` answers these in consumer language and hides irrelevant manufacturing detail behind optional disclosures. `metriCreate` adds parameter constraints, revision history, printer/process envelope, file contents and technical exclusions.

## Initial positioning hypotheses

The research suggests exact-fit organizers and personalized bookends are promising, but it does not prove demand, conversion, or price. Treat the reported price ranges as hypotheses. Validate message, measurement effort, format preference, and willingness to pay before building a broad catalog.
