# Initial portfolio decision

## Target launch catalog

The minimum credible catalog is three related products, not five unrelated prototypes. None is currently ready for sale.

| SKU | Product | Why it belongs | Current state | Required path to initial portfolio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MM-ORG-001` | DrawerFit Modular | strongest proof of exact-fit small-space value; a digitally validated local draft exists | `P2 Digital candidate` | freeze one simple fixed-size revision; remove unresolved connector issue; slice; print coupons; complete three final prints and drawer-fit/use tests; clear rights/safety; create package/media/economics; stage |
| `MM-PER-001` | NameForm Bookends | top-ranked research idea; simple personalization; decorative and functional | `P0 Idea` | define size/weight range and anti-tip boundary; build parametric CAD and readable-name rules; test pair stability with specified books; make three final sets; clear names/fonts; package and stage |
| `MM-ORG-002` | ShelfFit Mini Bins | directly serves narrow shelves and wasted depth; supports repeat purchase and later systems | `P0 Idea` | define non-stacking light-duty scope and standard envelope; build modular CAD for common 220 mm bed; test fit, deflection, grip, and cleaning; make three final sets; package and stage |

## Launch constraints

- Digital file only, Germany only, until the legal, tax, payment, and digital-delivery gates are signed.
- Exactly one safe-core 3MF per product revision because that is what the current operator supports.
- No STL, PDF, slicer-profile, ZIP, or multi-file promise on the product page until the release manifest and publisher support them.
- Fixed released sizes first. No unrestricted self-service dimensions.
- PLA/PETG only if the exact released profile and claims support them.
- No load-bearing, wall-mounted, body-contact, child-directed, food-contact, water-treatment, or electrical claims.

## Pipeline pilot, not launch hero

`MM-MKR-001` CyberVault Nozzle Case is the closest local model to a technical release: the R4 files are digitally approved and inherited basic fit/hinge/latch evidence is documented. R4-specific relief, loaded inversion, cycle, rights, safety, commercial-package, and shop evidence remain open. Use it to exercise the release pipeline if useful, but do not let it displace the three-product small-space validation.

## Existing-model reserve

The following local work is valuable but belongs after the launch catalog:

- Modern Carbon Desk Organizer: on-strategy, but 320 mm wide and still experimental; create a common-printer mini derivative or offer it only after printed fulfillment is ready.
- Parametric Labyrinth Gift Box: digitally strong, but needs physical fit testing and sits outside the first storage narrative.
- Dice Tower: digitally final, physical fall tests open; hobby category later.
- System-furniture drawer concepts: promising, but all are provisional and need real furniture revision measurements and physical fit proof.

## Explicit non-selection

Over-toilet storage, wall shelves, camera arms, printer enclosures, shoes, hair clips, toys/projectile devices, boats, water filters, cups/measuring spoons, branded vehicle shapes, raw generated figures, and structural/system accessories are excluded from the initial launch. They require category-specific risk, durability, rights, or fulfillment work disproportionate to the MVP.
