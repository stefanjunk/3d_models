# Market validation plan

## Goal

Validate demand and workflow before building a broad catalog or self-service configurator.

## Four-week evidence plan

| Test | Method | Minimum useful evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem/message | 10–15 interviews with people in small homes/workshops | repeated examples of poorly served spaces and current workaround | keep or revise primary problem |
| Measurement workflow | ask 8 people to measure one drawer and one shelf from a draft guide | completion rate, time, ambiguity, measurement variance | design guide and tolerances |
| Offer preference | clickable prototype with file vs printed choice | stated preference plus email/waitlist action | choose first fulfillment order |
| Price | present 3 realistic price points per launch SKU | purchase-intent distribution; no leading “would you buy?” only | set test price band |
| Design direction | compare 2–3 coherent render styles | ranked preference and reason | freeze launch visual language |
| Digital usability | supervised file handoff to 5 printer owners | successful slice without help, warnings, questions | revise package/support |

## Landing-page experiment

Show only the three target products. Each card should state whether it is “in development,” “digital file planned,” or “printed pilot planned.” Collect:

- product of interest;
- file or printed preference;
- printer build volume if file;
- approximate dimensions/use case;
- country;
- consented email.

Do not accept money, promise a delivery date, or label a render as a photograph until a product release exists.

## Success criteria before catalog expansion

- at least 20 qualified expressions of interest across the three products;
- at least 5 users complete the measurement workflow without assisted correction;
- at least 5 target-printer users successfully slice the release candidate;
- one product reaches `P5 Commercial release` with a viable cost model;
- support questions can be answered from the package documentation;
- no unresolved safety, rights, or legal blockers for the intended country and delivery mode.

If the criteria fail, improve the problem, measurement flow, or product—not the catalog size.
