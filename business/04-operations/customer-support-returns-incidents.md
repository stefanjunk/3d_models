# Customer support, returns and incidents

## Minimum service desk

Before launch, assign an owner, monitored email, response target and case log for:

- pre-purchase measurement and printer compatibility;
- payment, invoice, account and download problems;
- slicing/printing questions within the promised support scope;
- defect, incorrect fit and misleading-description reports;
- withdrawal/cancellation and refunds;
- printed-product damage/return when enabled;
- privacy access/deletion requests;
- rights complaints and takedowns;
- product-safety incidents and recall communication.

## Case data

Record case ID, customer/order, SKU/revision/hash, delivery mode, printer/material/profile if relevant, issue category, photos/measurements, severity, response, refund/replacement, root cause, corrective action and closure. Minimize personal data and apply the retention policy.

## Triage

| Severity | Example | Immediate action |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | injury, fire, serious hazard, systemic unsafe claim | stop affected sale/download/production; preserve evidence; notify safety/legal owner |
| High | repeated breakage, fit failure across revisions, rights complaint | pause affected SKU; investigate batch/revision; communicate with affected buyers |
| Normal | one print issue, measurement error, delivery or account issue | resolve under support/refund policy; feed data to product owner |

## Incident capability

- Kill switch by SKU and revision.
- Trace affected orders/entitlements and printed batches.
- Preserve released files, notices and investigation evidence.
- Prepare customer communication and competent-authority route where required.
- Record corrective action: documentation, customer support, replacement revision, refund, withdrawal, recall or permanent retirement.
- Run one tabletop exercise before launch and after material architecture changes.

## Policy decisions still required

- support languages and hours;
- digital troubleshooting boundary;
- remedies for customer measurement error versus a defective configuration workflow;
- digital update entitlement;
- printed custom-goods return and withdrawal treatment after legal review;
- deletion/retention interaction with tax, safety and contract records.
