# 90-day execution scenario

This is a capacity-aware scenario for a small team, not a promise. Adviser response, print duration and iteration failures can extend it. If one person is responsible for CAD, testing, engineering, legal coordination and operations, launch only the first completed SKU and keep the other two staged until their evidence is complete.

## Days 1–15: scope and foundations

- Name owners; finalize operator/domain/brand-clearance workstreams and retain the selected metriMade `MM-BRAND-001-R1` source/hash record.
- Freeze the three SKU requirements and product test matrices.
- Select DrawerFit revision and start connector correction/coupons.
- Start simple NameForm and ShelfFit CAD.
- Conduct interviews and measurement tests.
- Reconcile website branch, add CI, establish Firebase staging and define one shared product schema with explicit `metriMade` eligibility/content.
- Begin legal/tax/provider data map and support/incident runbooks.

Exit: no unresolved product-scope ambiguity; CI/staging plan works; first coupons ready.

## Days 16–45: candidates and real prints

- Complete digital candidates for all three products.
- Slice on intended profiles and print coupons/prototypes.
- Revise failed designs and freeze candidate revisions.
- Begin three-print reproducibility runs one SKU at a time.
- Complete source/component registers, cost models, instructions and draft catalog content.
- Generate, integrate and coupon-test the exact `metriMade.com` + product-ID/version mark for every physical metriMade candidate.
- Configure Stripe test flow, Firebase permissions, backups and alerts.

Exit: at least one exact revision has complete physical evidence; the other two have honest blockers.

## Days 46–70: commercial package and staging

- Finish all achievable `P5` release packages; do not carry failed products into the shop.
- Produce real photos and compare them with renders.
- Approve legal/profile/privacy/terms/license/withdrawal content.
- Publish the first product to `metriCreate` staging and test catalog consistency; verify that any `metriMade` preview maps to the same ID/revision.
- Complete webhook, reconciliation, entitlement, refund and takedown paths.

Exit: at least one P5 product completes staging E2E; no placeholder claim is transactional.

## Days 71–90: launch decision

- Publish remaining qualified launch releases to staging.
- Finish accessibility/browser/content checks and security/restore drills.
- Run production-like purchase, download, expiry, unauthorized, refund, withdrawal and kill-switch scenarios.
- Record and sign the launch configuration.
- If all P0 gates pass, launch `metriCreate` Germany digital-only and monitor first orders. Keep `metriMade` printed checkout off until its fulfillment gate passes.

If fewer than three products qualify, choose between a one-product controlled beta and delaying the public shop. Do not label incomplete products as inventory to meet a date.
