# Metrics and review cadence

## Pre-launch weekly dashboard

- launch SKUs by lifecycle stage and blocker;
- open P0 tasks and ownerless tasks;
- coupon/prototype/final-print pass/fail and reprint hours;
- product rights/safety/commercial evidence completeness;
- staging CI/E2E pass rate and unresolved security/legal blockers;
- validated interview/measurement tests completed.

## First 10 orders

- checkout success/failure and duplicate/reconciliation errors;
- entitlement/download success and support contact rate;
- refund/withdrawal/fraud rate and reason;
- slicer/import warnings by printer/profile;
- reported fit/measurement errors by SKU/revision;
- support minutes per order;
- net contribution per order using actual fees/support.

## After stable operation

- qualified traffic to product page, add-to-cart and purchase conversion;
- digital versus printed interest;
- contribution margin and release-investment payback by SKU;
- return/refund/defect and physical failure rate;
- repeat purchase and cross-family adoption;
- support and incident trends by exact revision;
- delivery time, print yield and reprint rate when physical is enabled.

## Cadence

- Weekly: product gates, P0 tasks, risks and customer evidence.
- Per release: signed evidence and shop consistency review.
- First two launch weeks: daily payment/download/support reconciliation.
- Monthly for first quarter: economics, refunds, support and roadmap.
- Quarterly after stability: portfolio keep/improve/retire decisions and country/delivery expansion.

Avoid vanity totals such as raw model count, downloads without payment context, social followers, or the number of demo catalog cards. They do not demonstrate a working product business.
