# Unit economics and pricing

No current research price is approved. Build one signed model per SKU and delivery mode.

## Digital contribution model

`net revenue after VAT`  
`− payment fee`  
`− platform/storage/download variable cost`  
`− expected refund/fraud cost`  
`− expected support time × loaded hourly cost`  
`= contribution before design/release recovery`

Track design, testing, media, legal/commercial review and release-maintenance effort as product investment. Decide the number of sales over which each product should recover that investment; do not hide it because file replication is cheap.

## Printed contribution model

Add:

- filament and purchased components at actual yield;
- machine time at an approved hourly rate including depreciation/maintenance/energy;
- setup, removal, post-processing, assembly and QC labor;
- support removal/waste and failed-print allowance based on measured yield;
- packaging, labeling and outbound handling;
- carrier cost or customer shipping charge difference;
- return/warranty/reprint reserve;
- marketplace/payment/accounting fees and taxes.

## Required price sheet fields

- SKU/revision/variant and delivery country;
- gross customer price, VAT rate/treatment and net revenue;
- format or printed material/color/size;
- slicer-derived grams and machine time from the approved profile;
- labor minutes by task;
- failure-rate assumption and evidence period;
- packaging/carrier assumptions;
- fee, refund, support and warranty assumptions;
- contribution euros and percent;
- design/release investment and break-even units;
- owner, approval date and review trigger.

## Pricing experiments

The local research suggests ranges such as roughly €25–45 per personalized bookend pair and higher values for fitted sets, but these are hypotheses. Test at least three realistic price points alongside a concrete offer and ask for a behavioral commitment such as waitlist/signup—not only stated willingness.

## Review triggers

Recalculate when geometry, material, profile, package, country, carrier, Stripe/tax configuration, refund/support rate or failure rate changes. Review monthly during the first three months and quarterly after stable volume.
