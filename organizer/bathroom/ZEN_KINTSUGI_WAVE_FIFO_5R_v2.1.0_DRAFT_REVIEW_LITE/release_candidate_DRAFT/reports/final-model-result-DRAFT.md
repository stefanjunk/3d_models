# DRAFT model result – ZEN KINTSUGI WAVE v2.1

## Outcome

The approved concept has been rebuilt as a modular five-roll FIFO tower with substantially more design character than v2.0: five freeform oval front ribbons, closed thin textured side shells, conformal kintsugi and seigaiha colour solids, open crown loops, slender bronze spines, a removable fragrance tray and sparse non-crossing rear wave struts.

## Quantitative result

- Product body: 141.6 × 120.9 × at most 666 mm; removable tray extends the right side to X=106 mm.
- Module height: 124 mm; crown body height: 46 mm design envelope.
- FIFO proxy: Ø122 × 107 mm, 121 positions, zero collisions.
- Side wall: 1.80 mm nominal, 1.42 mm calculated minimum after the deepest pocket.
- CAD solid volume: 612,553 mm³, 31.20 % below the v2.0 reference.
- All 22 STL round trips and all five 3MF packages pass digital topology/structure validation.
- Watermark: JSI-WM-001-R1 compact, scale 1.0, 0°, 11.423 × 10.000 mm, normally readable and recessed 0.40 mm into a 5.20 mm bronze spine.
