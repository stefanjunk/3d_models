# DRAFT assembly guide

Rows are numbered front-to-back and columns left-to-right. Assemble each row `c1 → c2 → c3`, then join `r1 → r2 → r3`. Right and back edges carry male tabs; corresponding left and front edges carry female sockets.

Keep every floor on a flat surface and slide the matching edges together by hand. Do not bend modules vertically, hammer, clamp, heat, sand or force a connector. Stop if the seam becomes proud, the floor rocks, stress-whitens, cracks or requires more than comfortable hand force. A failed fit means the configured clearance must be revised and all affected artifacts rebuilt.

The comb rests across the tool lane with `0.5 mm` nominal side clearance per end. Its assembly preview datum is `70 mm` behind the front edge, but it remains removable and can be repositioned after the physical lane-width test.
