## Binding geometry beside the image

- Packed nominal envelope: `512 × 491 × 50 mm`.
- Nominal four-tray grid: `2 × 2`; each tray approximately `256 × 245.5 × 50 mm` before detailed wall and clearance resolution.
- Kobra 3 Max usable-footprint gate per tray: `≤416 × 416 mm`.
- Floor, wall and divider values remain those in `design-spec.yaml`; the image is not a dimensional drawing.

## Deliberate simplifications and visual ambiguity

- The sheet does not prove exact fit, wall thickness, corner radius, bed fit, strength, slicer behavior or flatness.
- Gaps and wall proportions are enlarged slightly for readability. The CAD set will use the binding packed envelope and deterministic dimensions.
- The loose shim strips indicate the fit-adjustment principle only. Their exact length, placement and 0.5/1.0/2.0 mm thickness variants will be resolved after the real-drawer measurement and coupon test.
- The inset shows the required flat underside intent, not final watermark placement. The `MM-WM-001-R1` underside mark remains blocked until final geometry Gate 0C.
- Color blocking is visual communication; the first physical prototype remains plain-surface PETG and may be single color.
- No tool lane, comb, product-specific holder, texture or inter-tray connector is part of this revision.
