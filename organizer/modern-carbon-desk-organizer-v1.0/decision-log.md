# Decision log

- **320 × 230 mm footprint:** large enough to exploit the Kobra 3 Max without making the organizer dominate the full 420 mm bed.
- **One-piece two-bay housing:** fewer joints and a cleaner exterior. It prints on its back, so the large drawer openings do not create unsupported roofs.
- **Separate top sorter:** permits a normal bottom-down print with clean compartment walls and makes the design easier to revise.
- **Two identical drawers:** reduces build complexity and makes either drawer replaceable from one STL.
- **Procedural carbon twill:** shallow two-level diagonal strands are printable and repeat seamlessly. The structure is cosmetic, not structural carbon fiber.
- **Emboss rather than engraving:** preserves the 3.2 mm housing wall and catches grazing light on matte black filament.
- **0.45 mm clearance per drawer side:** a reasonable starting value for a calibrated 0.4 mm FDM process; the supplied coupon decides whether to use the default or regenerate.
- **Mesh-first Manifold route:** the functional form is parametric CSG and the repeated texture remains stable as guaranteed-manifold mesh geometry. STL is the manufacturing deliverable; STEP is not claimed for this revision.
