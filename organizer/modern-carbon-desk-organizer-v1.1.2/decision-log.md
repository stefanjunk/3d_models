# Decision log

- **320 × 230 mm footprint:** large enough to exploit the Kobra 3 Max without making the organizer dominate the full 420 mm bed.
- **One-piece two-bay housing:** fewer joints and a cleaner exterior. It prints on its back, so the large drawer openings do not create unsupported roofs.
- **Separate top sorter:** permits a normal bottom-down print with clean compartment walls and makes the design easier to revise.
- **Two identical drawers:** reduces build complexity and makes either drawer replaceable from one STL.
- **Image-derived carbon twill:** a seamless 16-bit grayscale 2×2-twill height map drives the shallow geometric texture. The structure is cosmetic, not structural carbon fiber.
- **Recessed engraving on housing, sorter, and drawer fronts:** keeps the texture flush with the overall envelope. Handles, edge radii, and all sliding or mating interfaces remain untextured.
- **0.45 mm clearance per drawer side:** a reasonable starting value for a calibrated 0.4 mm FDM process; the supplied coupon decides whether to use the default or regenerate.
- **Mesh-first Manifold route:** the functional form is parametric CSG and the repeated texture remains stable as guaranteed-manifold mesh geometry. STL is the manufacturing deliverable; STEP is not claimed for this revision.
- **Requirements gate approved for revision 1.1.0 (2026-08-10):** the user approved the shared vertical-axis rounding, housing-following drawer-front curvature, and image-derived carbon engraving on side panels and both drawer fronts. Production geometry remains blocked pending concept approval.
- **Concept gate opened for revision 1.1.0 (2026-08-10):** corrected concept sheet `assets/concept/desk_organizer_concept_r1.1.0_v2.png` and the seamless 1024²/16-bit, 36 mm carbon-twill height map were created. Approval is pending; no production CAD or STL was regenerated.
- **Revision 1.1.1 requested (2026-08-10):** extend the same image-derived carbon engraving to the outer front and rear vertical walls of the top sorter. Its compartment interiors, top rim, bottom alignment interfaces, and small edge radii stay smooth. This changes an approved appearance requirement, so the requirements gate is reopened and the prior concept is invalidated.
- **Requirements gate approved for revision 1.1.1 (2026-08-10):** the user approved carbon engraving on all four exterior vertical walls of the top sorter. A revised concept sheet must visibly show both the front and rear sorter walls before production CAD resumes.
- **Revision 1.1.2 requested (2026-08-10):** extend the same image-derived carbon engraving to the outer rear vertical wall of the lower drawer housing. Drawer-bay interiors, sliding interfaces, small edge radii, and mating surfaces stay smooth. This appearance change invalidates the revision 1.1.1 concept and reopens requirements approval.
- **Requirements gate approved for revision 1.1.2 (2026-08-10):** the user approved the lower housing rear-wall engraving. The replacement concept sheet must show a front overview and an actual rear three-quarter view with the rear walls of both the sorter and lower housing visibly textured; production CAD remains blocked pending concept approval.
- **Concept gate opened for revision 1.1.2 (2026-08-10):** concept sheet `assets/concept/desk_organizer_concept_r1.1.2.png` shows the front surface assignment plus a true rear-right view with engraved sorter and lower-housing rear walls. Exact depth remains governed by the specification; production CAD is blocked pending user approval.
- **Concept gate approved for revision 1.1.2 (2026-08-10):** the user explicitly approved the concept. Production CAD and exports may proceed against revision 1.1.2.
- **Drawer-front width reconciliation:** the approved 2.35 mm offset on each side of a 320 mm housing mathematically yields 315.30 mm. This supersedes the obsolete 316 mm value while preserving the explicitly approved 9.65 mm derived radius and continuous housing-following curve.
- **Verified assembly height:** the sorter sockets overlap the housing pegs, so the closed height is 213.60 mm rather than the obsolete additive 216.80 mm estimate.
- **Dense image-relief architecture:** the approved 1024²/16-bit master is retained, while a periodic 120² uint16 tile drives 0.30 mm geometry sampling at a physical 36 mm repeat. The four cutter STLs remain separate reproducibility evidence.
- **Drawer Boolean cleanup:** extending the front cutter 0.10 mm beyond the top and bottom removed two internal non-manifold termination edges. The Boolean then produced three closed chips totaling 0.10394 mm³ at a pre-existing body/front overlap; a guarded largest-body rule removes them only below 0.20 mm³. The final drawer was independently revalidated as one watertight body.
- **Revision 1.1.2 final export:** all five manufacturing STLs, four cutters, and three untextured bases passed independent binary-STL topology, winding, body-count, positive-volume, and relevant build-envelope checks. Slicer and physical tests remain pending, so status stays `experimental`.
