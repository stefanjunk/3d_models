## Visible requirement mapping

- The books sit between two distinct ends: true left/right pair requirement.
- Both thin feet point inward under the books: under-book stabilization
  requirement.
- Tall perpendicular blades remain beside the outermost books: fixed functional
  stop-height requirement.
- `STE` and `FAN` face the viewer without mirroring: split-name reading-order
  requirement.
- Decorative text wings remain outside the book row: text is decoupled from the
  book-contact interface.
- Open rear ribs and localized triangular gussets communicate the lightweight
  shell/rib strategy.
- Rounded edges communicate the handling and book-protection requirement.

## Deliberate simplifications and ambiguities

- The inset rib pattern is illustrative; production rib count, pitch, and root
  radii will be calculated and sliced before selection.
- The visible foot and wing thicknesses are not dimensionally authoritative.
- Books obscure part of each inward foot; the lower-left inset exists to show
  the intended under-book direction.
- Color and finish are illustrative and remain nonblocking product options.
