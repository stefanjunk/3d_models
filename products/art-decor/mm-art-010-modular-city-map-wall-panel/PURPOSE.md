# Purpose — Permanent One Off City Map Wall Relief

A unique permanent Berlin street-map wall artwork offered in two controlled display modes: an irregular Berlin-boundary silhouette inside a maximum 600 x 400 mm envelope, or a 600 x 400 mm rectangular Umland field with the Berlin boundary marked. Each is printed as two four-color halves on the Anycubic Kobra 3 Max Combo. The halves are permanently registered by concealed one-way printed connectors into one non-serviceable picture. Local connector springs and isolated snap-in hanger/standoff parts provide assembly and an 18 mm halo cavity without a rear grid. Selected abstract paths pass optional customer-added light through the visible face.
