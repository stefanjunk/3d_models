# Purpose — Permanent One Off City Map Wall Relief

A unique permanent 600 x 400 mm Berlin street-map wall artwork printed as two 300 x 400 mm four-color halves on the Anycubic Kobra 3 Max Combo. The halves are permanently registered and bonded into one non-serviceable picture. Local rear seam keys and isolated hanger/standoff blocks provide assembly and an 18 mm halo cavity without a rear grid. Selected abstract paths pass optional customer-added light through the visible face.
