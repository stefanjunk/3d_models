# MM-ART-010 — Permanent One-off City Map Wall Relief

Status: **revision 0.4.2 records Oak/Mint Green/Midnight/Sky Blue as a non-geometric product colorway. The revision 0.4.0 digital-candidate-r9 bodies remain unchanged and are assigned to the four physical filaments in Anycubic Slicer Next or by ACE loading. The product-variant contract also reserves independent size and map-extent axes; only 600 × 400 mm and the frozen Berlin extent are currently production-supported. No human print candidate, physical qualification, watermark or release approval exists**.

The planned pilot is a unique Berlin street-map wall relief with two parameterized appearance modes. `boundary_crop` removes every printed body outside the Berlin administrative boundary and produces an irregular silhouette inside a maximum 600 × 400 mm envelope. `context_outline` keeps the 600 × 400 mm rectangle, maps surrounding context and marks Berlin with a Sky Blue boundary relief. Both use Oak as the base, Mint Green as the middle relief, Midnight for streets and Sky Blue for boundary/accents. They retain two permanent main prints, glue-free concealed connectors, isolated rear standoffs, an 18 mm halo-light cavity and protected light-through openings without a rear grid. Lighting and electrical hardware remain customer add-ons and are not part of the product.

The authoritative requirements are in `design-spec.yaml`; revision 0.4.2 normalization is in `requirements-review-0.4.2.md`, and the machine-readable axes and configured examples are in `product-variants.json`. The approved mode-aware decomposition remains `decomposition-review-0.4.0.md` and `plan/hybrid-design-plan-v0.4.0.json`.
