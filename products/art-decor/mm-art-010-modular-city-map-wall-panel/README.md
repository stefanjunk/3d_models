# MM-ART-010 — Modular City Map Wall Panel

Status: **requirements 0.2.0 approved; concept approval pending; no CAD or print candidate exists**.

The first planned pilot is a 600 × 400 mm Berlin street-map wall picture assembled from six 200 mm FDM modules. The product family uses broad abstract color bodies instead of pseudo-realistic four-color mapping. The serviceable segmented rear grid reserves a ventilated halo-light envelope, cable exits and protected through-openings for customer-supplied lighting; no lighting or electrical hardware is part of the product.

The authoritative requirements are in `design-spec.yaml`. Production geometry is blocked until concept approval.

Concept for review: `concepts/modular-relief-collection-concept-v01.png`; controlled review notes: `concept-review.md`. The image is a visual direction only; the dimensions and interfaces in `design-spec.yaml` remain authoritative.
