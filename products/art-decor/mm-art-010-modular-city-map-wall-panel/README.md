# MM-ART-010 — Permanent One-off City Map Wall Relief

Status: **revision 0.4.1 requirements fix the physical SUNLU palette to Oak, Mint Green, Midnight and Sky Blue. Recolored concept v04 awaits human approval before production solids and Anycubic project 3MFs are remapped. Revision 0.4.0 digital candidate r9 remains the validated geometry baseline. No human print candidate, physical qualification, watermark or release approval exists**.

The planned pilot is a unique Berlin street-map wall relief with two parameterized appearance modes. `boundary_crop` removes every printed body outside the Berlin administrative boundary and produces an irregular silhouette inside a maximum 600 × 400 mm envelope. `context_outline` keeps the 600 × 400 mm rectangle, maps surrounding context and marks Berlin with a Sky Blue boundary relief. Both use Oak as the base, Mint Green as the middle relief, Midnight for streets and Sky Blue for boundary/accents. They retain two permanent main prints, glue-free concealed connectors, isolated rear standoffs, an 18 mm halo-light cavity and protected light-through openings without a rear grid. Lighting and electrical hardware remain customer add-ons and are not part of the product.

The authoritative requirements are in `design-spec.yaml`; revision 0.4.1 normalization is in `requirements-review-0.4.1.md`, palette concept parameters are in `source/v0.4.1/berlin/display-mode-parameters.json`, and the pending visual gate is `concept-review-0.4.1.md` with `concepts/berlin-display-modes-concept-v04.png`. The approved mode-aware decomposition remains `decomposition-review-0.4.0.md` and `plan/hybrid-design-plan-v0.4.0.json` because the palette revision does not alter body topology or interfaces. The revision 0.4.0 DRAFT geometry baseline is summarized in `validation/v0.4.0/berlin/digital-candidate-r9/digital-candidate-summary.md`. Physical fit, final ACE slot/purge preview, print-candidate, watermark and release gates remain open.
