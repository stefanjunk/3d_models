# MM-ART-010 — Permanent One-off City Map Wall Relief

Status: **requirements, concept v02 and decomposition 0.3.0 approved; parametric source and coupon generation in progress; no print candidate exists**.

The planned pilot is a unique 600 × 400 mm Berlin street-map wall relief. Revision 0.3.0 uses two 300 × 400 mm four-color main prints, permanently joined without glue by three concealed one-way connector springs. Isolated snap-in hanger/standoff parts prepare an 18 mm halo-light cavity and protected light-through openings without a rear grid. Lighting and electrical hardware remain customer add-ons and are not part of the product.

The authoritative approved requirements are in `design-spec.yaml`; the concise approval record is `requirements-review-0.3.0.md`. The approved visual review is `concept-review-0.3.0.md` with `concepts/permanent-relief-collection-concept-v02.png`. The approved assembly review is `decomposition-review-0.3.0.md` backed by `plan/hybrid-design-plan-v0.3.0.json`. Production geometry and coupons are now authorized; physical fit, print-candidate and release gates remain open.

Historical concept: `concepts/modular-relief-collection-concept-v01.png`; controlled notes: `concept-review.md`. It documents the rejected six-tile rear-grid direction and is not current design authority.

Historical decomposition: `decomposition-review.md` and `plan/hybrid-design-plan.json`. Both are superseded by revision 0.3.0 and cannot authorize CAD.
