# MM-ART-010 — Permanent One-off City Map Wall Relief

Status: **revision 0.5.2 requirements are approved and concept v07 awaits explicit human approval. Revision 0.5.1 `digital-candidate-r4` is rejected for production because it omitted water-area multipolygons such as Tegeler See and assigned Sky Blue to motorway accents. No current 0.5.2 CAD, mesh or 3MF exists. Only after concept and revised decomposition approval may corrected manufacturing artifacts be generated. No human print candidate, physical qualification, watermark or commercial release approval exists**.

The planned pilot is a unique Berlin street-map wall relief with two parameterized appearance modes. `boundary_crop` removes every printed body outside the Berlin administrative boundary and produces an irregular silhouette inside a maximum 600 × 400 mm envelope. `context_outline` keeps the 600 × 400 mm rectangle, maps surrounding context and marks Berlin with a Sky Blue boundary relief. Both use Oak as the base, Mint Green as the middle relief, Midnight for the street network including motorway/trunk, and Sky Blue for S-Bahn/U-Bahn plus the context boundary and the metriMade site marker. Every retained mapped water area and river/canal/stream becomes through-part negative geometry for optional backlighting. They retain two permanent main prints, glue-free concealed connectors, isolated rear standoffs and an 18 mm halo-light cavity without a rear grid. Lighting and electrical hardware remain customer add-ons and are not part of the product.

The authoritative requirements are in `design-spec.yaml`; parameter axes and configured examples are in `product-variants.json`. The structural revision 0.4.0 decomposition remains historical input but must be updated for the new water/transit layer owners before production regeneration.
