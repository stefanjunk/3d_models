# Concept review — MM-ART-010 Berlin

> **Superseded 2026-08-30:** This revision 0.2.0 concept shows replaceable tiles and a rear grid rejected by the user. It is retained only as design history and cannot authorize current work. Revision 0.3.0 requirements approval and a replacement concept are required.

Review artifact: `concepts/modular-relief-collection-concept-v01.png`

The image is a non-authoritative product visualization. The Berlin pilot will use an exact frozen OpenStreetMap-derived vector snapshot only after concept approval; the roads shown in the concept image are deliberately schematic.

## Visual direction proposed for approval

- Abstract architectural map with broad regions, crisp relief/inlay boundaries and no realistic or photographic color claim.
- Selected palette `urban_signal`: Anycubic PLA Matte Bone White for the ground, Nardo Grey for urban fields/minor routes, Black for primary routes and Orange for restrained accent districts/title elements.
- A few simplified route or void paths become real light-through openings. They form a separate graphic layer and are not a geographic legend.
- Six individually printable and replaceable front tiles align on one segmented rear mounting grid. The product remains complete and legible without lighting.
- Customer-added lighting can create both a wall halo and controlled lines of light on the front; LEDs, controller, power supply, wiring and diffuser are not part of the product.

## Controlled dimensions and provisional tolerances

- Assembled artwork: 600 × 400 mm landscape.
- Grid: 3 columns × 2 rows; module pitch 200 × 200 mm; each printable part must fit a 220 × 220 mm build area.
- Berlin art-tile envelope: maximum 200 × 200 × 5 mm; 3 mm backer; nominal map relief/inlay step 0.6 mm.
- Internal visible seam target: 0.25 mm, selected later with a 0.15 / 0.25 / 0.35 / 0.45 mm physical coupon.
- Outer frame target: 8 mm.
- Wall standoff / halo cavity: 18 mm nominal.
- Optional LED envelope: 12 × 4 mm keep-out for nominal 8 or 10 mm strips; cable channel at least 6 × 4 mm; exits left, right and bottom.
- Light openings: at least 2.0 mm wide, at least 5.0 mm solid ligament between openings, no more than 12% open area per tile.
- Protected solid zones: 8 mm from tile seams and 12 mm from datums or retaining features; optional rear diffuser land 6 mm wide.

Approval of this concept authorizes detailed decomposition and production-CAD work, but not physical printing, electrical qualification or final commercial release.
