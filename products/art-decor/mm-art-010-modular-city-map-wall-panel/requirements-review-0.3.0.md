# Requirements review 0.3.0 — MM-ART-010 Berlin

Status: **approved by Stefan on 2026-08-31; replacement concept awaiting approval; CAD blocked**.

## User-stated

- One unique 600 × 400 mm Berlin wall relief, made from multiple 3D prints.
- No rear grid and no replaceable sections.
- Four-color printing on the Anycubic Kobra 3 Max Combo.
- Abstract rather than pseudo-realistic color treatment.
- Optional customer-added rear halo lighting plus deliberate light-through openings; electrical parts are excluded.
- Glue-free printed plug connectors for the permanent assembly.

## Inferred

- The separate prints become one permanently assembled, non-serviceable artwork.
- The artwork must remain visually complete, mechanically mountable and ventilated without lighting installed.
- Small local connectors and isolated wall spacers are permissible only as permanent assembly/mounting parts, not as a raster or exchange interface.

## Recommended for approval

- Split once vertically into two 300 × 400 mm main prints. Both fit the verified 420 × 420 mm bed and create only one visible internal seam.
- Permanently register the halves with discrete locating lands and three concealed loose one-way spring/tenon connectors. Select the pocket clearance and spring geometry by a process-matched coupon; use no structural adhesive.
- Create the 18 mm halo gap with isolated snap-in upper hanger/standoff and lower standoff parts; do not add a rear frame, grid, magnets or removable retainers.
- Reserve 12 × 4 mm open LED-strip envelopes for nominal 8/10 mm strips, 6 × 4 mm cable paths, left/right/bottom exits and protected ≥2 mm face openings. Lighting remains an unsupplied add-on.
- Retain the approved `urban_signal` palette: Bone White, Nardo Grey, Black and Orange as broad semantic solids without dithering.

## Approved consequential decision

Stefan approved the two-half permanent assembly with three concealed one-way connector springs and isolated snap-in hanger/standoff parts. The finished artwork is intentionally not serviceable; destructive removal is acceptable. Connector fit, flatness, crack/whitening, pull/step retention and the mounted proof load require coupons/tests.

Approval of this requirements revision authorizes a new visual concept. Production CAD and coupons begin only after concept approval; printing, electrical work, wall installation and commercial release remain separately gated.
