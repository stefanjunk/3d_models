# Berlin 0.4.0 build-iteration summary

The accepted digital draft is `digital-candidate-r9`. Iterations r1–r8 were fail-closed development candidates, not print files. They progressively exposed touching-shell composites, floating backer islands around closed light apertures, one raster seam-column ambiguity, STL serialization degeneracy, and Boolean seam artifacts.

The accepted construction adds 2.0 mm material bridges only where a light-through waterway would isolate printed material, retains one connected backer per half, terminates upper colors 0.5 mm inside every physical part edge, and rebuilds each convenience composite through Blender's Manifold Boolean. The final filter may discard only components smaller than 0.1 mm with absolute volume below 0.001 mm³; every retained composite then passes the exact-coordinate mesh audit.

Large r1–r8 geometry directories are reproducible intermediates and are not release artifacts. Their scoped findings remain in the adjacent JSON record; r9 preserves final Blender raw traces, build inputs, hashes, mesh audits, 3MF packaging and exact target-slicer evidence.
