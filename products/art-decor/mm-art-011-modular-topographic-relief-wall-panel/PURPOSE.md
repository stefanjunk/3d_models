# Purpose — Permanent One Off Topographic Relief Wall Panel

Two unique permanent 600 x 400 mm topographic wall artworks: Harz and the Rheinisches Braunkohlerevier. Each one-off is printed as two 300 x 400 mm four-color halves on the Anycubic Kobra 3 Max Combo and permanently joined into one non-serviceable relief. Local rear seam keys and isolated hanger/standoff blocks provide assembly and an 18 mm halo cavity without a rear grid. Selected terrain-derived paths pass optional customer-added light through the visible face.
