# MM-ART-011 — Permanent One-off Topographic Relief Wall Panels

Status: **requirements revision 0.3.0 approved; replacement concept v02 awaiting approval; no CAD, coupon or print candidate exists**.

The planned pilots are unique 600 × 400 mm Harz and Rheinisches Braunkohlerevier terrain reliefs. Revision 0.3.0 uses two 300 × 400 mm four-color main prints per pilot, permanently joined without glue by three concealed one-way connector springs. Continuous 16-bit elevation geometry remains independent from broad abstract four-color altitude bands. Isolated snap-in hanger/standoff parts prepare an 18 mm halo-light cavity and protected light-through openings without a rear grid; electrical hardware remains a customer add-on.

The authoritative approved requirements are in `design-spec.yaml`; the concise approval record is `requirements-review-0.3.0.md`. The current visual review is `concept-review-0.3.0.md` with `concepts/permanent-relief-collection-concept-v02.png`. Terrain acquisition, production geometry and coupons remain blocked until concept approval.

Historical concept: `concepts/modular-relief-collection-concept-v01.png`; controlled notes: `concept-review.md`. It documents the rejected six-tile rear-grid direction and is not current design authority.

Historical decomposition: `decomposition-review.md` and `plan/hybrid-design-plan.json`. Both are superseded by revision 0.3.0 and cannot authorize CAD.
