# Concept review — MM-ART-011 Harz and Rheinisches Revier

Review artifact: `concepts/modular-relief-collection-concept-v01.png`

The image is a non-authoritative product visualization. The Harz and Rheinisches Braunkohlerevier pilots will use frozen official terrain sources only after concept approval; the terrain shown in the concept image is deliberately schematic.

## Visual direction proposed for approval

- Continuous sculptural terrain carries four broad horizontal color bands; no dithering, tiny color islands or realistic land-cover claim.
- Harz palette `harz_moss_stone`, low to high: Anycubic PLA Matte Dark Green, Chocolate Brown, Caramel and Bone White.
- Rheinisches-Revier palette `rhenish_industrial_earth`, low to high/semantic bench: Anycubic PLA Matte Black, Chocolate Brown, Desert Tan and Orange.
- Harz light-through paths are derived from a few simplified valley or contour lines. Rheinisches-Revier paths use selected simplified mine-bench or infrastructure traces.
- Six individually printable and replaceable front tiles align on the same segmented rear-grid family as Berlin. Each relief remains complete and readable without lighting.
- Customer-added lighting can create both a wall halo and controlled lines of light on the front; LEDs, controller, power supply, wiring and diffuser are not part of the product.

## Controlled dimensions and provisional tolerances

- Assembled artwork per pilot: 600 × 400 mm landscape.
- Grid: 3 columns × 2 rows; module pitch 200 × 200 mm; each printable part must fit a 220 × 220 mm build area.
- Terrain art-tile envelope: maximum 200 × 200 × 12 mm; 3 mm backer; relief depth no more than 7 mm; target total terrain tile thickness no more than 10 mm.
- Internal visible seam target: 0.25 mm, selected later with a 0.15 / 0.25 / 0.35 / 0.45 mm physical coupon.
- Outer frame target: 8 mm.
- Wall standoff / halo cavity: 18 mm nominal.
- Optional LED envelope: 12 × 4 mm keep-out for nominal 8 or 10 mm strips; cable channel at least 6 × 4 mm; exits left, right and bottom.
- Light openings: at least 2.0 mm wide, at least 5.0 mm solid ligament between openings, no more than 12% open area per tile.
- Protected solid zones: 8 mm from tile seams and 12 mm from datums or retaining features; optional rear diffuser land 6 mm wide.

Approval of this concept authorizes detailed decomposition and production-CAD work, but not physical printing, electrical qualification or final commercial release.
