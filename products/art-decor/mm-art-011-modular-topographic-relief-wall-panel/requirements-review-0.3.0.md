# Requirements review 0.3.0 — MM-ART-011 Harz and Rheinisches Revier

Status: **approval requested; concept, terrain acquisition and CAD blocked**.

## User-stated

- Two unique 600 × 400 mm topographic wall reliefs: Harz and Rheinisches Braunkohlerevier, each made from multiple 3D prints.
- No rear grid and no replaceable sections.
- Four-color printing on the Anycubic Kobra 3 Max Combo.
- Abstract rather than pseudo-realistic color treatment with low purge waste.
- Optional customer-added rear halo lighting plus deliberate light-through openings; electrical parts are excluded.

## Inferred

- Each pilot becomes one permanently assembled, non-serviceable artwork.
- Continuous 16-bit height geometry remains independent from the four broad color bands.
- The artworks must remain visually complete, mechanically mountable and ventilated without lighting installed.
- Small local connectors and isolated wall spacers are permissible only as permanent assembly/mounting parts, not as a raster or exchange interface.

## Recommended for approval

- Split each pilot once vertically into two 300 × 400 mm main prints. Both fit the verified 420 × 420 mm bed and create only one visible internal seam.
- Permanently register and bond each pair with a shallow tongue/groove and three small rear bridge keys. Select the actual allowance and adhesive by coupons before full assembly.
- Create the 18 mm halo gap with isolated bonded upper hanger/standoff and lower standoff blocks; do not add a rear frame, grid, magnets or removable retainers.
- Reserve 12 × 4 mm open LED-strip envelopes for nominal 8/10 mm strips, 6 × 4 mm cable paths, left/right/bottom exits and protected ≥2 mm face openings. Lighting remains an unsupplied add-on.
- Retain four continuous-height bands with only three global Z changes per half: `harz_moss_stone` for Harz and `rhenish_industrial_earth` for the mining region; no dithering or realistic terrain-color claim.
- Build Harz from a frozen Copernicus GLO-30 source and the changing mining region from a frozen current GeoBasis NRW DGM1 source after concept approval.

## Consequential item requiring approval

Approve the two-half permanent assembly, including the small local rear bridge keys and isolated bonded hanger/standoff blocks. These reduce the seam count and enable the halo gap, but each finished artwork is intentionally not serviceable and therefore requires bond, flatness and proof-load coupons.

Approval of this requirements revision authorizes a new visual concept. It does not authorize terrain download/processing, production CAD, printing, electrical work, wall installation or commercial release.
