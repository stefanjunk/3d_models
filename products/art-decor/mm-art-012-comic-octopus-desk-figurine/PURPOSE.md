# Purpose — MM-ART-012 Comic Octopus Desk Figurine

Create a funny, uplifting comic octopus figurine for decorative display on a dry
indoor adult desk or shelf. The character should read as cheerful from across a
desk, stand securely on its own tentacles, and remain printable as one coherent
organic body.

The workflow is intentionally gated:

1. register and preflight the product;
2. approve requirements revision `0.1.0`;
3. create and review a product concept image;
4. only after explicit concept approval, create the organic geometry with the
   licensed local geometry-only Step1X-3D workflow;
5. engineer, validate, print-test and commercially clear any release candidate.

This is not a children's toy, a functional holder, a load-bearing part, or a
character derived from third-party artwork. No concept image, mesh, print or
commercial release exists in the intake phase.
