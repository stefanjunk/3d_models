# Contributor IP Agreement — Counsel Checklist

Use before work starts. Obtain a localized signed agreement; this is not a contract.

- Parties, authority, employment/contractor/client status, governing law
- Services, deliverables, acceptance, payment
- Background IP schedule and license to use it
- Assignment or sufficiently broad license for CAD, code, meshes, drawings, images, renders, documentation, inventions, designs and modifications
- Rights to reproduce, adapt, manufacture, distribute files, sell products, advertise, sublicense customers/contractors and enforce
- Patent/design filing, inventorship and further-assurance cooperation
- Moral-right waiver/consent where lawful
- Third-party/open-source/asset disclosure and approval policy
- Warranty/evidence for every input
- AI tools, approved accounts/models, input rights, confidentiality, output review and disclosure
- Confidential information, unpublished inventions, security, export-controlled data
- Privacy/personal data/model releases
- Ownership of improvements and reusable tools
- Portfolio/publicity permission
- Indemnity/liability/insurance aligned with law
- Termination, survival, return/deletion and record retention
- Signatures and evidence hash
