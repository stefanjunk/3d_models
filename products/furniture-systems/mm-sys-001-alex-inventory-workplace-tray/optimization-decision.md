# Optimization decision

The shared R0.2 concept is the recoverable baseline. This revision keeps its proven asymmetric layout and 210 × 160 × 32 mm envelope, but makes the product independently controlled for a 0.4 mm nozzle:

- perimeter and divider walls are 2.70 mm, exactly six nominal 0.45 mm lines;
- the circular tool-zone wall is also parameterized at 2.70 mm;
- the 2.40 mm floor and all divider roots remain protected;
- no dense surface texture, decorative mesh or global decimation is introduced;
- full-width gauges reduce the cost of learning the real drawer interface before a full tray print.

Direct CAD tessellation is selected. Mesh simplification is not beneficial because the analytic model is already small and the interface end faces must remain exact.
