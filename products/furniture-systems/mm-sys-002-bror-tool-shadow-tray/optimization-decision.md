# Optimization decision

The selected 216 mm envelope retains 2 mm build-plate margin per side on a nominal 220 mm bed, unlike the zero-margin 220 mm shared concept. A 2.70 mm perimeter equals six nominal 0.45 mm lines. The 2.40 mm protected floor ligament is unchanged beneath 5.40 mm recesses.

Tool features are modeled as shallow negative pockets rather than separate dense meshes. Direct process-sized CAD tessellation is selected; global mesh decimation is not beneficial.
