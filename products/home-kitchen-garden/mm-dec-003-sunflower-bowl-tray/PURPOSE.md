# Purpose — MM-DEC-003 Sunflower Bowl / Tray

Provide a one-piece sunflower-inspired printable catchall tray for adult indoor decorative storage of lightweight, dry, non-food items.

## Current scope

- Revision: `0.2.0` digital candidate.
- Geometry source: fresh prompt-bound image and local geometry-only Step1X-3D run.
- Geometry boundary: the Step1X flower body is not repaired or parametrically reconstructed; only uniform registration and the owner-confirmed foot Boolean are allowed.
- Parametric authority: 80 mm diameter × 6 mm underside disc foot only.
- Manufacturing baseline: Anycubic Kobra 3 Max, 0.4 mm hardened nozzle, 0.20 mm SUNLU PETG profile with build-plate-only automatic tree support.
- Forbidden claims: food contact, tableware, watertightness, liquid service, plant-pot use, outdoor use, child toy, structural or safety-critical use.

## Evidence and status

The legacy `1.stl`, `2.stl`, old 3MF, legacy OpenSCAD package and external model folders are excluded from the new derivative chain because their commercial provenance is inadequate. If the suspected old generator was Hunyuan3D-2, the current public community licence also excludes EU territory; see `evidence/legacy-license-audit-v0.2.0.md`.

Physical rocking, tilt, support removal, edge comfort, snagging, final layer preview, final dimensions/yellow material, marking, IP/market review and signed commercial approvals remain open.
