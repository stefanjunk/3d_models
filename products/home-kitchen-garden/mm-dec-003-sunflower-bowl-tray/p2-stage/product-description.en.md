# MM-DEC-003 Sunflower Bowl / Tray — digital draft

MM-DEC-003 is a one-piece, sunflower-shaped decorative catchall tray for lightweight, dry, non-food items such as keys or jewellery. The print set contains one Step1X-generated flower body with an independently generated 80 mm diameter × 6 mm parametric disc foot.

This P2 handoff represents revision 0.2.0 as a digital prototype only. The flower body has not been repaired, simplified or parametrically reconstructed. Its visible asymmetric, fused and wrinkled petal regions remain subject to owner appearance review. The current Anycubic Kobra 3 Max PETG plan uses automatic tree support from the build plate; support access and surface damage must be checked in the slicer preview and by a supervised physical print.

No claim is made for food contact, liquid containment, outdoor exposure, child use, structural loading, physical qualification, rights clearance, product safety or commercial readiness. The exact self-intersection review, release marking and all human release approvals remain open.
