# MM-DEC-004 — Sleepy Cat nursery-pot sleeve

This product is a decorative, dry-indoor sleeve for a removable succulent nursery pot. A sleeping cat curls around the inserted pot and hides the utilitarian container.

The Step1X stage owns only the appearance-led cat body. The pot cavity, wall, flat seat and drainage/vent path remain parametric CAD features. The launch product does not hold soil or water directly and makes no watertight planter claim.

Out of scope are child-toy use, food contact, outdoor exposure, a fixed fit before the customer's nursery pot is measured, and any commercial offer before pot-fit, drainage and tip-stability tests pass.
