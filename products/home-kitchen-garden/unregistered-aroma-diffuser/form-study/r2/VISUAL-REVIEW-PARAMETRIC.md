# Read-only review of actual geometry, Run 002

Reviewer: /root/concept_visual_review, 3d_design_reviewer.
Evidence: R1 hero and actual Run 002 hero, side and back renders.
No files modified by reviewer. No human appearance approval recorded.

Result: recognizable and coherent flame study across all three views, but
substantial deviation from the elegant source. The back reads as an open
wavy shell. This is style approximation, not faithful reconstruction.

Three priority changes:

1. Develop the crown from bundled ribs into two soft unequal tongues.
   Current broad wall endings, blunt tips and open U-cut dominate.
2. Sharpen rounded rib crests and separate valleys; continue the rails
   through waist/crown without visible terminations.
3. Carry the full lower belly higher before tapering to balance the long
   crown and reduce the screw-like appearance.

Main-agent decision: retain Run 002 as the reproducible editable baseline;
evaluate a separate Step1X preform before choosing the exterior method.
Geometry/fit/printability were explicitly outside this visual review.
