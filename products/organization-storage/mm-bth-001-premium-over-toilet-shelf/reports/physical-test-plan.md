# Revision 0.2.0 physical qualification plan

The complete machine-readable plan is in `test-plan.yaml`. All physical results are currently **not run**. The 4 kg value is a design target, not a released rating.

## Required test identity

Record printer, firmware, slicer/version, 3MF/profile hash, PETG supplier/product/batch/color, drying/conditioning, nozzle, line width, layer height, orientation, products/organization-storage/mm-wall-001-honeycomb-wood-wall-shelf/infill, ambient condition, purchased hardware part numbers, specimen hashes and installation substrate.

## Safe sequence

1. Exact slicer review and 3MF archive.
2. Frame/fit, M3 seam, floor-foot/TPU-lock and heat-set/fastener coupons.
3. Unloaded full assembly on a level floor and measured wall mockup.
4. Site/service-clearance review and module/drawer checks.
5. 4 kg/24 h shelf creep test.
6. 8 kg/1 h guarded proof test.
7. 1000 shelf cycles and 5000 drawer cycles.
8. Substrate-specific guarded 100 N anti-tip test.
9. Cleaning/moisture check.
10. Long-term creep/inspection evidence before any five-year durability statement.

Use nonfragile ballast, calibrated force/deflection measurement, an exclusion zone and a physical barrier. Do not conduct the first proof or anti-tip tests over an installed toilet or near people, glass, valuables or plumbing. Stop on cracking, audible fracture, fastener motion, anchor movement, excessive deflection or loss of stability.

## Acceptance highlights

- 4 kg evenly distributed for 24 h: midspan deflection <= 2.0 mm; residual set <= 0.5 mm.
- 8 kg evenly distributed for one hour: no crack/slip; residual set <= 0.5 mm.
- Shelf: 1000 nonimpact service cycles without functional failure.
- M3 seam coupon: each plate sits flat on both boss tops before tightening; no crack, pull-out, harmful slip or permanent seam step above 0.5 mm after the defined proof/cycle sequence.
- Drawer: 5000 full-travel cycles without jam, seam release or functional failure.
- Modules: 1.0 kg drawer, 2.0 kg bin/tray individual targets and 10 N horizontal retention without crossing the front stop; keep the shelf total <= 4 kg.
- Hangers: 0.5 kg for 500 cycles and 1.0 kg proof for 60 s without release or residual set above 0.5 mm.
- Floor interface coupon: M4 lock retained; TPU pad survives 100 N shear and 50 N pull-off without release.
- Anti-tip: 100 N at the upper front edge for 60 s without release, anchor movement, frame slip or restraint crack.
- Four floor pads bear without rocking; all toilet/lid/pipe/flush/baseboard/service paths remain clear.

No result may be transferred to another printer, filament, profile, wall substrate or anchor system without justified repeat evidence.
The five-year service-life value remains an unverified target; the short qualification sequence cannot establish it.
