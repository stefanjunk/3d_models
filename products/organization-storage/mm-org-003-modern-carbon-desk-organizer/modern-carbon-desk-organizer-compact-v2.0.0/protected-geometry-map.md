# Protected geometry map

| Region | Owner | Protection |
|---|---|---|
| Housing drawer-bay side faces | housing | exact; no texture, windows or relief |
| Housing deck side rails | housing | continuous 12 mm guide/load paths on all three decks |
| Housing deck front/rear beams | housing | continuous 12 mm crossmembers; no texture or openings |
| Rear stops/back wall | housing | continuous, flat and untextured internally |
| Housing bottom and rear print datum | housing | zero texture; exact plane |
| Drawer side and bottom contact faces | drawer | exact; zero texture |
| Drawer front scoop and rim | drawer | smooth hand-contact keep-out |
| Drawer bottom skin | drawer | continuous 2.0 mm small-item containment |
| Sorter compartment interiors | sorter | smooth, cleanable and untextured |
| Sorter divider junctions | sorter | continuous into bottom and outer shell |
| Stack registration features | housing + sorter | exact nominal 0.35 mm/side clearance |
| Texture badge borders | every textured body | minimum 4 mm smooth frame |
| Engraved host walls | every textured body | minimum 1.76 mm residual wall |

The procedural texture may only alter named exterior badge patches. Global displacement, decimation or automatic repair is prohibited on protected regions.
