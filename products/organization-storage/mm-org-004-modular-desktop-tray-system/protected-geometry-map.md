# Protected geometry map

- `BED_DATUM`: continuous tray floor perimeter and connector lower face.
- `CONTAINMENT_SKIN`: closed floor and all inner tray walls; no windows or exposed infill.
- `HAND_RIM`: rounded top rim and all user-contact edges.
- `RECEIVER_BOSSES`: local material around four side socket centers.
- `SOCKET_ROOF`: minimum roof thickness above the bottom-inserted connector.
- `LINK_DATUM`: connector lower face and paired dovetail head outline.
- `INTERFACE_KEEPOUT`: socket volume plus 4 mm margin; no cosmetic or lightweight cuts.
- `ASSEMBLY_GAP`: 1.0 mm nominal wall-to-wall gap between joined modules.

Only redundant bulk outside these zones may be reduced. The clean inner surface, desk-contact plane and all module-interface dimensions are protected.
