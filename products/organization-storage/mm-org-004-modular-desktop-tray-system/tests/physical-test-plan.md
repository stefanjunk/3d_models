# Deferred physical test plan

The user asked to skip final validation during this modeling pass. These tests are therefore `NOT_RUN` and must be performed on the unchanged `0.1.0-draft.1` geometry before any product or fit claim.

1. Print the interface coupon in the intended printer/material/profile; record socket bridge quality and connector insertion force.
2. If needed, sweep per-side clearance at 0.20, 0.30 and 0.40 mm without changing the receiver datum.
3. Assemble two trays on a flat desk; verify the desk traps the link and that ordinary handling does not separate the modules.
4. Complete 100 insert/remove cycles; inspect cracks, whitening, looseness and receiver-roof damage.
5. Load each full tray with 1.0 kg distributed mass for 24 h; measure permanent floor deflection and desk rocking.
6. Inspect every top edge and socket area for sharpness or snagging.
7. Slice and print the unchanged full set; confirm floor flatness and the intended arrangement.
