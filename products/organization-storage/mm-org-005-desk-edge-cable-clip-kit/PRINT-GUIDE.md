# DRAFT print guide

- Material: PETG, dry and supplier-profiled.
- Starting point: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, 0.45 mm line width.
- Orientation: lay the large side profile flat; do not print upright across the flexure root.
- Supports: none intended.
- First print: combined fit coupon; do not scale clip and coupon independently.
- Use at least four perimeters as a starting point so the 1.8–2.4 mm flexure is path-dominated; confirm actual wall paths in the slicer.

No exact slicer profile or fatigue result is included.
