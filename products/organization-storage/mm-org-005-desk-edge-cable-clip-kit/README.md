# MM-ORG-005 — Desk-edge Cable Clip Kit

Parametric PETG clip family for keeping passive low-voltage charging/data cables accessible at a desk edge. This package implements research idea `SKU-003` and remains a DRAFT digital engineering candidate until printed fit, insertion-cycle, cable-pinch and desk-marking tests pass.

The controlled generator accepts desk thickness, cable diameter and clip count. The default kit exports three size combinations plus a low-material desk/cable fit gauge.

Not electrical insulation, strain relief, a load-bearing clamp or a child-use product.
