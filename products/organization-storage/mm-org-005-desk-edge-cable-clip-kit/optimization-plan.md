# Optimization plan and comparison

The frozen A baseline uses uniform 3.0 mm upper/lower arms and the same C-ring, printer hypothesis and orientation. The selected B geometry uses a 2.4→1.8 mm tapered lower leaf, 2.0 mm fixed arm, hollow C-ring and local front spine. All desk/cable contacts, the flexure root, hard stop and print datum remain protected.

| Candidate | Lever | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A | 3.0 mm rectangular U arms; 0.4 mm process | baseline, thick preset exceeds the preferred section economy |
| B | tapered leaf and C-ring at same process | selected digital geometry; about 23.1% lower analytic CAD volume |
| C | B with 0.6 mm nozzle hypothesis | not selected without exact slicing and resolved flexure path count |

No print-time, deposited-mass, retention-force or fatigue saving is claimed. Exact slicing and process-matched PETG coupons are mandatory before a print candidate.
