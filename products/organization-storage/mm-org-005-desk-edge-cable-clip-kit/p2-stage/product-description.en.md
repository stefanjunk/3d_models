# Desk-edge Cable Clip Kit

Parametric PETG clip family for keeping passive low-voltage charging/data cables accessible at a desk edge. This package implements research idea `SKU-003` and remains a DRAFT digital engineering candidate until printed fit, insertion-cycle, cable-pinch and desk-marking tests pass.

## P2 digital-candidate contents

This English development description applies to `MM-ORG-005` revision `0.1.0-draft.1`.
The complete candidate 3MF contains 3 declared print object(s):
thin_12mm_3p5, standard_15mm_5p0, thick_18mm_7p0. The included project preserves the selected build orientation and
the recorded support decision for the candidate.

## Development boundary

This is a digital P2 candidate, not a photograph or a physically qualified or
commercially released product. Fit, finish, strength, safety, rights clearance,
material behaviour and storefront claims remain governed by their separate
evidence and approval gates.
