# Deferred physical test plan

All items are `NOT_RUN` for `0.1.0-draft.1`.

1. Print the gauge/coupon in the intended dry PETG profile; measure actual jaw and cable-slot dimensions.
2. Check all three desk classes on representative sealed wood and laminate edges, including edge radii.
3. Record insertion/removal force, then complete 250 desk cycles and inspect root whitening, cracks and permanent set.
4. Insert matching cables; verify free sliding without visible jacket flattening, then complete 500 cable cycles.
5. Leave each clip installed 7 days; inspect desk finish for gloss change, dents or marks.
6. Confirm the clip releases before damaging a cable when pulled accidentally; no numerical release claim is allowed without a repeatable fixture.
7. Slice and print the unchanged full set only after the coupon is accepted.
