# DRAFT print guide

- PETG bar: base down, 0.4 mm nozzle / 0.20 mm layer starting point, no supports intended.
- TPU inserts: flat base down, dry TPU 95A starting assumption; slow bridge/perimeter settings must be chosen in the exact slicer.
- First print: paired socket/insert coupon; do not scale mating parts independently.
- Keep material/profile identity with every coupon result. A fit in PLA does not qualify the PETG/TPU interface.

No exact slicer project or physical result is included.
