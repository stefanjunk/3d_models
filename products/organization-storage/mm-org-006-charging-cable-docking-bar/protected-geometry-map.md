# Protected geometry map

- `BASE_FOOTPRINT`: full 156 × 40 mm stability and desk-contact envelope.
- `BASE_SKIN`: continuous 3.0 mm skin; no open underside lattice.
- `EDGE_BEAMS`: front/rear perimeter strips tying all socket cells.
- `SOCKET_DATUMS`: pocket floors, X/Y walls and top insertion edges.
- `INSERT_ENVELOPE`: 22 × 16 × 11 mm cartridge body and its print datum.
- `RETENTION_RIBS`: two side ribs and their pocket relief zones.
- `CABLE_BORE`: diameter-specific circular jacket interface plus radial clearance.
- `ENTRY_NOSES`: top slit edges; no labels, ribs or texture may intrude.

Only material outside the base skin, edge beams and local socket walls may be removed from the rigid bar.
