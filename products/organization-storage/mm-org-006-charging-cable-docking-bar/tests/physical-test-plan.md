# Deferred physical test plan

All items are `NOT_RUN` for `0.1.0-draft.1`.

1. Print the paired socket/insert coupon in the exact PETG and TPU profiles; record insertion/removal force and visible rib compression.
2. Repeat with 0.15, 0.25 and 0.35 mm pocket clearance if the nominal pair binds or rattles.
3. Complete 100 insert replacement cycles; inspect pocket walls, ribs, permanent set and loosening.
4. Test matching cable jackets at 3.5, 5, 7 and 9 mm; verify no sharp contact or visible flattening.
5. Complete 500 cable insertion cycles per insert and inspect jacket and slit noses.
6. Dock and remove cables one-handed on a clean desk; record bar sliding/tipping and decide whether a non-slip pad or mounting variant is required.
7. Print the unchanged full set only after coupon acceptance.
