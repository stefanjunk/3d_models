# DRAFT print guide

- PETG starting point: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, 0.45 mm line width.
- Base: plate down. Backrest: rear panel down. Pin: vertical with brim if needed. Coupon: largest flat face down.
- No generated supports intended; inspect the small hinge-bore bridges in the exact slicer.
- Print coupon first and do not mix pin/base/backrest revisions.

No exact profile, G-code or physical hinge/stability result is included.
