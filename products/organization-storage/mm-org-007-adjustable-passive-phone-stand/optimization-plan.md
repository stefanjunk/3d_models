# Optimization plan and comparison

The A baseline is a solid 95 mm wide triangular wedge with the same footprint and back height. The selected B design protects the support polygon, rear beam, hinge axis, detents, pin stops and phone-contact shelf while replacing the wedge bulk with a 5 mm plate, local hinge ears and a windowed backrest.

| Candidate | Lever | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A | solid wedge, 0.4 mm process | frozen analytic baseline |
| B | wide plate + local ears + windowed hinged backrest | selected; about 80.9% lower selected-set CAD volume |
| C | B plus 0.6 mm nozzle hypothesis | not selected without exact PETG slicing |

The reduction is an analytic CAD-volume comparison, not a claim for deposited mass or print time. Stability, hinge friction and the preferred detent profile require the unchanged physical assembly and coupon.
