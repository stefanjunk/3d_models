# Protected geometry map

- `SUPPORT_POLYGON`: full 95 × 95 mm base and rear beam/mass.
- `HINGE_EARS`: two side plates, bores and root gussets.
- `HINGE_BARREL`: continuous central backrest barrel and bore.
- `PIN_AXIS`: 6 mm pin, 0.25 mm radial clearance and axial stops.
- `DETENT_PATH`: 9 mm nub radius, three angle sockets and side-face contact.
- `DEVICE_SHELF`: 14 mm case capacity, front lip, central cable notch and rounded access edges.
- `BACKREST_FRAME`: perimeter and crossmembers surrounding the central material-saving window.

Material removal may occur only inside the backrest window and non-interface base zones.
