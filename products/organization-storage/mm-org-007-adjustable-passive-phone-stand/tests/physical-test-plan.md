# Deferred physical test plan

1. Print hinge/detent coupon in the exact PETG profile; compare soft, medium and firm socket engagement.
2. Select one base without altering common hinge/nub geometry; record insertion and adjustment force.
3. Complete 500 full-angle cycles and inspect nub/socket wear, barrel cracks, pin migration and looseness.
4. Test up to 350 g phones in portrait and landscape at 55°, 65° and 75°; record tip/slide with one-handed docking and screen taps.
5. Inspect every device contact and cable bend/notch for scratching, button interference and connector stress.
6. Print the unchanged selected base/backrest/pin set. All tests remain `NOT_RUN` in this modeling pass.
