# Print guide

Print the diameter guide first, flat in PETG at 0.20 mm layers. Its holes run left-to-right as 14, 16, 18, 20, 22, 24 and 26 mm plus 0.20 mm capture clearance. Record each tube at its widest insertion region. Print the grid base-down without supports only after updating the JSON diameter list. Start with 4 walls and 20% infill; exact slicing is intentionally not claimed.
