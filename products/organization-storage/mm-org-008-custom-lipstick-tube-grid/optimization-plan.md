# Optimization plan

Baseline A is a solid 161 × 107 × 34 mm block with blind pockets. Candidate B, selected digitally, uses a 4 mm floor, 4 mm top deck, 2.4 mm perimeter and orthogonal ribs. Candidate C adds a 0.6 mm nozzle hypothesis but remains unselected without exact slicing. The reported CAD-volume reduction is not a print-time or mass claim.
