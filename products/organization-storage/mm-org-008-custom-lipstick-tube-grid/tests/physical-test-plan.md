# Physical test plan

1. Confirm every intended tube passes the selected guide hole without forcing.
2. Load all 12 pockets; tip the assembly 10° in four directions and observe movement.
3. Perform 100 insert/remove cycles on smallest and largest pockets; inspect abrasion and cracking.
4. Wipe all exposed floors, ribs and walls with the intended cleaning method; record unreachable residue zones.
5. Inspect edges and verify no uncontained cosmetic formulation is stored in the organizer.
