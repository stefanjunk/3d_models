# Concept review — MM-ORG-009 / concept v1

The concept uses two independently tapered, open-underside shell rails. A straight inner face registers loosely against the organizer while the outer face follows the measured drawer-wall taper. Rounded top edges, end relief and two finger scallops allow removal without forcing fingertips into a closed pinch gap. A separate gauge exposes small front/rear taper and clearance choices before the full rails are printed.

The concept deliberately avoids clamps, adhesives and interference. It is a dimensional customization system, not a load-bearing drawer component.
