# Functional decomposition

## Printed parts

1. `left-rail`: an independently tapered shell rail for the left perimeter gap.
2. `right-rail`: an independently tapered shell rail for the right perimeter gap.
3. `taper-gauge`: a compact 2–26 mm wedge gauge with countable edge notches for front/rear measurement and clearance checks.

Each rail is one integral solid. The protected straight organizer-side wall, tapered drawer-side wall, end walls and top skin surround an open hidden underside. Cross-ribs touch both walls and the top, restrict roof bridge spans, and provide continuous load paths to the print bed.

## Purchased parts

None. Adhesives, fasteners, magnets and clamps would add assembly, surface or retention risks without improving the passive filler function.

## Interfaces and datums

- Datum A: planar rail underside / drawer bottom.
- Datum B: straight organizer-side face at local `y = 0`.
- Datum C: tapered drawer-side face defined by the effective front and rear widths.
- The effective width is the measured gap minus organizer and drawer-wall clearances.
- Both rails remain loose parts. They are not intended to wedge, clamp or carry structural drawer loads.

## Assembly and maintenance

1. Measure the gap near the drawer front and rear with the gauge or calipers.
2. Enter the independent left/right front/rear gap values.
3. Print the gauge first, then the unchanged rails after confirming the chosen clearance.
4. Place each rail with the straight face toward the organizer and tapered face toward the drawer wall.
5. Lift from a top scallop for removal; clean with a dry or lightly damp cloth compatible with the selected filament.

No reusable component is promoted to the parts library before physical fit and finish-contact evidence exists.
