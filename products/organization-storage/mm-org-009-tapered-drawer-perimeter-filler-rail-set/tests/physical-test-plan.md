# Deferred physical test plan

Record printer/unit, firmware, slicer/version, complete profile hash, nozzle, exact filament manufacturer/product/color/batch, conditioning, bed surface, ambient conditions and measurement tool for every result.

| ID | Test | Method | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| TP-01 | Gauge dimensional accuracy | Measure indicated 6/10/14/18/22/26 mm widths with calibrated calipers. | Each within the project-specific dimensional tolerance selected after printer calibration. |
| TP-02 | Clearance selection | Insert gauge at front/rear on both sides without forcing. | Chosen width is repeatable and leaves a removable loose fit. |
| TP-03 | First customized rail fit | Print one rail from captured measurements. | Sits flat, closes the intended gap and neither wedges nor rattles objectionably. |
| TP-04 | Pinch-free removal | Remove via each lift scallop with the organizer installed. | No trapped fingertip/tool need; no sharp or painful contact. |
| TP-05 | Finish contact | Install/remove on three representative drawer finishes. | No visible scratch, dent, rub polish or pigment transfer under the documented method. |
| TP-06 | Cycle | Complete 100 removal/reinstallation cycles. | No cracking, permanent warp, loss of acceptable fit or new sharp edge. |
| TP-07 | Drawer operation | Open/close the loaded drawer through full travel. | Rails stay below the usable height envelope and do not obstruct travel or contents. |
| TP-08 | Appearance and cleaning | Inspect edges, bridge roofs and clean with intended method. | Acceptable appearance; no inaccessible debris trap for the stated dry-organizer use. |

No `PASS` is inferred in this repository until measured results are attached.
