# Concept review

Status: `AUTO_APPROVED` for digital implementation.

Selected concept: a stepped, support-free caddy chassis with a continuous closed base, three rear wells, one front small-parts well, an angled base-connected phone backrest and a vertically sliding personalized front plate.

The plate is printed flat and uses engraved 5x7 geometric glyphs. This removes installed-font variance, external font licensing and unsupported thin curves. The body prints upright without modeled support. A miniature channel holder and plate reuse the production clearances as the first-print coupon.

Rejected alternatives:

- External system fonts: non-reproducible and create a separate licence/provenance dependency.
- Permanent name text fused to the body: makes every name regenerate and reprint the largest part.
- Phone shelf attached only to a thin upper wall: contradicted by current review evidence about instability.
- Mesh or perforated base: conflicts with the pen-tip problem signal.
