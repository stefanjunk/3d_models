# Optimization plan

Protected first: continuous base, wall/partition load paths, phone slot, plate guides and glyph pixels.

Baseline A is a full 150 x 120 x 128 mm block. Candidate B is the selected thin-base, thin-wall stepped shell. The deterministic build requires at least 65% CAD-volume reduction and one valid solid. Exact grams and print time require the eventual exact slicer profile and remain `NOT_RUN`.

No downstream mesh decimation is planned: analytic tessellation is already below budget and the small engraved pixels and fit channels are protected.
