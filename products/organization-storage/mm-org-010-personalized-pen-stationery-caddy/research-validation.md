# SKU-018 demand and problem validation

Checked 2026-08-27 for the German market. This is directional planning evidence, not a sales forecast.

## Decision

Proceed to the smallest fit coupon and a parametric digital print candidate. The evidence supports the job-to-be-done, while competition is strong enough that the design must differentiate through measured compartments, a stable phone cradle and a repeatable personalization workflow.

## Qualified signals

1. German and Germany-deliverable listings actively sell personalized pen holders from EUR 14.50 to above EUR 30; Etsy also exposes 3D-printed personalized desk organizers around the upper end of that range. Sources: https://www.etsy.com/de/market/schreibtisch_organizer_personalisiert and https://gluecks-dinge.de/produkt/stiftehalter-schreibtisch/
2. A current Etsy 3D-printed product varies overall length with the entered name and warns buyers to verify capitalization. This validates personalization demand and exposes a proofing/long-name failure mode. Source: https://www.etsy.com/de/listing/4545579708/personalisierter-schreibtisch-organizer
3. A German 3D-printed competitor caps names at 16 characters and scales long names down. This is direct evidence for an explicit length gate and minimum-feature check. Source: https://fyda-printwerk.com/products/personalisierter-hoodie-stiftehalter-mit-name-3d-gedruckter-schreibtisch-organizer
4. Reviews of a four-compartment organizer report that standard notes and business cards do not fit; the reviewed pocket was only about 83 mm wide. This supports dimension-driven compartments rather than generic-looking pockets. Source: https://www.testbericht.de/produkte/relaxdays-schreibtisch-organizer-schwarz-lederimitat-4-faecher-18x12x10cm-10021945_0
5. The same review set reports that placing a phone in an upper shelf makes it fold or tip backward. The phone load path must therefore terminate in the base and rear compartment structure. Same source as signal 4.
6. Verified German-market reviews repeatedly say organizers are smaller than imagery suggests, while another model is judged too large for stationery. This supports explicit envelope dimensions and a parameterized footprint. Sources: https://www.otto.de/kundenbewertungen/S0Z0Q0BR/ and https://www.testbericht.de/produkte/relaxdays-organizer-bambus-mit-griff-10020543
7. A verified review reports pencil and pen tips contacting the desk through a mesh bottom. The candidate therefore uses one continuous closed base. Source: https://www.kaufland.de/product/330761828/
8. Current German listings repeatedly combine separate storage for pens, scissors, rulers, small items and phones. This supports the proposed compartment set without proving the exact layout. Sources: https://www.otto.de/p/relaxdays-organizer-stiftehalter-schreibtisch-1-st-S0Y1F03F/ and https://praesent-promotion.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/roemer-lifestyle_2025.pdf

## Competitive boundary

- Avoid copying decorative competitor silhouettes, motifs, product photography or wording.
- Use original analytic geometry and a repository-owned 5x7 glyph system.
- Do not claim universal phone, stationery or name compatibility; publish the supported envelopes.
- Physical stability, tall-item tipping and plate fit remain print-test gates.
