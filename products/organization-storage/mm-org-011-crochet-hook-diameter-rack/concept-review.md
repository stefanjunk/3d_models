# Concept review

Selected concept: a one-piece three-tier comb rack with five independently sized side-entry slots per tier, plus a separate flat measurement card.

The side-entry path is the decisive feature: a shaft slides horizontally into its labelled pocket and the handle rests above the shelf. Continuous rising undersides create direct base-to-shelf load paths and avoid mid-air shelf starts in the intended orientation. The card captures shaft diameter and handle envelope before a custom parameter set is generated.

Rejected concepts: uniform closed holes repeat the documented ergonomic-handle mismatch; deep individual cups hide labels and consume more material; fitted brand-specific cavities would narrow compatibility and create avoidable IP/provenance risk.
