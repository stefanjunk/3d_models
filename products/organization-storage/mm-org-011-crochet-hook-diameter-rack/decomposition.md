# Decomposition

- **Parametric rack chassis:** base, three shelves, continuous rising support wedges, side-entry cuts and engraved position labels; one watertight manufacturing solid.
- **Measurement card:** independent flat solid driven by the same shaft range plus a handle-envelope series.
- **Profile data:** JSON is the source of truth for all fifteen sizes, labels and fit checks.
- **Build set:** two-object millimetre 3MF retains rack/card separation.
- **Deferred physical evidence:** actual hook measurements, insertion/removal, abrasion, cycle and stability results remain in this product folder after printing.

No purchased or organic components are required.
