# Deferred physical test plan

1. Print the gauge card and compare all 15 actual shafts plus handle major/minor envelopes; record printer, material, batch, nozzle, orientation and caliper method.
2. Replace the default profile values, rebuild unchanged revision inputs and print the rack.
3. Insert/remove all 15 hooks laterally ten times; no binding, forced flex or visible shaft abrasion.
4. Cycle the smallest, median and largest slot 500 times; inspect slot wear and label legibility.
5. Load all positions and retrieve front, middle and rear hooks one-handed; rack must not tip or lift.
6. Inspect all hand-contact edges and hook-contact surfaces for burrs or sharp seams.

These tests are intentionally not claimed as passed in the digital candidate.
