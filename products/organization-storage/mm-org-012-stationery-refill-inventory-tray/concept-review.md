# Concept review

Selected concept: a one-piece shallow inventory tray with five long rear packet lanes and three front scoop wells. Every floor rises toward the rear so the last package remains reachable at the labelled front stop. Thumb notches expose the package edge without eliminating retention.

Rejected concepts: loose-lead dispensers add spill and breakage risk; uniform rectangular cells waste space and do not encode stock type; removable bins add fit interfaces without improving the initial refill workflow.
