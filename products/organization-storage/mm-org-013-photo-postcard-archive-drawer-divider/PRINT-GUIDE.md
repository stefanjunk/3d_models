# Draft print guide

- PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer height.
- Print the cassette base-down and each divider broad-face-down with its engraving upward.
- Generated supports: none.
- Print the three U-gauges first and slide sleeved samples through the open, chamfered end.
- Confirm the tallest photo, the 68 mm tab and the target drawer all clear before printing the cassette.
- Do not place valuable originals directly against unqualified printed plastic; use a suitable photo-safe primary sleeve.
- No automated printer upload/start is authorized.
