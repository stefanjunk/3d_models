# Concept review

Selected a low one-piece slotted cassette plus separately flat-printed divider plates. A lateral index gutter keeps tabs visible beside the longest supported print; a low guide wall keeps the sleeved media out of that gutter. Full-size three-sided gauges test the actual paper/sleeve envelope without committing to the full frame.

Rejected a closed printed photo box because unqualified PLA cannot support archival claims, and rejected a fixed-cell tray because it cannot adapt to collection categories.

Decision: `AUTO_APPROVED` for parametric decomposition.
