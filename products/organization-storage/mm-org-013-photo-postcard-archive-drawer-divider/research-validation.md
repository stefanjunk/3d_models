# SKU-112 research validation

Checked 2026-08-27. The opportunity is positive for a measured drawer cassette with replaceable index dividers. It is not positioned as an archival enclosure.

## Qualified signals

1. A 2025 photo-organization discussion reports labelled lab sleeves still being stuffed into a drawer that is running out of room, with no durable physical indexing system: <https://www.reddit.com/r/AnalogCommunity/comments/1o5w7vq/how_do_you_organize_your_photos/>
2. Another collection owner describes decades of prints piled in boxes and calls finding a specific item a needle-in-a-haystack problem: <https://www.reddit.com/r/AnalogCommunity/comments/p1a55n/how_do_you_organizecatalog_your_negatives_and/>
3. A current German archive-box supplier sells separate 10 x 15 and 13 x 18 cm variants with loose, staggered labelled dividers, validating format-specific rails and readable index positions: <https://shop.zfb.com/Fotobox-mit-vier-losen-Stegen-und-verkuerztem-Deckel/FB1-14302>
4. German-language customer feedback on a 13 x 18 cm box reports nominal prints with excess width and asks for more divider cards, supporting explicit clearance and replaceable parametric dividers: <https://www.bawowna.eu/xl-photo-box-kandra-700-photos-13x18-cm-steel-blue>
5. A divider buyer had to trim 4 x 6 inch tabs because the box would not close, directly supporting a customer-supplied drawer-height limit and bounded tab height: <https://www.etsy.com/listing/934268384/30-nostalgia-blank-tab-dividers-for>
6. A 2026 multi-country photo survey including Germany reports that curation feels difficult while memories remain valuable, supporting a physical index workflow: <https://popsa.com/en-gb/perspectives/memory-economy-2026-photo-trends-report/>
7. Deutsche Post specifies the common A6 postcard as 148 x 105 mm; the other pilot formats are the German retail standards 10 x 15 and 13 x 18 cm: <https://www.deutschepost.de/de/p/print-mailing/einsteiger/vorlagen.html>
8. The Library of Congress recommends photo-safe primary sleeves/enclosures and warns that safe materials require ISO/PAT evidence. Untested printed PLA therefore gets no archival, acid-free or direct-contact claim: <https://www.loc.gov/preservation/care/photo.html>

## Design response

- Use a 210 x 170 mm fitted base with two low side rails, a low media stop and ten indexed divider positions.
- Keep labels in a lateral gutter beyond the 180 mm photo edge so they remain visible from above.
- Chamfer divider shoulders and gauge entrances; use positive Boolean cutter overlap at every open slot.
- Include full-size U-gauges for 10 x 15 cm, DIN A6 and 13 x 18 cm media.
- Require photo-safe sleeves for valuable originals and state dry indoor organization only.

## Gate decision

`PASS` for a digital CAD candidate. Physical paper snagging, tab visibility, actual drawer closure and three-format gauge trials remain the deliberately deferred print gate.
