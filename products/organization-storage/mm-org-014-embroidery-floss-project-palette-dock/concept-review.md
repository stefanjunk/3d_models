# Concept review

Selected concept: a low lattice chassis with three separated longitudinal lanes. Ten transverse receiver bars per lane hold cards upright; open gaps expose card faces and keep neighbouring bundles from sharing a continuous pocket. The front rail uses one, two and three recessed dots to identify lanes without fragile text geometry. A three-position coupon copies the receiver bar, pitch and slot profile.

Rejected: one dense 30-slot row, because it provides only about 7 mm pitch and weak separation; a proprietary printed floss-card ecosystem, because users already own incompatible card styles; and a portable lid, because loaded-floss retention is a separate validation problem.

Approved under the delegated autonomy policy for revision `0.1.0-draft.1`.
