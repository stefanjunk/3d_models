# Decomposition

1. **Dock source solid** — CadQuery-owned perimeter, three lane spines, thirty receiver bars, thirty stepped slots and three lane markers; one manufacturing STL.
2. **Fit coupon source solid** — three receiver bars and a common spine copied from the production dimensions; one coupon STL.
3. **Virtual card standards** — parameter-only cardstock, 2 mm acrylic and 3 mm acrylic blocks used for interface calculations, assembly STEP and preview; never included as manufacturing parts.
4. **3MF package** — unchanged dock and coupon meshes with deterministic, central-bed translations.
5. **Purchased/user parts** — labelled floss cards and floss remain external; no parametric geometry owns their brands or hole patterns.
6. **Evidence** — parameter, topology, interface, 3MF, slicer and deferred physical gates remain separate and hash-bound.
