# Deferred physical test plan

1. Print the coupon and full dock with the exact intended filament/profile.
2. Measure three loaded card specimens near 0.6, 2.0 and 3.0 mm at the inserted edge.
3. Insert all three into one coupon simultaneously; record seating depth, wobble, edge marks and one-handed removal.
4. Load 30 representative cards; verify A/B/C order, visibility and no cross-lane tangling during ten colour changes.
5. Run 500 insert/remove cycles on the worst-fitting class and inspect slot growth, card damage and fiber snagging.
6. Verify the dock does not tip or slide during normal retrieval on the target desk surface.
7. Keep needles outside the dock throughout the test.
