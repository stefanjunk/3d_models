# Draft print guide

- Print the measurement card before the full cassette and verify its ruler/notches against calipers.
- Cassette and card print flat on their broad bases; no generated support is intended.
- Reference profile: Anycubic Kobra 3 Max, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm Standard, Anycubic PLA.
- Exact digital preflight: 42 layers, 14,701 s slicer estimate, 92,786.41 mm3 extruded volume, one tool and no native object warnings.
- Use ordinary perimeter/infill generation from the reference profile; do not add supports inside the connector keep-out windows.
- Confirm the 220 × 160 mm cassette and 150 × 88 mm card remain flat on the bed after import; STL units are millimetres by project contract.
- Store only cool, undamaged, unpowered items. Do not store batteries or energized electronics.
- Confirm connector contacts remain over their keep-out windows and do not carry body-location load.
- Inspect contact edges and reject parts with sharp burrs, lifted corners or stringing in a connector window.
- Before extended use, test all intended items for non-marking retrieval, verify the target drawer closes, and cycle the tightest position 250 times.
- This digital candidate has not been physically printed or validated; no printer upload or print-start action was performed.
