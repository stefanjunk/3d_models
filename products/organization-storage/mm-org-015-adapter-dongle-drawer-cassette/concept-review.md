# Concept review

Approved under delegated autonomy for revision `0.1.0-draft.1`.

The selected concept is a thin full-area drawer base with twenty local three-sided cradle fences in a four-column by five-row map. Each body rests on the base, is laterally located by its own U-shaped fence, stops against a protected rear rail and leaves its connector over a clearance window. Recessed two-bar slot codes encode tens and units near the open connector end without fonts. A separate flat measurement card provides coarse physical intake checks before exact caliper entry.

This is preferred over deep milled-looking pockets because it preserves connector access, uses substantially less material, avoids support and makes each collection-specific envelope inspectable. The product remains a draft until actual items pass scratch, snag, retrieval and drawer-clearance tests.
