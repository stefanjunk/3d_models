# Decomposition

- Parametric cassette B-Rep: authoritative printable storage geometry.
- Twenty class records: authoritative body, connector and clearance interfaces.
- Recessed segment codes: geometry generated from line primitives without fonts.
- Measurement card B-Rep: separate production coupon/intake aid with coarse gauges and ruler ticks.
- Virtual item assembly: non-manufacturing bodies and connector blocks for visual/interface review.
- User-owned adapters/dongles: purchased/existing items, never included or energized.
- Physical evidence: deferred card/item fit, contact inspection, retrieval, drawer closure, slip and cycle results.

The cassette and measurement card export separately and share only declared measurement conventions; the virtual assembly is never a manufacturing source.
