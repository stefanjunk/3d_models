# Protected geometry

- Per-class cradle inside width, rear stop datum and open connector end
- Through-base connector keep-out width and reach
- Minimum cradle wall thickness and cassette base thickness
- Outer drawer-contact perimeter and bed-contact datum
- Recessed slot-code depth and font-independent tens/units bar construction
- Measurement-card notch widths, edge ruler pitch and all bed-contact faces

Mesh simplification must not move these surfaces or merge adjacent cradles.
