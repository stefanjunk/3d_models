# Requirements review

Approved under the delegated autonomy policy for revision `0.1.0-draft.1`.

- Store twenty small passive adapters, readers, receivers or dongles as a visible drawer inventory.
- Default cassette envelope is 220 x 160 mm and must remain one printable connected solid.
- Every position is driven by its own measured body length, width, height, connector width/reach, side clearance and end clearance.
- Connector contacts receive a local through-base keep-out rather than being used as the locating interface.
- Use unique tens/units bar position codes generated without installed fonts or brand marks.
- Provide a separate no-brand card with body-width notches, thickness notches and a 100 mm edge ruler.
- Produce JSON/CadQuery source, STEP, STL, DRAFT 3MF, preview and hash-bound validation evidence.
- Exclude energized storage, batteries, damaged electronics, ESD/data/water/impact protection, certified connector fit, child use and universal compatibility.
