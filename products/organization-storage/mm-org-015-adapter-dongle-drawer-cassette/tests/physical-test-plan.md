# Deferred physical test plan

1. Measure twenty inert, cool, undamaged items with calipers and compare to their class records.
2. Print the measurement card, verify its 100 mm ruler against calipers and inspect every notch for burrs.
3. Print representative smallest, median and largest cradle coupons or the full draft cassette.
4. Verify bodies locate without marking, connectors do not carry locating load and all items retrieve one-handed.
5. Load all twenty positions, confirm codes remain readable and close the target drawer without collision.
6. Run 250 insert/remove cycles on the tightest item; inspect cradle walls, device surfaces and connector relief.
7. Reject use with energized devices, batteries, hot/damaged electronics or any claimed ESD/data/water/impact protection.
