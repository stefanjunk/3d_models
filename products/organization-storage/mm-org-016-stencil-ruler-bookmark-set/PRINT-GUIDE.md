# Draft print guide

- Print the minimum-feature coupon first and qualify the intended printer/material/pen pair.
- Print every part flat on its broad face with no generated support.
- Reference profile: Anycubic Kobra 3 Max, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm Standard, Anycubic PLA.
- Exact digital preflight for all four objects: four layers, 3,262 s estimate, 16,939.98 mm3 extruded volume, one tool and no native object warnings.
- Do not scale the STL or 3MF; scaling invalidates the 4/5 mm pitch and every minimum-feature rule.
- Remove stringing without enlarging apertures and reject rough paper-contact edges.
- Test each pen and paper on sacrificial stock; wet ink can bleed under or smear when the plate moves.
- PLA is the digital reference only. PETG flexibility and surface behavior require a separate physical branch.
- This digital candidate has not been physically printed or validated; no G-code was retained and no printer action was performed.
