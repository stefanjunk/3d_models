# Concept review

Selected concept: `GridPair + Signal-12`.

- `Layout-5`: 5 mm registration holes, matching rule slots, proportional boxes and column/header guides.
- `Layout-4`: the same visual grammar rebuilt on a 4 mm cadence rather than scaled from the 5 mm plate.
- `Signal-12`: twelve original parameter-driven silhouettes named Orbit, Facet, Seed, Gate, Ribbon, Pulse, Bracket, Comet, Tile, Flare, Pin and Bridge.
- `Feature coupon`: slot and round-opening sweep from 0.8 to 1.6 mm for the user's printer/pen combination.

All plates remain flat and clip-free. Rounded outer corners, generous paper boundary and bounded cutout density answer the problem signals without promising untested paper or pen compatibility.
