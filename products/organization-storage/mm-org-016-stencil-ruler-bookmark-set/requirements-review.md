# Requirements normalization

- Source: SKU-026, score 91.0, positive demand gate dated 2026-08-28.
- Deliver exactly three coordinated bookmark stencil plates plus one minimum-feature coupon.
- Plate envelope: 142 × 40 × 0.8 mm; clip-free; rounded external corners; flat, support-free orientation.
- Provide independent 4 mm and 5 mm dot-grid registration systems over 120 mm.
- Provide twelve original, project-owned analytic `Signal-12` silhouettes; use no fonts, logos, third-party vector assets or copied themed art.
- Enforce minimum through-opening 0.8 mm only on the coupon; production openings at least 1.2 mm, internal ligament at least 1.2 mm, paper-edge boundary at least 2.4 mm and internal cutter radius at least 0.6 mm where applicable.
- Keep each production plate a single watertight positive-volume solid and package the four independent parts in one 3MF.
- Reference process: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer height, PLA draft profile; PETG is an optional physical test branch, not a validated recommendation.
- Exclude a page clip because current users report metal clips damaging lightweight paper.
- Exclude fountain-pen compatibility and dimensional-instrument claims.

User-delegated digital scope ends at a fully parametric, slicer-preflighted draft candidate. Physical edge, flex, ink, page and repeated-use testing is deferred to printing.
