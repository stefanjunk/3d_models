# Concept review

Selected concept: three equal-pitch open bowl modules joined left-to-right by integral trapezoid tabs and sockets.

The concept separates functional invariants from appearance. All variants retain the same base, ramp, front sweep mouth and connector datums. Soft Arc uses broad radii, Clean Facet uses an eight-sided shell, and Utility Rib adds three rear ribs to a tighter-radius shell. This gives three genuinely parameterized visual directions without multiplying functional uncertainty.

Rejected directions:

- Magnetic modules: introduce purchased parts, polarity and retention uncertainty.
- Snap hooks above the floor: add stress concentration and more process-dependent flex behavior.
- Fixed phone/watch/key pockets: conflict with the research signal that carried sets and desired layouts vary.
- Thin leather-look folded tray: copies a saturated competitor architecture and does not exploit parametric printing.
- Bare hard-plastic scratch-safety claim: unsupported and specifically excluded.

The SVG concept is a diagram, not a dimensional manufacturing source. Decision: APPROVED under delegated authority.
