# Optimization plan

The selected architecture removes the unused interior of three solid 56 × 76 × 22 mm envelope blocks while preserving a continuous 2.4 mm base, 2.0 mm walls, the rear ramp thickness and local connector flange.

The digital selection gate requires at least 55% CAD-volume reduction relative to those solid envelopes, one valid solid per part, and unchanged protected interfaces. More aggressive floor thinning, perforation and sparse ribs are deferred because the physical consequences for impact sound, furniture marking, stiffness and coin feel are currently unknown.

Analytic tessellation is preferred over decimation. If any mesh exceeds 80,000 triangles or 10 MiB, curve tessellation is relaxed before any interface surface is simplified.
