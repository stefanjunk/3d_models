# Requirements review

## Normalized brief

Build the ranked SKU-173 as a fully parametric, support-free FDM product that remains within the 180 × 160 × 45 mm portfolio envelope. It must expose a usable coin-sweep slope, a tolerance-testable rearrangement connector and three visual styles. The current phase ends at a digitally checked DRAFT print candidate; the user retains physical validation.

## Resolved requirements

- Three interchangeable modules share a 56 mm connection pitch and form a 172 × 76 × 22 mm chain.
- Each module has one bowl rather than fixed object-specific pockets, so the user can rearrange capacity by habit.
- The interior floor falls toward the front at a nominal 4.98° and ends 0.6 mm below the front lip.
- A 32 mm mouth with 4 mm lower corner radii creates a broad sweep path without a sharp central notch.
- The connector is printed in the 2.4 mm base plane and needs no support or separate hardware.
- The default 0.25 mm offset is bracketed by 0.15 and 0.35 mm coupon sockets.
- Soft Arc, Clean Facet and Utility Rib alter the exterior shell only; protected functional geometry stays common.
- No fonts, copied organizer layouts, external meshes, logos or electronics enter the source.

## Assumptions and open evidence

- PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers and 0.45 mm line width are reference assumptions rather than universal process guarantees.
- The integral connector is intended for planar alignment, not lifting the complete chain by one module.
- The slope is expected to help sweep coins, but no direct market complaint or physical trial currently proves it.
- The hard bowl can mark sensitive finishes. A removable soft liner could be a later accessory, but is not silently added to this product.
- Exact connector preference, hand comfort, coin bridging, furniture contact and cycle life remain for the user's print validation.

Decision: APPROVED under the user's delegated autonomous-to-print-candidate authority.
