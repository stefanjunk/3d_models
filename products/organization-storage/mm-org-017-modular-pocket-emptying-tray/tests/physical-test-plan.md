# Deferred physical test plan

Record machine, firmware, filament manufacturer/product/color/batch, drying/conditioning, nozzle material/wear, profile hash, ambient conditions and measurement method for every result.

## Coupon gate

1. Measure key head, neck, depth and thickness at three positions; record mean and range.
2. Test sockets in order 0.35, 0.25, 0.15 mm to avoid forcing a tight fit first.
3. Grade insertion and vertical removal force consistently with a force gauge if available, otherwise with a predefined hand rubric.
4. Reject any socket that requires impact, causes whitening/cracking, cannot be removed by hand or allows the key to rattle visibly.
5. Repeat 50 insert/remove cycles on the selected clearance and measure permanent play.

## Module connector gate

1. Connect all style pairs in both available chain positions; verify that style does not change the interface.
2. Confirm every base rests flat with no rocking greater than 0.5 mm at a corner under a 2 N alternating finger load.
3. Lift only the socket-side module 20 mm above a padded surface five times. Record whether the intended joint remains engaged; this is diagnostic, not a current lifting rating.
4. Complete 500 vertical connect/disconnect cycles on the worst pair or stop at the first crack, whitening, delamination or loss of useful alignment.

## Coin-sweep gate

1. Test clean 1 c, 2 c, 5 c, 10 c, 20 c, 50 c, €1 and €2 coins individually and in mixed groups of 5, 10 and 20.
2. Empty from the rear half, then sweep with one finger toward the front without lifting the module.
3. Record first-pass exit rate, bridging, coins trapped at side walls, finger contact discomfort and unintended module movement.
4. Repeat with at least three adult hand sizes if available. Do not claim ergonomic benefit from a single operator.
5. Reject if sharp edges contact skin, the 0.6 mm lip repeatedly traps a coin, or the tray slides enough to defeat one-handed use.

## Surface and durability gate

1. Place sacrificial samples matching each intended sensitive finish in a module with representative key/coin contact for 100 shake/set-down cycles.
2. Inspect under consistent grazing light. Any visible new scratch blocks a scratch-safe claim and requires a liner branch.
3. Load each module with 0.75 kg distributed blunt mass for 24 h at 30 °C, cool, and measure base flatness and connector pitch change.
4. Perform 10 drops from 300 mm onto a padded but firm surface with representative blunt contents; reject cracks, sharp fragments or connector loss.
5. Complete 1,000 pocket-empty/sweep cycles and re-check lip, ramp, base, wall and connector geometry.

Physical validation remains `DEFERRED` until signed measurements and photos are added to this product folder. Passing the digital checks alone does not authorize final watermarking or commercial release.
