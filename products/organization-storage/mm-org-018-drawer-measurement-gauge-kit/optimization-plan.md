# Optimization plan

The kit is flat and support-free. Material is removed only where it does not erase a protected measurement surface: the height cards use a spine/foot/ledge skeleton, the comb uses narrow probes on one backbone, and the calibration frame uses a large central window.

The selection gate compares six tile envelopes, two height-card envelopes, the full comb envelope and the frame envelope against the actual production CAD volume. At least 25% volume reduction is required. Radius tiles stay mostly solid because local stiffness and reliable corner seating matter more than small material savings.

Analytic tessellation is retained below 60,000 triangles and 8 MiB per mesh. Decimation is prohibited on protected arcs, ledge tops, finger sides and calibration references.
