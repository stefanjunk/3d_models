# Drawer measurement gauge and clearance coupon kit

Help a customer record drawer corner radius, width at repeatable heights, and a conservative side-clearance preference before ordering a full custom organizer.

## P2 digital-candidate contents

This English development description applies to `MM-ORG-018` revision `0.1.0-draft.1`.
The complete candidate 3MF contains 10 declared print object(s):
radius-r08, radius-r10, calibration-frame, radius-r02, radius-r04, radius-r12, height-card-left, height-card-right, clearance-comb, radius-r06. The included project preserves the selected build orientation and
the recorded support decision for the candidate.

## Development boundary

This is a digital P2 candidate, not a photograph or a physically qualified or
commercially released product. Fit, finish, strength, safety, rights clearance,
material behaviour and storefront claims remain governed by their separate
evidence and approval gates.
