# Requirements review

## Normalized brief

Build SKU-115 as a flat, support-free, fully parametric metric kit within 180 × 90 × 12 mm per part. The kit must help capture corner radius, tapered drawer width at repeatable heights and intended side clearance, while making printer error and user uncertainty explicit. Physical comparison against calipers and ten drawers remains with the user.

## Resolved requirements

- Six separate 30 mm square radius tiles cover R2–R12 in 2 mm steps; one-to-six holes provide durable project-owned identity without fonts.
- Two identical height cards can be flipped against opposite walls and support a rigid ruler at 15, 35 and 55 mm.
- One flat comb exposes 0.8–2.0 mm finger widths in 0.2 mm order; actual printed width must be measured before interpreting fit.
- One calibration frame exposes external 130 × 32 × 3 mm, internal 80 × 12 mm, 10 mm round and 10 mm square references.
- All unique shapes are single solids and all ten print objects share a broad z = 0 datum.
- The worksheet requires front/middle/rear and multiple-height readings, narrowest-value selection, insertion-path check and signed tool-error records.
- The product is a guided aid, not a calibrated instrument.

## Open evidence

- Exact print error by axis/material/color/batch is unknown.
- Height-card stability with different rulers and drawer finishes is unknown.
- Radius selection repeatability and the practical effect of corner daylight are unknown.
- Narrow finger durability and user tendency to force a fit are unknown.
- Mean and worst-case customer error across ten drawers are intentionally deferred.

Decision: APPROVED under the user's delegated autonomous-to-print-candidate authority.
