# Deferred physical validation plan

## Printed reference gate

- Condition the kit for 24 hours and measure every calibration-frame reference at three positions/directions.
- Record signed X/Y/Z error, repeatability and caliper resolution. Do not average opposite-sign axis errors into one correction.
- Measure each radius tangent span and each comb finger used; reject warped, sharp, stringy or damaged tools.
- Repeat after 30 days and 100 survey cycles to detect creep/wear.

## Ten-drawer comparison

- Include at least ten real drawers spanning straight and tapered sides, wood/metal/plastic finishes, square and rounded corners, and at least three insertion-path constraints.
- For each drawer, one trained operator and one first-time operator independently complete the worksheet without seeing the other's result.
- A reference operator then measures the same usable envelope with independent calipers, rigid rules and any necessary contour/radius tool.
- Calculate signed and absolute error for every reported width/depth/height, mean absolute user error, worst-case error and within-operator repeat range.
- Draft target: mean absolute user error ≤ 1.0 mm with no unreported worst-case error. This threshold is not a current rating.

## Radius-selection gate

- Use known R2/R4/R6/R8/R10/R12 reference corners plus at least five real drawer corners.
- Confirm that users select the smallest seating tile with both tangent legs in contact, not the loosest largest tile.
- Record interference/clearance bounds and whether daylight or surface texture prevents a unique selection.

## Height-card and comb gate

- Verify ledge tops at 15/35/55 mm against a height gauge or calipers.
- Test paired-card stability with three rigid rulers and five drawer finishes; record tip/slide events.
- Cycle every comb finger through a representative straight gap 100 times without forcing. Reject permanent set, cracking, delamination or more than 0.10 mm width change.

Physical validation remains `DEFERRED` until raw worksheets, calculations and photos are stored in this folder. Digital PASS does not authorize calibrated, certified, precision, exact-fit, remake-reduction or commercial claims.
