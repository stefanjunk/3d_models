# Concept review

Three concepts were compared.

1. A deep closed bin is robust but hides cartridge color and wastes material.
2. A brand-shaped pocket maximizes nominal fit but creates compatibility, provenance and measurement risk.
3. An open comb rack generated from simple measured envelopes preserves visibility, works across ecosystems and supports a cheap coupon-first workflow.

Concept 3 is selected. A thin base, full-depth dividers, low front label rail and inclined rear rest form each rack. The rear rest is an 8 degree design hypothesis that fans cartridges toward the user. Two planar joints share the base datum and the absolute Y datums 25/55 mm so different rack depths can connect side by side. Recesses, rather than embossed lettering, reserve label and two-state marker fields without font dependencies.

The compact-six and extended-five presets are examples, not fit claims. Their identifiers use one and two holes respectively so the parts remain distinguishable without branding.
