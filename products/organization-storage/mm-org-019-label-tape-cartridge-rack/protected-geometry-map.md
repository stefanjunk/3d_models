# Protected geometry map

- Slot clear width = measured thickness + 2 x slot clearance.
- Slot bottom depth = measured depth + depth clearance.
- Rear inner surface leans 8 degrees toward the front from the base datum.
- Base and connector height remain 3.0 mm and share Z=0; both rack depths use absolute Y datums at 25 and 55 mm.
- Divider and front wall thickness remain at least four 0.45 mm line widths.
- Label recess and status-dot recess leave at least 1.55 mm of the 2.0 mm front wall.
- Coupon series must remain ordered 0.30/0.50/0.70 mm around the 0.50 mm rack default.
- Every deliverable remains one valid solid and prints broad-face down without supports.

Any change to these surfaces requires regeneration of STEP, STL, 3MF and all hash-bound reports.
