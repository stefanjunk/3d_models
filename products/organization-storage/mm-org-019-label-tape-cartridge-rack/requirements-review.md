# Requirements review

SKU-197 is normalized as a low-consequence, passive storage product. The rack must not imply compatibility with a named printer or cartridge family before printed fit tests.

The authoritative customer input is the measured rectangular envelope, not nominal tape width. A wrapper, protruding tape leader or asymmetric cassette feature must be included in the measurement if it is to enter the slot. Slot clearance is applied on both thickness sides; depth clearance is applied once to the rear datum.

The default output is two independent presets plus one fit coupon. Both racks print base-down without support, expose the upper cartridge body, provide a low front lip for retrieval, and accept ordinary adhesive labels/color dots. No fastener, purchased part, magnet or external asset is required.

Digital PASS does not establish fit, tip resistance, connector life or label adhesion. Those checks remain explicit physical gates and are skipped only because the user requested to validate by printing later.
