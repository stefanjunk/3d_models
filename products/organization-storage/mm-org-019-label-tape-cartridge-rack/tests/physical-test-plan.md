# Deferred physical test plan

The user owns this phase. Digital PASS is not a substitute for the checks below.

## Fit and retrieval

- Condition printed parts and representative empty cartridges for 24 hours in the use environment.
- Test at least three cartridges per selected envelope preset, including the largest measured specimen and any wrapper-on case.
- Record insertion/removal force qualitatively for all three coupon bays and select the smallest no-force clearance.
- Run 100 insert/remove cycles across the least- and most-clear cartridges. Reject cracking, whitening, binding, snagging or uncontrolled ejection.

## Module joint

- Join one compact and one extended rack at the shared Y=25/55 mm datums.
- Reject visible base step, inability to assemble by hand, free separation during normal retrieval, layer cracking or permanent distortion after ten connect/disconnect cycles.

## Stability and workflow

- Load every slot with representative empty cartridges and test desk, drawer and shelf use separately.
- Retrieve each front, middle and rear slot five times without lifting the rack or dislodging neighbours.
- Apply the chosen adhesive label and two status markers; reject if fields are hidden by cartridges or labels lift during 50 state updates.
- Record any tip or slide event. No stability claim is allowed without the actual surface and load evidence.

Photograph setups, keep measurements here and report any correction before promoting a compatibility claim.
