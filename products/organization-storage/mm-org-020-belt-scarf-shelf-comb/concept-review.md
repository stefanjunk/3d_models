# Concept review

Closed grids are familiar and retain rolls well, but they consume material, trap lint and force all items into one fixed depth. Hanger products save shelf space but fail with some wide plate buckles and are a different customer workflow.

The selected concept is an open comb: one low front rail, one rear stabilizer rail and full-depth rounded divider fins. Rolled items contact the existing shelf, remain visible from the front/top and can be lifted without threading through a ring. Belt-four uses four 46 mm clear cells; scarf-three uses three 64 mm cells. Both share 105 mm depth and two absolute side-joint datums.

Labels are adhesive fields rather than embossed text. The fabric coupon exposes 0.6/1.0/1.4 mm leading radii; the production fins select 1.4 mm pending physical comparison.
