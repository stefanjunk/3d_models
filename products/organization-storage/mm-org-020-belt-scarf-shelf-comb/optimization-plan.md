# Optimization plan

Compare the candidate against solid blocks equal to both comb retaining envelopes. The candidate removes the floor between dividers and keeps only a front rail, rear rail, divider fins and base-plane joints. Target at least 80% CAD-volume reduction.

Do not thin textile-contact fins below 3.0 mm or reduce the selected radii to save material. Keep every mesh below 60,000 triangles and 8 MiB; analytic fillets may increase tessellation but should not be decimated if within budget.

Use the exact Anycubic reference profile for candidate print-time/material evidence. G-code is temporary and must not be retained, uploaded or printed by this workflow.
