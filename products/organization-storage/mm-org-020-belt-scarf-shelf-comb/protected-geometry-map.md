# Protected geometry map

- Clear cell width equals the configured roll/buckle envelope plus the user's chosen allowance.
- Divider thickness remains 3.0 mm, with a 1.4 mm plan radius on vertical leading/trailing edges and a 1.2 mm top-edge radius.
- Front rail remains 9 mm high; label recesses leave more than 11 mm of rail depth.
- Both modules share depth 105 mm, front datum Y=0, base datum Z=0 and connector centers Y=28/77 mm.
- Connector and rail base height remain 3.0 mm.
- Coupon radii remain ordered 0.6/1.0/1.4 mm and are identified without fonts.
- Every output remains one solid and support-free in the declared orientation.

Changing a protected surface requires regeneration of all STEP/STL/3MF and hash-bound evidence.
