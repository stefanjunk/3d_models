# Requirements review

SKU-125 is a passive, dry-soft-goods organizer with low consequence. The design must not claim load-bearing capacity or universal scarf safety.

The roll/buckle envelope is measured at its widest point after the user chooses the storage roll method. Clear slot width is the primary interface. Items rest on the existing shelf; a 9 mm front rail limits roll-out while leaving most of the item visible. Full-depth dividers provide lateral separation and connect the front and rear rails into one printable solid.

Divider leading edges and top edges are protected textile interfaces. The build must create actual analytic radii rather than describe them only in documentation. A three-radius fabric coupon and a connector test key bound physical iteration before a full mixed set is printed.

The user has authorized autonomous digital development only. Fabric snagging, 100 retrieval cycles, connector fit and shelf-specific sliding remain physical gates.
