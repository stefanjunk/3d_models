# Research validation — SKU-125

Checked 2026-08-28. Result: **PASS for differentiated digital prototyping**.

## Market and workflow evidence

- IKEA Germany sells a 25 x 53 x 5 cm four-compartment felt insert for EUR 20 and explicitly describes rolled tie storage, visibility and reachability. It validates the compartment job but is fixed-size and uses a full soft-lined tray: <https://www.ikea.com/de/de/p/komplement-einsatz-4-faecher-hellgrau-50404032/>
- A German bamboo organizer sells six compartments at EUR 39 for cabinet, drawer or tabletop use; its fixed 35 x 15 x 9.5 cm envelope confirms a paid upright-roll format: <https://genuinestrap.de/product/gurtel-organizer-aus-bambus>
- A German side-wall product sells individual rolled-belt cells from EUR 14 each and states a 5 cm opening expandable to 8 cm. It validates visibility and varied diameters but requires a 5 mm cabinet hole row: <https://ease-c.com/products/ease-c%C2%AE-schwarz>
- Etsy lists a 3D-printed belt organizer at USD 16.91 with color, personalization and orientation variants, confirming additive-manufactured competition: <https://www.etsy.com/listing/1903856203/3d-printed-belt-organizer-custom-belt>
- An Etsy wooden compartment display starts at USD 64. A recent review says the cells were too small for a buckle, directly supporting measured diameter/buckle envelopes rather than a fixed slot: <https://www.etsy.com/listing/1720145850/belt-organizer-wood-belt-storage>
- A professional organizer describes scarves as hard to store because bins hide them; rolling into divided bins or using a rack restores at-a-glance access: <https://www.modularclosets.com/blogs/christinas-corner/christinas-corner-how-to-solve-your-scarf-storage-problem>
- User reports identify wide plate buckles as incompatible with hanger-style products and recommend rolled upright storage so buckles remain visible: <https://www.reddit.com/r/organization/comments/kqxvtq>
- Scarf users report that loose piles become invisible and that roll dimensions depend on container height; delicate and fringed fabrics create a distinct snag/crease constraint: <https://www.reddit.com/r/ModestDress/comments/m000o7>

## Selected gap

The opportunity is an open shelf-contact comb whose compartment widths derive from measured maximum roll/buckle diameter. It uses no closed floor, supports mixed modules with a common interface, reserves adhesive label fields and includes an edge-radius coupon. Differentiation is measured width plus fabric-aware verification, not generic compartment storage.

Physical snag, sliding and retention tests remain explicitly deferred to the owner's print phase.
