# Deferred physical test plan

| Gate | Method | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| Textile edge | Pull smooth woven, knit and loose/fringed specimens over each coupon rib in two directions, 20 passes each | Selected radius produces no visible fiber pull, snag or fringe capture |
| Roll envelope | Measure maximum rolled diameter including buckle/knot bulk | Diameter is at least 2 mm below the selected clear slot width |
| Retrieval | Test at least three representative items per preset for 100 remove/replace cycles | No crack, permanent fin bend, recurring snag or unusable migration |
| Connector | Assemble/disassemble key and two modules 25 times | Hand-fit without fracture/whitening; modules remain aligned on shelf |
| Shelf behavior | Load intended dry goods on actual shelf finish | No unacceptable sliding or tip-out during normal retrieval |
| Label | Apply intended adhesive label for 72 h | Label remains legible and adhered; recess skin is undamaged |

Record printer, material/product/color/batch, nozzle, slicer profile, shelf finish, fabric class, measurements and photos in this product folder.
