# Print and physical-check guide

## Reference slicing

- Printer profile: Anycubic Kobra 3 Max, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Process: 0.20 mm Standard
- Material reference: Anycubic PLA
- Orientation: flat build-plate face down
- Supports: off
- Candidate 3MF: `exports/3mf/DRAFT-MM-ORG-021-eyeglass-case-shelf-corral-0.1.0-draft.1.3mf`

The exact-profile preflight passed at 410 layers with one tool and no native object warnings. Its G-code was temporary and is not included.

## Recommended sequence

1. Measure each closed case: vertical length, fore-aft depth and maximum thickness. Confirm the shelf has at least case length plus 10 mm clear height.
2. Print the width gauge. Use the smallest notch that accepts the widest case section without force, then retain at least 2 mm total extra lane width. Calipers remain the authoritative measurement.
3. Generate a custom lane list if the 36/42/50/58 mm examples do not match the inventory.
4. Print one corral and test empty cases or inert dummies first. Verify the case foot rests on the sloped floor, remains against the rear wall and cannot hop the front stop during adjacent retrieval.
5. Test at least three rigid, three semi-rigid/EVA and three soft-pouch cases where available, then complete 100 remove/replace cycles.
6. Check shelf sliding, overhead clearance and label adhesion on the actual shelf.

This is a dry-storage corral only, not optical, crush, impact or child-safety protection.
