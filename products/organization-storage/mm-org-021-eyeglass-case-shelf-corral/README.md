# MM-ORG-021 — Parametric eyeglass-case shelf corral

Draft digital print candidate for SKU-199 (opportunity score 89.0). Closed eyeglass cases stand on their short end in measured lanes; a rear-falling floor, dividers, low front stop and rear wall organize them without touching lenses.

## Parametric outputs

- `slim-five`: five 36 mm clear lanes, 198 × 90 × 82 mm.
- `mixed-four`: 36 / 42 / 50 / 58 mm clear lanes, 201 × 90 × 82 mm.
- `width-gauge`: the same four clear widths, marked by one to four holes, 211 × 28 × 3 mm.

Edit `config/model-parameters.json`, then run `python cad/build.py`. STEP is authoritative; STL and 3MF are deterministic manufacturing derivatives. No brand profile, font, copied organizer interface or external geometry is used.

All digital gates pass: parameter relations, one-solid B-Reps, independent topology audits, three-object 3MF and exact Anycubic Slicer Next preflight. Physical fit, tipping, retrieval cycling, shelf sliding and label adhesion remain deferred. This corral makes no optical or impact-protection claim.
