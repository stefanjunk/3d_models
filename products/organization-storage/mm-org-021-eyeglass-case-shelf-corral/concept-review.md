# Concept review

Selected: a base-down shelf corral with a 3-degree rear-falling floor, full rounded separators, low front stop, rear wall and one label field per lane.

Why: standing closed cases retain their own protective function while measured lanes restore visibility. The sloped floor biases cases toward the rear wall; dividers control lateral tip; the low front stop keeps the foot captured without blocking upward retrieval.

Presets: five equal 36 mm lanes for slim cases and four mixed 36/42/50/58 mm lanes. A flat gauge reproduces all four clear widths before the full print.

Rejected: bare-glasses trays (duplicates dominant market and creates lens-contact claims), universal fixed cells (contradicted by fit evidence), adjustable hardware (unnecessary parts and pinch points), copied Gridfinity or commercial organizer interfaces.

Result: PASS to decomposition.
