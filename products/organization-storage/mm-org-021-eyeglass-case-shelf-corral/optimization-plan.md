# Optimization plan

Baseline: solid rectangular blocks at each preset's full width, depth and divider height.

Candidate: thin sloped floor, 3 mm full dividers, low front stop and partial-height rear wall. Select only if analytic volume falls at least 75% below the two solid envelopes while all protected fit/stability geometry remains.

Do not window dividers before physical tip tests; their continuous faces are the primary soft-case support. A slicer A/B against the solid baseline is optional because the baseline is only a geometric material bound.
