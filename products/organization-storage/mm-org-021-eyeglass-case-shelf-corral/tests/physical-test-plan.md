# Deferred physical test plan

Use empty cases or inert dummies for initial tip/drop-risk work; do not expose valuable eyewear while selecting geometry.

| Gate | Method | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| Measured fit | Measure maximum closed-case thickness and test gauge notch | Selected clear lane is at least 2 mm wider and accepts the full profile without force |
| Vertical clearance | Measure case length and shelf opening | At least 10 mm free height remains for upward retrieval |
| Static tip | Place rigid, semi-rigid/EVA and soft-pouch cases on their short ends; disturb shelf lightly by hand | No case exits its lane or crosses the front stop |
| Adjacent retrieval | Remove each case while neighbors remain loaded | Neighbors do not tip out and selected case clears without jamming |
| Cycling | Minimum three cases per available class, 100 remove/replace cycles | No crack, permanent divider bend, recurring jam or unusable migration |
| Shelf behavior | Repeat on intended clean, dry shelf finish | Corral does not slide unacceptably during one-handed retrieval |
| Label | Apply intended adhesive label for 72 h | Label remains legible and adhered; recess skin is undamaged |

Record printer, material/product/color/batch, nozzle, slicer profile, shelf finish, case class/dimensions/mass, measurements and photos in this folder.
