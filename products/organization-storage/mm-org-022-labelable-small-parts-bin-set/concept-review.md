# Concept review

Selected concept: **raster-free ScoopLabel bin matrix**.

Three footprints share one 75 mm body depth, 36 mm height, 1.8 mm shell, front finger opening, internal pickup ramp and replaceable card rails. Widths 45, 67.5 and 90 mm create two exact 180 mm row recipes. A 186 × 156 mm open frame registers two rows while leaving each bin floor directly on the drawer surface.

Rejected concepts:

- generic fixed trays: strong commodity substitution and no durable additive advantage;
- Gridfinity-compatible bases: mature prior art, more plastic and unnecessary interface dependency for this customer job;
- lidded transport organizer: introduces latch, migration and drop requirements outside the opportunity brief;
- printed text labels: forces body reprints and introduces font/customer-content dependencies.

Status: **AUTO_APPROVED for parametric implementation**.
