# Decision log

## 2026-08-28 — reject the commodity tray position

Current IKEA and MUJI trays are extremely inexpensive. Selected exact row packing, pickup geometry and replaceable paper labels as the additive-manufacturing value; price and demand remain hypotheses.

## 2026-08-28 — avoid proprietary grid compatibility

Gridfinity already has mature parametric bin and label generators. The candidate uses an original open perimeter frame with no magnet sockets, stacking profile, 42 mm grid or imported geometry.

## 2026-08-28 — use three widths on one common depth

45, 67.5 and 90 mm widths form three exact 180 mm recipes while retaining a shared 75 mm depth and 36 mm height. The frame adds 0.6 mm per-side and 1.2 mm inter-row clearance.

## 2026-08-28 — separate the label clearance decision

The nominal vertical paper slot is 0.7 mm for card up to 0.4 mm, bracketed by a 0.5/0.7/0.9 mm coupon. Physical coupon selection remains mandatory before a full set.

## 2026-08-28 — stop at digital print candidate

Exact-profile slicing may be agent-approved. Actual drawer fit, paper retention, retrieval, cycles, appearance and commercial release remain user-owned. No G-code is retained or sent to a printer.
