# Research validation — SKU-023

Date: 2026-08-28

Decision: **GO as a differentiated, exact-layout digital candidate; do not compete as a generic commodity tray.**

## Current evidence

- [IKEA NOJIG, Germany](https://www.ikea.com/de/de/p/nojig-sortierbox-kunststoff-beige-70457487/) currently offers 10 × 20 × 5 cm polypropylene drawer trays for €0.50 and reports 4.7/5 from 3,622 reviews. This is strong demand evidence but makes undifferentiated printed trays commercially unattractive.
- [MUJI PP drawer insert 3](https://germany.muji.eu/products/desk-organiser-storage-box-rectangle-narrow-4224) is 6.7 × 20 × 4 cm, includes a divider and is listed at €2.50. Fixed low-cost formats are already abundant.
- [Akro-Mils AkroBins](https://akro-mils.com/products/storage-bins/akrobins-plastic-storage-bins/) span many fixed footprints and combine stacking, hanging, dividers and label accessories. Their smallest listed 30210 is 5.375 × 4.125 × 3 in; this validates identification and modularity, not a need for another copied industrial bin.
- [Akro-Mils card-stock holders](https://akro-mils.com/product/card-stock-holders-for-bins/) and [extended label holders](https://akro-mils.com/product/label-holders-for-bins/) show that replaceable labels are a separate functional interface. The latter accepts labels up to 2 × 4 in.
- [RS PRO 12-compartment box](https://de.rs-online.com/web/p/sortimentkasten/0515568) is listed at €9.95 including VAT and explicitly targets electronic components and fasteners. [RS PRO's 7-compartment box](https://de.rs-online.com/web/p/sortimentkasten/8386535) is 175 × 122 × 30 mm and uses anti-migration features plus a lid. The present project is dry drawer/desktop storage only and makes no transport or spill claim.
- The [Fusion Gridfinity Generator documentation](https://github.com/Le0Michine/FusionGridfinityGenerator/wiki/Bin-generator-options) already exposes parametric label tabs and compartments. [gfthings](https://github.com/PaulBone/gfthings) similarly offers customizable Gridfinity bins with label shelves and front scoops under CC BY-NC-SA. This project therefore uses original raster-free geometry and no imported Gridfinity interface, source or mesh.

## Product opportunity

The defensible job is an exact drawer row assembled from measured clear-width bins, with low front pickup geometry and paper labels that can change without reprinting the body. A 180 × 150 mm reference matrix supports both portfolio pilot workflows:

- electronics row: 4 × 45 mm; second row: 90 + 45 + 45 mm;
- sewing rows: 67.5 + 67.5 + 45 mm, repeated twice.

The three bin widths, one open registration frame and one 0.5/0.7/0.9 mm label-slot gauge form the digital candidate. Demand, price and conversion remain hypotheses until a customer pilot is run.

## Boundaries

Adult indoor dry storage only. No child-directed use, food contact, ESD control, battery storage, energized-parts use, transport, spill retention, stacking load or named-system compatibility claim. Physical fit, label retention and small-part retrieval are deferred to printing.
