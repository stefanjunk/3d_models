# Physical test plan — deferred

1. Print the slot gauge in the production orientation and test at least three samples of the intended paper/card stock in all three stations. Select the smallest non-buckling, hand-removable gap.
2. Print one narrow bin and the matrix frame. Verify smooth insertion/removal, no frame overlap with the front label rails, and no rocking on the actual drawer surface.
3. Load one bin with at least three representative unpowered electronics lots and one with at least three adult sewing-notion lots. Confirm the front ramp permits fingertip or tweezer retrieval without parts lodging at the front-floor corner.
4. Check every exposed edge and label rail for cuts, snags, delamination or sharp burrs.
5. Complete 100 bin remove/replace cycles and 100 label insert/remove cycles. Record cracks, permanent set, looseness and paper damage.
6. Load each printed bin to no more than 0.25 kg for 24 h; inspect floor, rails and frame. This is a qualification check, not a public load claim.
7. Verify the assembled two-row recipe fits the measured drawer with operating clearance and does not jam when the drawer closes.

Pass/fail authority remains with the user operating the printer and measurement equipment.
