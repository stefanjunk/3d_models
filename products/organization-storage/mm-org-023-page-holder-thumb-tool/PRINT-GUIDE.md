# Print guide — draft candidate

1. Print `exports/coupons/DRAFT-MM-ORG-023-sizing-guide-0.1.0-draft.1.stl` first.
2. Reference process: Anycubic Kobra 3 Max, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm Standard profile, Anycubic PLA, broad face down, page pads upward, no supports.
3. Do not rescale. Select the smallest opening that accepts the thumb to the intended working depth without compression or catching; then print only that full-size holder first.
4. In use, flip the part so the two raised pads contact the pages. Do not drag the center body across paper.
5. Deburr and inspect every opening, outside edge and page pad. Reject sharpness, delamination, rough seams or distorted openings.
6. Stop immediately if the sample produces pressure, numbness, pain, skin marking or worsening discomfort.
7. Follow `tests/physical-test-plan.md` and record exact process scope in `tests/measurement-worksheet.md`.

No G-code is included or sent to a printer automatically.
