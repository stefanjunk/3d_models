# Concept review

Selected concept: **SoftBridge S/M/L thumb page-holder family**.

Each tool combines a rounded central oval body, two shallow capsule wings, an obround opening, and two local page pads on the use-side. The page pads print upward; the broad opposite face is the support-free bed datum. The sizing card repeats the exact three openings and identifies stations geometrically.

Rejected concepts:

- one round universal hole: current products and reviews expose fit variability;
- print-in-place adjustable ring: moving clearance, pinch and fatigue risk are unjustified at this stage;
- tall V-shaped wings: higher leverage but support/weak-layer and page-pressure uncertainty;
- copied decorative silhouettes or licensed marketplace meshes: unnecessary rights and provenance risk.

Status: **AUTO_APPROVED for parametric implementation**.
