# Decision log

## 2026-08-28 — treat fit and comfort as separate questions

Market examples cluster around roughly 20–25 mm openings and frequently offer multiple sizes. A current printed-product review reports worse thumb discomfort despite nominal fit. Selected a sizing guide followed by a separate full-holder comfort sample.

## 2026-08-28 — use obround openings

The thumb cross-section is represented by measured major/minor dimensions rather than a ring-size label or universal circular hole. Three starting samples preserve 10 mm wall around each opening.

## 2026-08-28 — localize page contact

Two rounded pads project 0.8 mm from the use-side. The opposite broad face remains the print datum, keeping the part support-free. Paper behavior remains physically unqualified.

## 2026-08-28 — reduce tessellation without changing CAD

The initial 0.06 mm / 0.12 rad tessellation produced 68–73k triangles per holder and failed the 60k budget. The 0.10 mm / 0.18 rad setting reduced holders to approximately 33–36k triangles while preserving valid watertight B-Reps and the exact CAD interfaces.

## 2026-08-28 — stop at digital print candidate

Exact-profile slicing may be agent-approved. Physical sizing, comfort, page interaction, cycles, appearance and commercial release remain user-owned. No G-code is retained or sent to a printer.
