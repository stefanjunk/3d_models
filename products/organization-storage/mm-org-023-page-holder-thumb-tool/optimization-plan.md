# Optimization plan

Baseline: four solid rectangular envelope blocks.

Candidate: capsule wings, an elliptical center body, obround openings and two local use-side page pads. Preserve the ring wall, rounded comfort edges, opening sizes and page-pad datums. Select only if CAD volume falls at least 35% below the envelope baseline and exact-profile slicing is warning-free.

Direct CadQuery tessellation remains preferred while every mesh stays below 60,000 triangles and 8 MiB. Decimation may not alter the thumb opening or page-contact surfaces.
