# Research validation — SKU-053

Date: 2026-08-28

Decision: **GO as a measured S/M/L comfort-sample system; reject universal-fit and medical claims.**

## Current evidence

- A current [Etsy Book Buddy listing](https://www.etsy.com/in-en/listing/1030392033/book-page-holder-thumb-ring-book) offers approximately 20.3 and 25.4 mm round openings, lists at US$10.99, and exposes comfort and sizing/fit as review themes. This supports multiple sizes rather than one opening.
- [Red Squirrel Wooden Designs](https://www.redsquirrelwoodendesigns.co.uk/product-page/oak-thumb-book-page-holder) offers 22 and 25 mm holes in a roughly 100 × 15 mm wooden holder. [The Orchard Embroidery Co.](https://www.notonthehighstreet.com/theorchardembroideryco/product/personalised-wooded-page-holder-one-handed-opener) lists an 85 × 35 mm holder with a 22 mm hole at £10.95.
- [Wood Tactics Mark&Hold](https://www.woodtactics.com/products/large-mark-hold) explicitly separates regular and large oval openings, roughly 19+ mm and 22+ mm wide. This validates an oval/obround interface and a size boundary, not any exact copied outline.
- A current printed [Etsy reading-ring listing](https://www.etsy.com/in-en/listing/398935951/thumb-thing-reading-ring-page-holder) offers three sizes and publishes a small oval of 18.5 × 13.4 mm. A [We Love 3D listing](https://welove3d.co.uk/products/book-page-holder-thumb-page-holder-61907) lists a 75 mm holder with a 20 mm hole and includes a customer statement that the product increased thumb discomfort. Comfort therefore cannot be inferred from fit alone.
- A current [MakerWorld page holder](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1660756-page-holder-read-books-one-handed-with-ease) demonstrates that quick, support-free PLA/PETG printable competition exists under a restrictive Standard Digital File License. No source, mesh, dimensions or form language from that model is used here.
- Reader/maker discussion notes that book size, wing angle and the ability to turn pages affect usefulness; this is treated as qualitative hypothesis only and becomes a three-book-class physical gate.

## Selected differentiation

The project generates original support-free S/M/L tools from measured obround openings, provides a separate three-opening sizing card, rounds all ring and outer edges, and limits page contact to two local pads on the use-side. The body prints flat with the pads upward, then flips for use.

Nominal openings are 20 × 16.5, 23 × 19 and 26 × 21.5 mm; spans are 82, 92 and 102 mm. These are starting samples, not population coverage or universal-fit claims.

## Boundaries

Adult reading accessory only. Small detachable object. Stop use immediately if pressure, numbness, pain or skin marking occurs. No medical, accessibility, therapeutic, child-use, book-binding protection, paper-protection, archival or universal book/hand compatibility claim. Demand and price remain hypotheses until a customer pilot.
