# Print guide — draft candidate

1. Print the gap gauge first and measure every notch with calibrated calipers.
2. Test only the smallest gap that enters the measured flat wall/rim zone without forcing, whitening or visible deformation.
3. Print one matching clip and the key-slot coupon. Confirm insertion, inward lock slide and deliberate removal before printing the face.
4. Reference process: Anycubic Kobra 3 Max, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm Standard, Anycubic PETG. Face back down; clips and coupons broad side down; no supports.
5. Print two identical selected clips for one face. Insert each head through its outer opening and slide both clips inward to the protected locked centers.
6. Use only to slide a dry indoor bin horizontally. Never lift or carry a bin with this accessory.
7. Follow `tests/physical-test-plan.md`; reject cracking, whitening, sharp edges, loose keys, host marking or progressive slip.

No G-code is included, retained or sent to a printer automatically.
