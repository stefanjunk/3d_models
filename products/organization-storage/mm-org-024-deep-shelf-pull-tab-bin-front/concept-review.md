# Concept review — BridgeKey pull face

Selected concept: a reusable face and three interchangeable planar U-clips.

- The face prints back-down. Its solid 45-degree-class lower wedge gives a broad pull edge without a closed hand opening or support material.
- A top-open paper-label recess uses side and bottom rails; it makes no dust, moisture or archival-protection claim.
- Two mirrored horizontal key slots accept identical clip keys through a larger entry, then retain the wider head after an inward slide.
- Each U-clip prints on its broad side so the spring section is continuous in the layer plane.
- A three-notch gap gauge and a one-slot face coupon are intentionally cheaper failure points than the full face.

Rejected: one nominal “universal” clip, adhesive attachment, fasteners, a carry handle and a monolithic clip/face whose spring arms would require a less reliable orientation.
