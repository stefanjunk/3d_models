# Requirements review

Normalized customer job: add a removable, clearly labeled pull face to a deep dry-storage bin so the bin can be slid forward without replacing or drilling the bin.

Required digital outcomes:

1. Generate one 120 × 48 mm face with a support-conscious low pull lip and open 76.2 × 20 mm paper-label interface.
2. Generate interchangeable 2.2, 2.9 and 3.6 mm U-clip gaps plus a first-print gap gauge.
3. Use the same bounded key/slot interface for all clips and include a small key-slot coupon.
4. Keep every part support-free in its documented orientation and inside 180 × 50 × 55 mm.
5. Keep 0.75 kg and 500 cycles as test targets only; prohibit lifting, carrying and universal-fit language.

Physical fit, fatigue, creep, bin marking, label retention and slide behavior are deferred.
