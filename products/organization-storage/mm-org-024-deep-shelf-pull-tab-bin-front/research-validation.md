# Research validation — SKU-118

Decision: **GO as a measured, light-slide accessory; no universal-fit or lifting claim.**

Current comparison evidence checked on 2026-08-28:

- Aigner's current Bin•Clip line uses removable compression clips with 1 × 3 inch and 2 × 4 inch label formats. This supports replaceable labels as a known job, but not a pulling or carrying claim: https://www.aignerlabelholder.com/products/bin-clip/
- Renzel's current hook-over label holder is 3.5 inches wide, accepts a 2.5-inch-high insert and is 0.5 inch deep; it also offers custom sizes. This supports dimensional fit as a differentiator: https://www.renzelusa.com/hook-over-basket-label-holder-78-0004-4.html
- Regency currently offers 3 × 1 inch and 4 × 2 inch clip-on PVC label holders for plastic bins. The product copy emphasizes labels and compression attachment, not pulling a loaded bin: https://www.webstaurantstore.com/regency-4-x-2-clear-clip-on-label-holder-for-bins-case/460LBCP24CS.html
- NIOSH's Revised Lifting Equation manual describes a full lifting handle as roughly 19–38 mm in diameter, at least 115 mm long and with about 50 mm clearance. The proposed low-profile tab does not meet that envelope, so it is deliberately excluded from lifting use: https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/media/pdfs/2026/06/94-110_2021-revision.pdf
- A recent marketplace STL combines a clip and label holder, but reports 103.3 × 150 × 70.6 mm and about 155 g of PLA. It is treated only as evidence that the job exists; no geometry or license-restricted asset is reused: https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/home-decor/clip-on-bin-label-holder-fits-storage-bins-changeable-3d-printable-stl-file

Local interface evidence:

- MM-ORG-002 has a 1.92 mm body wall and a 2.67 mm front-rim section. The new 2.2 and 2.9 mm clip gaps provide bounded digital candidates for those two local sections.
- Three gap presets and a separate gauge make measurement failure occur before the full face is printed.

Research boundary: market listings establish demand language and common label envelopes, not clip fatigue, fit, surface safety, comfort or a load rating. Those remain physical gates.
