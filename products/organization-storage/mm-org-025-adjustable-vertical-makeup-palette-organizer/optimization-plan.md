# Optimization plan

The selected form replaces a solid 190 × 106 × 10 mm tray with three full-width support rails and two low side ties. Each divider is an open frame rather than a solid plate. The deterministic comparison uses the actual one-base/seven-divider/two-coupon build set against solid bounding-envelope blocks; the candidate must remove at least 35% volume.

Protected interfaces are excluded from decimation. Further optimization should first reduce non-contact frame width or side-tie height, then run loaded tip and flex tests. Do not reduce the dual tongue section before the insertion-cycle test.

The exact Anycubic PETG slice supplies the production estimate. No baseline G-code comparison is claimed because the bounding blocks are only a geometric material proxy, not a printable alternative.
