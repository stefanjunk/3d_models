## Decision

Proceed with an original slotted base and movable open-frame dividers. The default six clear compartments are 20.6, 20.6, 20.6, 20.6, 32.1, and 43.6 mm. Declared usable palette thicknesses reserve 1.0 mm retrieval clearance. A photo worksheet captures closed face dimensions using a scale reference; maximum closed thickness must still be measured with calipers.

No brand compatibility, sanitation, cosmetic-contact, heat resistance, or universal-fit claim is made. Commercial demand remains unvalidated.
