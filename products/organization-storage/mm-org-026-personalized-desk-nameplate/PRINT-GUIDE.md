Reference preflight: Anycubic Kobra 3 Max, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm Standard, Anycubic PLA, no supports.

- Print the slot gauge and fit key first.
- Print insert back face down with engraving upward.
- Print both identical stands and the gauge on their flat bases.
- Do not scale parts independently; it changes glyph pixels and insert fit.
- Keep elephant-foot compensation identical between key, gauge, insert, and stands.
