# Concept review — SignRail

Selected: one flat engraved insert, two identical inclined end stands, an inclined slot gauge, and an exact-thickness fit key.

Reasons:

- A name/title change reprints only the thin insert.
- Two small reusable stands replace a material-heavy full-width wedge and can move with insert width.
- Engraving keeps the insert one material and support-free while retaining a 2.4 mm backing.
- The live SVG proof comes from the exact glyph layout used by CAD.
- The 3.2/3.4/3.6 mm coupon isolates machine/material fit before the visible insert or stands.

Rejected: installed system fonts (portability and provenance dependency), copied logo/icon art, one-piece text/wedge (large personalized reprint), and a thin commercial 1/16 in insert (outside the FDM stiffness/interface candidate).
