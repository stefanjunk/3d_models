# Optimization plan

The selected design replaces a conventional solid full-width 200 × 62 × 20 mm holder with two reusable 26 mm end stands. The thin insert carries all personalization. The deterministic proxy compares the complete candidate set with a full-width solid holder plus equivalent insert/coupon envelopes and requires at least 35% volume reduction.

Do not thin the insert below the 2.4 mm post-engraving backing or reduce stand floor depth before physical fit and tip testing. Any later stand pocketing must preserve the 70-degree slot, 4.5 mm bottom datum, desk contact footprint, and 40 mm push-height tip test.
