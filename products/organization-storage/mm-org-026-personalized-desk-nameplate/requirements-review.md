# Requirements review

Status: normalized and ready for autonomous digital implementation; physical and commercial gates remain deferred.

| ID | Requirement | Digital acceptance | Deferred evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Replaceable personalization | one 200 × 50 × 3 mm insert; two identical end stands | retention and rattle |
| R2 | Live proof | SVG and CAD share normalized text, font ID, pitch, and pixel source | customer approval and real display size |
| R3 | Approved font input | one allowlisted repository-owned geometric glyph set; no font binary | commercial provenance sign-off |
| R4 | Measured fit | 3.2/3.4/3.6 mm inclined gauge plus 3.0 mm key | station selection and 250 cycles |
| R5 | Envelope/supports | 200 × 62 × 51.5 mm nominal installed; flat/base-down parts | print and desk tip test |
| R6 | Claims/privacy | decorative identification only; minimize retained names | order/proof workflow and terms review |

Allowed input normalizes to uppercase A–Z, digits, space, period, hyphen, and ampersand. Diacritics normalize; Ä/Ö/Ü/ß become AE/OE/UE/SS. Names are not retained in the reusable font library or learning store.
