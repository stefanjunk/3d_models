## Decision

Proceed with an original two-end-stand architecture and a 200 × 50 × 3 mm engraved insert. The 3.0 mm FDM insert is intentionally thicker than conventional 1/16 in engraved stock and is not claimed compatible with commercial holders. The internal `MM-GRID-5X7-v1` geometric alphabet avoids installed font binaries and keeps minimum strokes measurable. A generated SVG preview is hash-bound to the same normalized text and glyph source used by CAD.

No logo, official title verification, employer affiliation, accessibility/wayfinding, outdoor, or universal-holder claim is included. Customer name/title content and personalized-return terms require human/commercial review.
