# Deferred physical test plan

1. Print the 3.0 mm key and 3.2/3.4/3.6 mm inclined gauge first. Select the narrowest station that accepts ten hand cycles without tool force, whitening, fracture, or uncontrolled rattle.
2. If 3.4 mm is not selected, update `stand.slot_width_mm`, regenerate every artifact, and invalidate old proof/approval hashes.
3. Print the proofed insert and two stands. Place stand centers at ±78 mm; verify full insertion, equal angle, and no rocking on the intended desk.
4. At normal seated viewing distance, have three adult reviewers read name and title in daylight and typical office light. Record distance, errors, and pixel-edge defects.
5. Apply gentle front/rear pushes at 40 mm above the desk and lateral pushes at the insert ends. Reject tip initiation, stand lift, or insert escape during expected desk contact.
6. Inspect all touch edges and desk contact faces. Reject sharp burrs, scratching, visible transfer, or instability.
7. Run 250 insert/remove cycles distributed between both stands. Reject slot cracking, fit loss, permanent stand spread, or insert-edge damage.

Record PLA manufacturer/product/color/batch, conditioning, nozzle, machine, orientation, slicer/profile, elephant-foot compensation, room conditions, viewing distance, lighting, and measurement method.
