# Draft print guide

- Material/profile: Anycubic PETG reference profile, Kobra 3 Max 0.4 mm, 0.20 mm Standard.
- Orientation: every part broad-face-down as arranged in the selected 3MF; no generated supports.
- Print the 1.8/1.9/2.0 mm gauge and exact 1.6 mm key first. Do not assume the nominal 1.9 mm cap slot before the real coupon result.
- Long 1.6 mm carriers are warp-sensitive. Clean the plate and use the profile's normal adhesion strategy; any added brim is a slicer decision and must be removed without leaving sleeve-contact burrs.
- Inspect every edge before placing the carrier between protective outer sleeves. Do not use against bare records and do not overpack the shelf.
- The cap slides over the carrier's upper front edge; left/center/right offsets stagger nearby labels.

No G-code is included. Review the final slicer preview before printing.
