# Concept review — ShelfCue

Selected concept: a smooth 230 × 35 × 1.6 mm carrier strip sits between protected outer sleeves. A thin engraved cap slides over the carrier's front upper edge through a U-slot and turns the label plane 90 degrees toward the viewer. Slot position shifts the cap left, center or right to stagger adjacent categories.

Rejected: a 235 × 105 mini-panel hidden behind 12-inch jackets; a segmented full-size panel with unnecessary print/assembly burden; a one-piece bent tab requiring support or post-forming; and an aggressively windowed selected carrier whose internal edges add snag/racking uncertainty.

The concept proceeds as a digital draft under the delegated autonomy policy. Physical and release decisions remain human-owned.
