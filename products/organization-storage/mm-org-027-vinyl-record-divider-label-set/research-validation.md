# Research validation — SKU-024

Checked 2026-08-28. The portfolio row is a planning estimate, not release approval.

## Demand and workflow signals

- Etsy's current personalized vinyl-divider market page showed 44+ items, custom alphabetical/genre/category offers and listings with substantial review counts. This supports a real but competitive personalization niche; it does not establish German conversion or the workbook's price model. Source: [Etsy market page](https://www.etsy.com/market/vinyl_record_dividers_personalized).
- RIAA's 2025 year-end report records roughly USD 1 billion US vinyl revenue, 9.3% growth and a nineteenth year of vinyl growth. This is directional US physical-media evidence, not a German demand estimate. Source: [RIAA 2025 report](https://www.riaa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/RIAA-Year-End-Revenue-2025.pdf).
- Discogs' current taxonomy separates genre from style, supporting free category labels rather than a fixed A-Z-only set. Source: [Discogs genre/style guideline](https://support.discogs.com/hc/en-us/articles/360005055213-Database-Guidelines-9-Genres-Styles).
- A current record-divider retailer describes use in both front-forward and sideways storage and a 0.5 × 4.5 inch writable tab. This supports a visible, compact label zone and the need to state the actual storage orientation. Source: [Turntable Lab LP dividers](https://www.turntablelab.com/products/turntable-lab-lp-dividers-for-vinyl-records-10-pack).

## Dimensional conflict and resolution

- A current pressing supplier specifies a 12-inch jacket panel around 12.3125 × 12.383 inches (about 312.7 × 314.5 mm). Source: [National Audio Company jacket template](https://www.nationalaudiocompany.com/vinyl-record-pressing/vinyl-record-templates-and-forms/).
- Commercial full dividers are correspondingly around 12.5 × 13 inches with a 4.25-inch tab, while another supplier uses approximately 12-inch size plus a 23 mm tab. Sources: [BCW 12-inch divider](https://www.bcwsupplies.com/12in-record-divider-black), [Analogue Studio divider](https://www.analoguestudiodesigns.co.uk/products/vd-01-professional-tabbed-pvc-12-record-dividers).
- Therefore the retained 235 × 105 × 5 mm single-part envelope cannot honestly produce a full 12-inch divider. `ShelfCue` is intentionally a label/index system: a 230 × 35 × 1.6 mm carrier enters between protected outer sleeves and a perpendicular 80 × 38 × 2.4 mm cap makes the category readable from the shelf front.

## Product gate

Proceed as a dry-indoor shelf index only. Reject claims of record support, anti-lean behavior, archival material safety, preservation, bare-record contact or universal shelf fit. The CSV batch and nesting workflow are implemented before CAD capacity is considered complete. Customer shelf depth, protective-sleeve use, label list and tab offsets remain order inputs.
