# Draft print guide

- Material/profile: Anycubic PLA reference profile, Kobra 3 Max 0.4 mm, 0.20 mm Standard.
- Print the rack kit and divider set from their separate 3MF files. All parts are already in broad/base-face-down support-free orientation.
- Print the 10.9/11.2/11.5 mm gauge and exact 10.8 mm key before the full divider set. Do not assume the nominal 0.4 mm total clearance without the real coupon result.
- Deburr and inspect every envelope-contact edge. Load only filled protective paper/polypropylene envelopes or cases; never load loose exposed dies or blades.
- Keep loaded thickness at or below the qualified coupon result and distribute heavy magnet-card cases rather than concentrating them in the outer lanes.

No G-code is included. Review both final slicer previews before printing.
