# Concept review

Selected concept: `IndexDock 15`.

One support-free rack combines three long base rails, sixteen aligned boundary fins per rail and two sloped end panels. Fifteen 11.2 mm lanes accept filled protective envelope/case stacks up to the qualified thickness. Four flat-printed high index frames use one-sided 10.8 mm foot pads in all three rails, an 8 mm perimeter, a 12 mm center rib and staggered engraved tabs.

Rejected concepts: a full bin consumes the dimensional/material advantage; loose-die slots violate the safety boundary; clips on customer envelopes add puncture/marking risk; full-solid 145 × 198 mm dividers materially increase print burden; a single combined build plate cannot fit the complete selected set without unsafe overlap.
