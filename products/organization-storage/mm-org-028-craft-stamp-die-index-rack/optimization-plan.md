# Optimization plan

Baseline B0 is the selected 8 mm frame / 12 mm center-rib divider set and skeletal rack at 0.20 mm. Process candidate A slices unchanged CAD at 0.28 mm and must retain at least three nominal engraving layers. Geometry candidate B narrows divider frames to 6 mm and center ribs to 8 mm; it is generated only as a digital variant and rejected until loaded racking and envelope-snag evidence exists.

The three-rail rack and open-frame dividers are compared geometrically with a full 215 × 153 × 3 mm tray plus four solid 145 × 198 × 2.4 mm dividers. Exact slicer metrics are required for both selected build plates and both process profiles. Infill percentage is not a substitute for explicit rails, frames and ribs.
