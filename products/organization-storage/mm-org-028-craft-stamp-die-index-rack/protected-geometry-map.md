# Protected geometry map

- Lane gap 11.2 mm, fin thickness 2.4 mm and 10.8 mm divider pads form one interface; the 10.9/11.2/11.5 mm gauge and exact key are mandatory first articles.
- Three rail origins and three divider pad origins are registered datums. Do not optimize their count or alignment independently.
- Divider perimeter width ≥8 mm and center rib ≥12 mm remain protected until loaded racking and envelope-snag tests pass.
- Divider tabs are 72 mm wide with centers at ±36/0 mm; backing is ≥1.8 mm after 0.6 mm engraving and ten-character glyph pixels remain ≥0.8 mm.
- Filled protective envelopes/cases are the only approved contact class. All edges require real burr/snare inspection.
- End panels, outer ties and all three long rails form the rack stability loop; remove no member without a new tip/racking gate.
