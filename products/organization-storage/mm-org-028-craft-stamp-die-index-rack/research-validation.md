# Research validation

## Portfolio hypothesis

SKU-158 proposes a 220 × 180 × 200 mm PLA rack that stores thin stamp, die or template envelopes vertically with searchable labels. The current rescored opportunity is 87.8 at new-build rank 20. The legacy row explicitly prefers filled envelopes over loose dies and excludes exposed cutting-tool handling.

## Current evidence

- Michaels' 2026 report is directional category evidence, not conversion proof: it cites company sales/search data and recent surveys, including a 136% rise in analog-hobby searches and strong growth in hands-on skill categories. Source: [The Michaels Companies / PR Newswire, 2026-03-10](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/michaels-unveils-2026-creativity-trend-report-revealing-a-shift-towards-creative-living-in-the-analog-era-302709301.html).
- Stamp-n-Storage explicitly markets a library workflow that keeps stamps/dies visible, sorted and reachable in pockets, magnet cards, cases, crates and cabinets. This supports vertical indexed storage as an established workflow, not this exact geometry. Source: [Stamp-n-Storage](https://www.stampnstorage.com/).
- Its 5×7 case bundle lists a 5-3/8 × 7-1/2 inch case (about 136.5 × 190.5 mm), which fits the selected 145 × 195 mm default contract. Source: [Stamp-n-Storage Stamp-n-Die Bundle](https://www.stampnstorage.com/stamp-n-die-bundle/).
- Its Storage Pocket Plus is materially larger: 6×7 overall is 6-7/8 × 7-3/8 inch, and 9.5×7 overall is 7-5/8 × 9-7/8 inch. Those formats exceed this default rack depth and are explicit regeneration cases. Source: [Stamp-n-Storage Storage Pocket Plus](https://www.stampnstorage.com/storage-pocket-plus/).
- Scrapbook.com's large storage envelope is 7 × 10 inches, also outside the default. Source: [Scrapbook.com 7×10 storage envelopes](https://www.scrapbook.com/store/sbc-storage-3979.html).
- Etsy's current die-storage-envelope market page shows 62+ items and repeated 5×7/6×8 envelope/magnet-card offers. This demonstrates active supply and competition but does not prove demand for this rack. Source: [Etsy die storage envelope market](https://www.etsy.com/market/die_storage_envelope).

## Decision

Proceed with `IndexDock 15`: a skeletal three-rail comb plus high labeled follower/index frames. The rails keep the print and material burden below a full tray; each divider uses three 10.8 mm one-sided foot pads in a nominal 11.2 mm lane. The high frame keeps a tab visible above the 5×7 case class while printing flat on a second plate. Minimum 8 mm perimeter and 12 mm center rib are protected until physical racking and snag tests exist.

The product is a dry-indoor organizer, not protective packaging. No claim is made for loose-die safety, archival storage, rust prevention, magnet retention, blade guarding, child safety or compatibility with named brands.
