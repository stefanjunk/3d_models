# Physical test plan — user-owned

1. Print the lane-gap gauge and exact 10.8 mm key with the production machine/material/profile. Record the cleanest free-sliding, non-rattling candidate and update the source parameter before any full rack.
2. Load representative filled protective paper/polypropylene envelopes or cases at minimum, median and maximum qualified width/height/thickness. Never use loose dies or blades.
3. Perform 100 one-handed retrieval/reinsertions per representative envelope and inspect edges for snagging, scuffing, burrs and label obstruction.
4. Place a loaded divider at the first, middle and last lane; apply a 10° quasi-static lean in both rack directions. Reject cracking, lane escape, permanent deformation or rack tip.
5. Measure rack and divider corner lift after 24 h and seven days; maximum 0.8 mm on the test surface.
6. Run 250 divider remove/reinsert cycles and 500 envelope retrieval cycles. Recheck clearance, racking, label legibility and pad damage.
7. Record machine/unit, material/product/color/batch/conditioning, nozzle diameter/material/wear, orientation, exact slicer/profile hashes, ambient conditions and measurement method.

No physical pass may be inferred from the digital candidate.
