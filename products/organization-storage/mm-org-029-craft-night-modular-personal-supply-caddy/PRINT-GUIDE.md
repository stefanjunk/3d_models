# Draft print guide

- Use the selected 3MF with Anycubic Kobra 3 Max, 0.4 mm nozzle, Anycubic PLA reference profile and 0.20 mm Standard. All eleven objects are base/broad-face down and require no supports.
- Before the full plate, print `dock-clearance-gauge` and `dock-interface-key`. Try the 0.20, 0.40 and 0.60 mm total-clearance channels after cooling; regenerate `selected_total_clearance_mm` if 0.40 mm is not the best repeatable sliding fit.
- Do not use the rejected 2.4 mm caddy STL as a production part before flex, drop and loaded docking tests.
- Deburr channel entrances and nameplate slots without rounding the dovetail capture faces. Insert caddies vertically; do not force them laterally over the keys.
- Load dry adult craft supplies only, with no more than 750 g per caddy during the planned validation. Do not store hot tools, solvents or liquids, and do not use the nameplate boss as a handle.

No G-code is included. Review the final layer preview before printing.
