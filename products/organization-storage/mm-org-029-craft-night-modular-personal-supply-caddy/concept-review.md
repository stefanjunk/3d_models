# Concept review

Selected concept: `CraftOrbit 4`.

Four support-free two-compartment caddies slide vertically onto four captive dovetail keys around a shared open center cup. Each caddy accepts a separate flat-printed engraved nameplate. A three-station 0.20/0.40/0.60 mm docking gauge and exact key are included as first articles.

Rejected concepts: four unrelated monolithic named caddies do not create a group workflow; a common carry handle adds a high bending load and support burden; magnetic docks add purchased components and weak evidence; bottomed dovetail channels caused a native floating-cantilever warning and were replaced by bed-through channels and keys; snap tabs add fatigue risk before cycle evidence exists.
