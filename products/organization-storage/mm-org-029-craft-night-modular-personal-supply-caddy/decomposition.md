# Decomposition

- `personal-caddy`: authoritative 145 × 95 × 65 mm shell, two compartments, female vertical dock and nameplate receiver; one mesh is instantiated four times.
- `shared-center-hub`: authoritative 72 mm open cup and four cardinal bed-built male dovetail keys.
- `nameplate-*`: authoritative normalized participant name and engraved repository glyph geometry; each prints flat and installs vertically.
- `dock-clearance-gauge`: three production-representative female channels at 0.20/0.40/0.60 mm total clearance.
- `dock-interface-key`: exact male dovetail section used to choose a working clearance before the full system.
- `names.csv` → `name-batch.json`: normalized personalization source.
- `gridfont.py`: sole normalization, layout and glyph-geometry source for CAD and exact SVG proof.
- `four-caddy-system.3mf`: four caddy instances, hub, four plates and coupon pair; no installed assembly is fused into a monolithic manufacturing mesh.
