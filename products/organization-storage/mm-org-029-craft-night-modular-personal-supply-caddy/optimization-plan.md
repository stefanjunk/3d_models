# Optimization plan

Baseline B0 is the selected four-caddy system with 3.0 mm shells at 0.20 mm layer height. Process candidate A slices unchanged CAD at 0.28 mm and must retain at least three nominal engraving layers. Geometry candidate B reduces one caddy wall/base to 2.4 mm; it is generated only as a digital variant and rejected until loaded flex, drop and docking evidence exists.

The selected hollow system is compared geometrically with four solid caddy envelopes plus a solid hub. Exact whole-plate slicer metrics determine the process tradeoff. Infill percentage is not used as a substitute for explicit walls, base, divider, dock boss and key roots.
