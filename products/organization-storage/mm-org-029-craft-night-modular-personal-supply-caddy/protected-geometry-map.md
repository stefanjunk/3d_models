- Dovetail base/head/depth are 16/22/6 mm. The selected channel adds 0.40 mm total lateral clearance; the 0.20/0.40/0.60 mm gauge and exact key are mandatory first articles.
- Hub keys and caddy channels begin at Z=0. This revision removes the native floating-cantilever warning; do not restore a bottomed mid-air key without a new exact slice.
- Selected caddy wall/base are 3.0 mm. The 2.4 mm shell is a non-manufacturing variant until loaded flex, drop and docking tests pass.
- Nameplates are 70 × 24 × 2.0 mm in 70.4 × 2.4 mm slots. Engraving is 0.6 mm with at least 1.4 mm backing; glyph pixels remain at least 0.8 mm.
- The dock boss, the full caddy bottom loop, the internal divider and hub key roots are load-path members. Do not thin or window them independently.
- Dry adult craft supplies are the only approved content class. The nameplate boss is not a handle or structural carry point.
