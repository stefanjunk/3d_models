# Research validation

## Portfolio hypothesis

SKU-160 proposes a named personal caddy for each adult craft-night participant, docked around a shared center. The rescored opportunity is 87.8 at new-build rank 21. The portfolio row allows PLA and no supports, caps each part at 180 × 140 × 120 mm and excludes hot tools, solvents and food contact.

## Current evidence

- Michaels' 2026 trend report is directional category evidence, not conversion proof. It reports craft-night searches up 103%, girls-night craft searches up 242% and guided-kit sales up 86%, based on Michaels sales/search data and surveys. Source: [The Michaels Companies / PR Newswire, 2026-03-10](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/michaels-unveils-2026-creativity-trend-report-revealing-a-shift-towards-creative-living-in-the-analog-era-302709301.html).
- A current Etsy personalized craft-caddy listing offers a name/font choice, multiple caddy colors, two craft compartments and a 9.25 × 9 × 4.75 inch envelope. The personalization is a vinyl decal. This supports established name-personalization and caddy use, not demand for this docking geometry. Source: [Etsy personalized craft caddy](https://www.etsy.com/ca/listing/657196931/personalized-craft-caddy).
- A current Etsy 3D-printed PLA organizer listing offers an 8.5 × 4.1 × 5.5 inch multi-compartment caddy with a custom name tag and reports recent buyer reviews. This supports direct printed competition and makes an isolated personalized caddy insufficient differentiation. Source: [Etsy personalized 3D-printed organizer](https://www.etsy.com/in-en/listing/4460580615/personalized-3d-printed-organizer-with).

## Decision

Proceed with `CraftOrbit 4`: four small personal caddies dock vertically to a shared open center cup. The differentiator is a repeatable event system—one body mesh repeated four times, CSV-driven replaceable nameplates, a shared center and a fail-first docking coupon—rather than a monolithic personalized tote.

No claim is made for carrying by the nameplate boss, child safety, hot-tool storage, chemical resistance, liquid containment, food contact, transport locking or compatibility with named craft brands. Marketplace pages are competition evidence only; they do not establish sales volume for this product.
