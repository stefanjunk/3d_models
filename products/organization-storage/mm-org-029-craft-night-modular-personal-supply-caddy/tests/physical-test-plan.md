# Deferred physical test plan

1. Print the exact key and three-channel gauge. Record material batch, nozzle, orientation, measured key/channel widths and the lowest-clearance channel that slides through 25 mm without tools, binding or accidental release.
2. Regenerate the system if the working channel is not the selected 0.40 mm total clearance. Do not sand the production interface to make the digital result appear valid.
3. Print one caddy, hub and one nameplate. Verify vertical insertion/removal, full seating on a flat table, nameplate insertion, legibility and no sharp burrs.
4. Load the caddy with a representative dry 750 g tool mix. Check wall/divider flex, dock-root whitening or cracks, maximum 0.8 mm corner lift and no tip at 10° table tilt.
5. Complete one 300 mm tabletop slide and a controlled 100 mm unloaded drop onto each caddy base edge. Reject cracks, permanent rocking or loss of docking fit.
6. Complete 250 dock/remove cycles and 100 nameplate cycles. Record insertion force at cycles 1, 25, 100 and 250 and inspect for capture loss or debris.
7. Only after one position passes, print and load all four caddies. Verify no mutual interference, center-cup access and stability with asymmetric 0/250/500/750 g participant loads.

Physical execution and release remain user-owned. No child, hot-tool, solvent, liquid or food-contact test is authorized by this plan.
