# Draft print guide

- Print the correct SVG at 100%, disable fit-to-page and measure its 100 mm line. Reject outside 99.5–100.5 mm.
- Trace/test the paper outline in the empty drawer; measure drawer height separately. Do not infer depth from a photo.
- Print the 20 mm reference key and clearance gauge first. Regenerate if 1.0 mm per side is not the lowest repeatable no-bind fit.
- Selected plate: Kobra 3 Max, 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA, 0.28 mm Standard, no supports. No G-code is included.
- Dry small items only, maximum planned validation load 500 g per tray. Deburr edges and verify the drawer closes without interference.
