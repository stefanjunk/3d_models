# Concept review

Selected: three open support-free shells—quarter-round relief, rectangular notch relief and diagonal corner—generated from one polygon/offset engine. Exact 1:1 paper templates use the same outline functions. A three-slot coupon qualifies clearance before large prints.

Rejected: a universal flexible lattice cannot guarantee corner capture; a full custom drawer insert increases measurement and print risk; image-only scaling lacks an in-plane verification line; thin 2.4 mm shells remain a digital variant pending physical evidence.
