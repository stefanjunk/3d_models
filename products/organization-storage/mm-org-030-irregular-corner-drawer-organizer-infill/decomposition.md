# Decomposition

- `geometry.py`: sole owner of the three 2D footprints and wall offsets used by CAD, tests and paper templates.
- `round-corner`, `rectangular-notch`, `skewed-corner`: independent base-down open trays.
- `clearance-gauge` + `reference-key`: 21/22/23 mm openings around an exact 20 mm cylinder.
- Three SVG templates: 1:1 A4 customer measurement inputs with a 100 mm calibration line.
- Selected 3MF: five separate printable objects; light round tray remains outside manufacturing outputs.
