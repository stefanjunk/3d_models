# Irregular-corner drawer organizer infill

Fully parametric draft digital candidate for three irregular drawer-corner infill families. Edit `config/model-parameters.json`, run `tools/generate_templates.py`, then `cad/build.py`. Print/measure the exact template and coupon before a full tray. Physical fit/function and commercial release are deferred.

## P2 digital-candidate contents

This English development description applies to `MM-ORG-030` revision `0.1.0-draft.1`.
The complete candidate 3MF contains 5 declared print object(s):
skewed-corner, clearance-gauge, reference-key, round-corner, rectangular-notch. The included project preserves the selected build orientation and
the recorded support decision for the candidate.

## Development boundary

This is a digital P2 candidate, not a photograph or a physically qualified or
commercially released product. Fit, finish, strength, safety, rights clearance,
material behaviour and storefront claims remain governed by their separate
evidence and approval gates.
