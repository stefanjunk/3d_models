# Protected geometry map

- CAD and paper templates must call the same footprint functions; never redraw customer outlines separately.
- Selected clearance is 1.0 mm per side and must remain bracketed by 0.5/1.0/1.5 mm coupon stations.
- Selected wall/base are 3.0 mm; the 2.4 mm variant remains non-manufacturing.
- The complete perimeter wall and base are the stability loop. Do not window them without new spill, flex and cycle gates.
- A paper page is valid only after the printed 100 mm line measures within ±0.5%.
