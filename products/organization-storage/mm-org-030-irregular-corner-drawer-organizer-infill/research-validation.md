A current Etsy custom drawer-organizer listing explicitly promotes tailored corner-to-corner fit and reduced shifting. This supports active exact-fit competition but does not address hinge, pipe, notch or skewed-corner infill. Source: [Etsy custom drawer organizer](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1821928382/custom-drawer-organizer-kitchen-office).
