# Deferred physical test plan

1. Verify each printed template's 100 mm line within ±0.5%, cut it, trace the real corner and record caliper height separately.
2. Print key/gauge and choose the lowest clearance completing ten insertions without binding.
3. Print one matching tray; record edge gaps at five points and require no forced insertion, rocking above 0.8 mm or drawer interference.
4. Load 500 g of dry small items, complete 500 drawer cycles, then inspect wall/base cracks, abrasion, migration and retained clearance.
5. Perform an unloaded 100 mm base-edge drop. Reject cracks or permanent rocking.

Physical execution and commercial release remain user-owned.
