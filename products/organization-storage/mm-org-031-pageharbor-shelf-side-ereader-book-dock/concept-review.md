# Concept review — PageHarbor Duo 5

The selected concept uses two parallel full-width parking zones in a 176 × 108 mm footprint: a 31 mm book slot at the front and a 13 mm selected cased-device slot behind it. The e-reader sits 14 mm above the base on two separated shoes against four 11°-leaning rails; their 40 mm central discontinuity leaves the bottom connector region and rear cable route unobstructed.

Five device and three book fit stations are supplied as two U-slot gauges with matching exact key combs. The selected one-piece dock is generated for 12 mm device-case and 30 mm book thickness, while both dimensions remain source parameters. Four sparse back rails and two end gussets replace a solid back panel.

Status: approved for parametric CAD. Physical comfort, connector access and loaded stability remain outside this digital approval.
