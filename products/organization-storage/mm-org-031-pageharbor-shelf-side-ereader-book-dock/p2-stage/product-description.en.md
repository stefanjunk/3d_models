# Shelf-side e-reader and book dock

Fully parametric draft digital print candidate for portfolio SKU-123, a narrow passive shelf dock for one cased e-reader and one closed current book. Start with [PRINT-GUIDE.md](PRINT-GUIDE.md); physical fit and stability validation remain intentionally deferred.

## P2 digital-candidate contents

This English development description applies to `MM-ORG-031` revision `0.1.0-draft.1`.
The complete candidate 3MF contains 5 declared print object(s):
dock, device-fit-gauge, device-key-comb, book-fit-gauge, book-key-comb. The included project preserves the selected build orientation and
the recorded support decision for the candidate.

## Development boundary

This is a digital P2 candidate, not a photograph or a physically qualified or
commercially released product. Fit, finish, strength, safety, rights clearance,
material behaviour and storefront claims remain governed by their separate
evidence and approval gates.
