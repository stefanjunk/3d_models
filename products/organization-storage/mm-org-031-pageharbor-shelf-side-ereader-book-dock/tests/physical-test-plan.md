# Deferred physical test plan

- Print both exact key/gauge pairs; record insertion/removal at every device station (8/10/12/14/16 mm) and book station (18/30/42 mm). Select the lowest repeatable sliding clearance without force or visible wall spread.
- Measure five representative case thicknesses and test full-dock insertion/retrieval without button contact, screen/bezel interference or case marking.
- Verify the actual bottom connector can be reached in the 40 mm keepout and routed rearward without sharp bending. This is not a charging-performance test.
- Test one representative paperback near 18 mm, one selected 30 mm closed book and one hardcover near 42 mm; record cover width, mass, lean and retrieval.
- With representative device and book together, perform four-direction 10° tip tests, require self-return and no ejection, and record maximum base lift (target ≤0.8 mm).
- Perform five unloaded 100 mm edge drops onto a sacrificial hard surface, then inspect base, walls, rails and gussets.
- Perform 250 device and 250 book insertion/removal cycles, then repeat fit and tip checks and record cracks/permanent set.
- Stop on cracking, rail separation, excessive wobble, blocked controls/vents/connectors or unstable book behavior; revise parameters and regenerate evidence.
