# Concept review — PairPin 8

Four aligned columns each carry two spool/bobbin pairs. The front row places the spool at Y=25 mm and bobbin at Y=60 mm; the rear row mirrors the order at Y=91/126 mm so adjacent 45 mm spools and 22 mm bobbins retain at least 1.5 mm nominal envelope separation inside a 208 × 151 mm base.

Selected spool/bobbin posts are 5.0/4.5 mm with rounded tips and filleted support collars. Separate four-station gauges bracket 4.0–5.5 mm spool pins and 3.5–5.0 mm bobbin pins; real items select the largest repeatable sliding pin before the full rack is printed. Pair identity is the shared X centerline, and four 42 × 10 mm front-edge regions are reserved as flat adhesive/paper label datums without embossed content.

Status: approved for parametric CAD. Machine compatibility, snag resistance and loaded stability remain physical gates.
