# Deferred physical test plan

- Print both pin gauges and test at least four real spool styles plus Class 15, 15J and one additional machine-correct bobbin where available; record bore, outside diameter, height, selected pin and insertion feel.
- Verify bobbin compatibility from the sewing-machine manual independently. Storage fit must never be used to infer machine compatibility.
- Inspect every printed post, rounded tip, collar and collar/base transition visually and by drawing 500 mm of sacrificial loose thread across it in eight directions; require zero catch, fuzz or cut fibers.
- Load all eight stations with representative spools/bobbins. Perform four-direction 10° tip tests; require self-return, no ejection and ≤0.8 mm maximum base lift.
- Perform five unloaded 100 mm edge drops onto a sacrificial hard surface and inspect base/post/collar roots for cracks or permanent lean.
- Perform 250 insertion/removal cycles on one spool and one bobbin post, then repeat dimensional, snag and tip observations.
- Stop on tight fit, whitening, cracked roots, post lean, thread snag, unstable loading or label interference; revise parameters and regenerate all evidence.
