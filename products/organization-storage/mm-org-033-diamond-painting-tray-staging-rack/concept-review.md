# Concept review — MM-ORG-033 / concept v1

The selected concept is a single-piece six-level rack plus a compact front-mouth coupon. In use, trays enter from the open front and fall 6 mm toward a continuous rear stop. Two front corner tabs retain the tray while a 46 mm middle opening preserves label, finger and pour-spout access.

The rack is modeled in functional coordinates but its manufacturing mesh is rotated onto the left frame. This turns every shelf, rail and stop into a vertical web and removes the need for supports. The one-piece form avoids assembly fit risk; the coupon isolates tray-width, lip-clearance and removal ergonomics before committing to the full rack.

The concept satisfies the normalized envelope and low-risk adult craft boundary. Physical spill resistance and one-handed removal remain explicit human tests.
