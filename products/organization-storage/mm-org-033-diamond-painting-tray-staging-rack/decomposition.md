# Decomposition — MM-ORG-033

- `rack-master`: functional-coordinate CAD source with six sloped shelves, rear stop, side rails and split front tabs.
- `rack-manufacturing`: unchanged master rotated so the left frame is the build-plate datum.
- `mouth-coupon`: one shortened lane preserving the selected width, rearward fall, rail and front-tab geometry.
- `virtual-tray`: non-manufacturing envelope used only for clearance and concept validation.
- `print-set.3mf`: rack plus coupon as two independently printable watertight objects.

Named datums: `BED_SIDE_X0`, `TRAY_ENTRY_Y0`, `REAR_STOP_Y154`, `LEVEL_FRONT_TOP_n`, and `CENTER_SPOUT_OPENING`.
