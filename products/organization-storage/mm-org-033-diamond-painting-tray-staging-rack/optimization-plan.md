# Optimization plan

Baseline: the functional bounding block plus six solid tray-height layers.

Candidate: 2.4 mm sloped shelves, 3.2 mm side rails, a 4 mm rear stop and split 5 mm front tabs. Preserve tray clearances, rearward fall, entry opening, bed-side datum and the six independent levels. Accept only a single watertight component, no supports in the declared orientation, build-volume fit and at least 70% CAD-volume reduction versus the bounding-block proxy.
