# Requirements review — MM-ORG-033 / 0.1.0-draft.1

| Requirement | Normalized contract |
|---|---|
| Customer job | Stage six active, empty or lightly loaded diamond-painting sorting trays in a visible order while keeping their front labels and pour spouts accessible. |
| Reference tray | Maximum 86 x 145 x 12 mm; user must measure the actual tray rather than scale an STL. |
| Product envelope | Maximum 220 x 160 x 140 mm; selected rack target no larger than 96 x 154 x 128 mm. |
| Retrieval | One-hand front insertion/removal with a 46 mm central finger/spout opening and two independent retention tabs per level. |
| Spill resistance | Each level slopes down toward the rear by 6 mm over 145 mm and has rear, left/right and split front retention. Physical spill testing remains required. |
| Manufacturing | PLA, Anycubic Kobra 3 Max reference machine, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, no supports; rack prints on its left side. |
| Parametric scope | Tray width/depth/height, station count, pitch, rearward fall, rail height, front opening and wall/shelf thickness. |
| Safety boundary | Adult dry craft organization only; not a child product and no claim for loose-piece containment during transport. |
| Human boundary | Physical printing, loaded spill/retrieval tests, surface/edge review, safety and commercial release. |

User authority in `autonomy-policy.json` permits agent approval through the deterministic print-candidate stage. All outputs remain `DRAFT`.
