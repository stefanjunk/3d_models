# Physical test plan — human stage

1. Measure each generic tray at three width and height locations; reject trays outside 86 x 145 x 12 mm.
2. Print the mouth coupon unchanged. Insert/remove each empty tray 25 times with one hand; record snags, whitening, edge contact and whether the front tabs obstruct its pour spout or label.
3. Print the rack unchanged. Load six empty trays, then repeat with 20 g and 120 g of inert test mass per tray.
4. Tilt the desk board 5 degrees forward and laterally; record any tray crossing a stop. Do not use loose craft pieces for the first test.
5. Perform 100 one-handed insertion/removal cycles on the most-used level and inspect shelves, rails and tabs under raking light.
6. Reject sharp edges, rocking, cracks, visible permanent deformation or any tray that can leave its level during the defined stationary-use test.

Physical PASS must identify printer, firmware, exact filament/batch, nozzle, slicer version/profile hashes, orientation and measurements. It cannot be inferred from this plan.
