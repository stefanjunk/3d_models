# Print guide

1. Print the TPU pen gauge and test the intended pen at 9, 12, and 15 mm openings.
2. For the modular version, print the PETG S/M/L clip plate and choose the smallest clip that seats without damaging or permanently bowing the cover.
3. Print one TPU rail-loop insert and snap its socket over the outside rail of the chosen PETG clip.
4. Alternatively, print the one-piece all-TPU variant for covers within the declared flexible range.
5. Keep supports disabled. Use a brim if the 30 mm side-printed clips need more bed adhesion.
6. Complete `tests/physical-test-plan.md` before treating the design as compatible or saleable.

The supplied G-code is local validation evidence for the exact Kobra 3 Max/profile inputs, not authorization to start a printer. Perform the final slicer layer, seam, support, and tool review yourself.
