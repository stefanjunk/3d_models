# Concept review — FlexDock

Selected concept: a size-coded PETG U-clip carries a short external mushroom rail. A replaceable TPU ring insert snaps laterally over that rail through a compliant C-socket. The same pen-ring section is reused in a one-piece TPU cover clip.

Why selected:

- removes the dominant adhesive dependency found in current products;
- separates the fatigue-prone loop from the cover clip;
- avoids a multi-material single-tool slice by using separate PETG and TPU plates;
- uses constant side profiles so both clips and loops can print without generated supports;
- offers a low-cost all-TPU fallback for covers outside the PETG fit preference.

Rejected: adhesive-backed loop (insufficient differentiation), metal spring clip (purchased part and cover-marking risk), magnet pair (swallowing/electronic-device risk), and print-in-place hinge (unnecessary tolerance and fatigue complexity).
