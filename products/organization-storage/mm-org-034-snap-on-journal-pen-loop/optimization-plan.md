# Optimization plan

- Preserve cover gaps, rail/socket engagement, ring bore, edge radii, and material separation.
- Use constant side profiles and short axial widths to remove generated supports.
- Minimize PETG with a cantilever tongue rather than a full sleeve.
- Reuse one TPU insert across all PETG sizes and keep the gauge below a full-loop print cost.
- Reject optimizations that thin PETG below 1.6 mm or TPU load paths below 1.6 mm before physical fatigue evidence.
- Keep the integral TPU loop flush to a clip side in the side-print orientation; centering it creates a native floating-cantilever warning.
