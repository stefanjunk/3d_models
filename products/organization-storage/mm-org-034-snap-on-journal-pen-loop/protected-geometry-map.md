# Protected geometry

- PETG clip gaps: 1.8 / 2.6 / 3.4 mm nominal before retention rib.
- PETG rail: common geometry across S/M/L; head and neck may not vary independently from the TPU socket.
- TPU socket: radial snap mouth must remain narrower than the rail head but wider than the neck.
- Pen ring: 10.8 mm relaxed ID and at least 1.8 mm radial wall.
- Cover-contact edges: minimum modeled 0.6 mm fillet where CAD permits.
- Manufacturing orientation: profile side down; generated support remains disabled.
