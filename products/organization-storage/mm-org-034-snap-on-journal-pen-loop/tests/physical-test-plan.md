# Human physical test plan

1. Measure cover thickness at the intended edge; record cover material and finish.
2. Print the TPU 9/12/15 mm gauge. Select the smallest bore that accepts the pen without surface damage or excessive force.
3. Test PETG S/M/L on a sacrificial or replaceable cover sample; select the smallest non-damaging size. Inspect after 24 hours for dents, glossing, delamination, and bowing.
4. Snap the TPU insert over the common rail. Confirm full seating and no torn layers.
5. Perform 100 cover attach/remove cycles and 500 pen insert/remove cycles; record cracks, set, rail migration, and retention change.
6. Carry the closed notebook in a representative bag and perform ten 0.5 m soft-surface handling drops. Record pen loss and snagging; this is not a safety-retention test.
7. Repeat essential tests for the one-piece TPU variant.
8. Photograph results, record machine/material/color/batch/nozzle/orientation/profile, and keep measurements in this product folder.
