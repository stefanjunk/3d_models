# Concept review — InkNest Duo

Selected concept: two standalone open-scaffold cassettes share a lane pitch and construction but have different measured case envelopes. Three longitudinal beams support each closed pad with side rails, split front tabs, and a full rear stop. A thin full-width top deck with perimeter lips becomes a stamp-rest tray when lined with scrap paper.

Why selected:

- explicitly pilots the two portfolio-required craft-system dimensions;
- stores cases substantially flat while keeping their front labels accessible;
- separates brands/sizes into independent modules rather than forcing a universal loose slot;
- side orientation turns shelves, lips, and rear stops into vertical webs without generated supports;
- open beam shelves reduce material while retaining a flat three-line support condition.

Rejected: a tall universal vertical-slot tower (pad-orientation and mixed-thickness uncertainty), a brand-specific molded insert (low transfer), an ink-well or cleaning tray (chemical/sealing scope), and a monolithic two-size rack (poor selective printing and portfolio reuse).
