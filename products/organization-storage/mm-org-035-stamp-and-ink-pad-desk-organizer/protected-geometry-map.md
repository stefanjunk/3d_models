# Protected geometry

- Square case envelope: 78 x 78 x 21 mm.
- Rectangular case envelope: 100 x 69 x 21 mm.
- Nominal width clearance: 1.5 mm per side; minimum vertical clearance: 3.0 mm.
- Three support beams per lane, including a center beam.
- Split front tabs leave at least 42 mm square / 56 mm rectangular label and finger access.
- Rear stops and uprights connect all levels.
- Top tray remains full-width and requires a paper liner for inked-stamp use.
- Manufacturing orientation remains left-frame down with generated support disabled.
