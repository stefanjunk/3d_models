# Research and competition gate — 2026-08-28

Decision: proceed to controlled CAD with two measured envelopes. Evidence supports active demand and recurring organization problems, but not a universal-fit or ink-preservation claim.

## Dimension and competition evidence

- Ranger's official Distress Ink Pad Storage Tin holds fifteen 3 x 3 inch closed pads and has 85 displayed reviews, supporting the square pilot and visible/organized access demand: https://rangerink.com/products/tim-holtz-distress-ink-pad-storage-tin
- A German VersaColor listing publishes 98 x 67 x 20 mm, supporting the rectangular pilot with 2/2/1 mm allowance: https://www.frauhoelle.com/produkt/versacolor-stempelkissen/
- A German organizer seller describes unclear stacks, lost colors, slipping, and jamming as the category problem: https://lillify.de/collections/stempelkissenaufbewahrung

## Qualified problem signals

1. Overflowing drawers: a Ranger customer explicitly describes an overflowing ink drawer as the storage trigger.
2. Visibility: Ranger reviews value seeing/selecting the exact color; another asks for labels nearer the top.
3. Unstable stacks: the German tower seller identifies slipping and jamming stacks.
4. Mixed case sizes: published 3 x 3 inch and 98 x 67 x 20 mm families require different controlled envelopes.
5. Workflow transition: German crafters describe bringing pad towers onto the desk and returning them to dust-protected storage afterward: https://www.handarbeitsfrau.de/threads/stempelkissen-turm.37987/
6. Lid dependence: German guidance warns ink pads can dry out; this organizer therefore holds closed manufacturer cases only and does not replace their lids: https://wunderlich-s.de/blog/stempeln/grundwissen/stempel-reinigen/
7. Label tracking: crafters report adding swatch/brand/color labels to identify pads, supporting an unobstructed front opening: https://www.reddit.com/r/cardmaking/comments/17z2x4q/

## Design response and limits

The candidate uses separate square/rectangular modules, shallow first-fit coupons, front label openings, rear retention, flat pad lanes, and a paper-lined stamp rest. Evidence is qualitative; it does not establish market size, willingness to pay, compatibility beyond the two envelopes, ink chemistry, or physical durability.
