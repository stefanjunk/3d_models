# Human physical test plan

1. Record closed case width/depth/height, brand/family, lid closure, and intended storage orientation.
2. Print the dual coupon; verify insertion, 30-second inverted hand retention over a soft surface, label access, and removal without lid opening.
3. Print only the matching unchanged module first. Load three closed cases and check desk slip/tip during one-hand removal from each lane.
4. Perform 100 insertion/removal cycles per lane; record wear, cracks, retention change, lid scuffing, and label obstruction.
5. Line the top tray with scrap paper. Rest the representative stamp for ten workflow cycles; inspect for transfer beyond the liner, staining, and cleaning damage.
6. Confirm rear stops retain cases during normal desk repositioning; this is not transport qualification.
7. Photograph results and record printer, PETG manufacturer/product/color/batch/drying, nozzle, orientation, exact profile, environment, and measurement method.
