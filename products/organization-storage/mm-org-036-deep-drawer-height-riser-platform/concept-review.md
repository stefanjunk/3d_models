# Concept review

Selected concept: a one-piece lattice deck printed with its use-top face on the build plate. Four corner posts and one center post then grow vertically without support; after printing the platform is flipped for use.

Why selected:

- reuses an existing tray rather than replacing it;
- exposes the cavity from all four sides;
- avoids assembly and purchased hardware;
- keeps the wide deck on the build plate and all posts support-free;
- uses orthogonal ribs rather than a full 180 x 140 mm slab.

Alternatives retained as test evidence:

- four-post common-grid coupon: lowest obstruction and direct load paths;
- six-rib common-grid coupon: more distributed side support but less open access;
- hanging second tray: proven retail format, but drawer-wall compatibility and removal motion are harder to parameterize;
- stacked boxes: easy to buy, but does not preserve a single accessible hidden cavity.
