# Decomposition

- `full-platform`: parametric one-piece manufacturing body; owns deck perimeter, orthogonal ribs, four corner posts, center post, footprint and lift.
- `corner-post-coupon`: physical A coupon; owns common 80 x 70 mm perimeter-and-cross-rib load deck and four 12 x 12 mm posts.
- `rib-support-coupon`: physical B coupon; owns the same load deck and six 3.2 x 10 mm side ribs.
- `reference-upper-tray`: visualization envelope only; never included in manufacturing meshes.
- `3mf-plates`: one full product plate and one two-coupon plate, both PLA and independently sliced.
- Protected datums: use-top plane, foot plane, external footprint, centered load point, open front access, and manufacturing top-face-down orientation.
