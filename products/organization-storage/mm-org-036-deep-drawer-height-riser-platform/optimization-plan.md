# Optimization plan

- Compare the selected orthogonal rib grid against a solid-deck proxy while preserving footprint, lift and support locations.
- Keep perimeter beams, rib intersections and every post/deck joint volumetrically connected.
- Prefer the five-post full pilot for smaller unsupported deck spans; retain the lower-obstruction four-post coupon and side-rib coupon for physical comparison.
- Do not thin sections or remove the center post based only on CAD volume.
- Stop optimization if the exact slicer reports floating regions, support generation, multiple tools, flow above policy, or a non-empty native warning.
