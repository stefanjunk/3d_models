# Requirements review

- Portfolio source: `SKU-104`, Deep-drawer height riser platform; priority score 85.8 and next-candidate rank 1.
- Customer job: lift a small existing tray to an accessible height while retaining a usable hidden cavity.
- Pilot envelope: 180 x 140 x 50 mm; maximum future configured envelope remains 220 x 220 x 90 mm.
- Reference upper-tray footprint: 180 x 140 mm maximum and 80 x 70 mm minimum, flat-bottomed.
- Declared load program: 2 kg distributed static load; 30-day creep evidence is mandatory before a physical-performance claim.
- Process: FFF, Anycubic Kobra 3 Max reference, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer, Anycubic PLA profile, supports disabled.
- Required outputs: corner-post coupon, rib-support coupon, selected full pilot, STL/STEP/3MF, exact local slices, and machine-readable validation.
- Exclusions: hot drawers above 40 °C, impact loads, stepping/sitting, children, food contact, safety-critical support, and universal drawer/tray compatibility.
