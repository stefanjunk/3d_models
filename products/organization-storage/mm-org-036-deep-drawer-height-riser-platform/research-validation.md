# Demand and competition gate

Checked 2026-08-28. Gate result: **positive for a bounded pilot**, not validated sales demand.

Qualified signals:

1. IKEA Germany lists 52 UPPDATERA drawer-organization products and describes configurable exact-fit layouts; multiple core products have 100+ reviews. This validates the category, not LiftDeck sales. <https://www.ikea.com/de/de/cat/uppdatera-serie-54968/>
2. Tchibo's variable organizer explicitly works as a second level in high drawers and has 238 reviews, but is currently unavailable and depends on drawer walls up to 12 mm. <https://www.tchibo.de/products/182198739990/variabler-schubladen-organizer>
3. IKEA's adjustable UPPDATERA insert has 136 reviews; related divider reviews report slipping, worn clamps, unclear half-drawer installation, and instability. A freestanding measured platform avoids wall-clamp dependence. <https://www.ikea.com/de/de/p/uppdatera-einsatz-verstellbar-grau-50544179/> and <https://www.ikea.com/de/de/p/uppdatera-trennsteg-fuer-schublade-90459999/>
4. A German made-to-measure organizer seller requests exact drawer width/depth/height and supplies a removal tab, supporting willingness to order measured drawer products. <https://stich-haltig.de/produkt/organizer-nach-mass/>
5. A 2024 deep-drawer thread describes wasted vertical space and specifically asks for another accessible level; the thread had 23 upvotes at the captured result. <https://www.reddit.com/r/organization/comments/1d5i6a2/deep_kitchen_drawer_organizer/>
6. A 2026 Gridfinity thread asks how to maximize vertical space in deep drawers and considers sliding/tiered storage, showing the same current maker workflow problem. <https://www.reddit.com/r/gridfinity/comments/1rf0cc3/managing_deep_drawers/>
7. Etsy Germany shows 257 relevant results for custom 3D-printed drawer organizers, including generator downloads around 5.95 EUR and fitted physical offers around 29.90–44.99 EUR. Competition is real, but the captured results emphasize complete trays/dividers rather than a reusable under-tray riser. <https://www.etsy.com/market/custom_drawer_organizer_3d_print>
8. A current German Kleinanzeigen offer sells made-to-measure PLA/PETG drawer organizers from 6 EUR, reinforcing local price pressure and the need for a distinct height-reuse function. <https://www.kleinanzeigen.de/s-anzeige/schubladen-organizer-nach-mass-3d-gedruckt/3462693232-93-3436>

Interpretation:

- Problem evidence: positive.
- Exact-fit customization evidence: positive.
- Direct riser-platform competition: sparse in the captured German results.
- Price evidence: crowded low end; the pilot must win on measured fit, material efficiency, reuse of an existing tray, and a clear coupon-led load workflow.
- Remaining unknowns: German product-level search volume, conversion, return rate, willingness to pay for the riser alone, long-term PLA creep, drawer temperature, and actual tray stability.
