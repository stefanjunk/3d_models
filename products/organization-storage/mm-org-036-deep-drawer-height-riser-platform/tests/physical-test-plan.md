# Physical test plan

1. Condition both coupons and the chosen PLA spool in the intended indoor environment for 24 hours.
2. Measure coupon top-plane height at all four corners and center to 0.1 mm or better.
3. Place each coupon on the intended drawer liner or a flat reference plate. Apply the same flat 80 x 70 mm load spreader and a verified 2.0 kg static mass.
4. Record center and corner height at 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days, and 30 days; photograph the same datum view each time.
5. Fail a coupon for cracking, delamination, permanent rocking, visible buckling, or more than 1.0 mm center-height loss after unloading for 24 hours.
6. Print the unchanged full candidate only after reviewing the coupon comparison. Repeat a distributed 2 kg test for 30 days on the full platform with the real upper tray.
7. In the real drawer, record opening/closing for 100 cycles, tray slide/tip, cavity access, top clearance, surface marking, and maximum measured drawer temperature.
8. Keep commercial release blocked until safety, appearance, rights/provenance, offer, and product documentation reviews pass.
