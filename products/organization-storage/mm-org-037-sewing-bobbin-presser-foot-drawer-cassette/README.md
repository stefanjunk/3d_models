# MM-ORG-037 StitchCell 7+10

Controlled parametric implementation of portfolio research item `SKU-107`.

StitchCell 7+10 is a 210 x 150 x 28 mm sewing-accessory drawer cassette with ten generic, non-retaining presser-foot cells and a removable seven-bobbin insert. Two inserts are supplied: a `20.5 mm` CB-family nominal target and a `21.6 mm` horizontal-family nominal target. They are dimensional variants, not brand compatibility claims.

Start with `PRINT-GUIDE.md` and print the gauge plate before either full kit. The gauge plate tests both bobbin diameters, 30 / 35 / 40 mm presser-foot cell widths, and the R2 Full metriMade identity at the exact process. Select only the insert variant that fits the intended bobbins with usable finger clearance and no forced seating.

Revision `0.1.0-draft.1` is a supportless digital print candidate. The cassette and both inserts are one-component watertight meshes, each reusable insert has a 0.4 mm-per-side cassette clearance, and all three marked product parts carry the unscaled R2 Full identity on their underside. Exact Anycubic Slicer Next 1.3.9.4 jobs are warning-free. The selected shell-and-divider cassette saves 30.16% CAD volume against an equally marked conservative solid proxy.

Physical bobbin fit, presser-foot fit, insert retention, drawer fit and cycling, thread-snag inspection, Full-mark legibility, appearance, safety, and commercial release remain human gates.
