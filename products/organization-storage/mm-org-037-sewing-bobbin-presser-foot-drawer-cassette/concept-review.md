# Concept review

Selected concept: **StitchCell 7+10**.

The 210 x 150 x 28 mm one-piece cassette has a full-width rear bay for one drop-in bobbin insert and a 5 x 2 grid of open presser-foot cells. The inserts retain bobbins only by a shallow circular pocket, avoiding flexible clips, thread contact pressure and machine-brand geometry. One versus two raised index bars distinguish the 20.5 and 21.6 mm inserts.

Rejected concepts:

- Hinged lidded case: higher print time, fatigue and latch risk; weaker drawer-specific differentiation.
- Snap-fit bobbin clips: high thread-snag and tolerance risk.
- Brand-shaped presser-foot sockets: compatibility, measurement and IP burden is disproportionate for the first pilot.
- One universal bobbin pocket: would hide the known dimensional variation and make the fit claim untestable.
