# Product decomposition

- `cassette`: primary durable body; drawer envelope, rear insert bay, ten open foot cells, front retrieval notches and Full underside identity.
- `cb-20p5-insert`: reusable seven-pocket insert for nominal 20.5 mm bobbins; one index bar and Full underside identity.
- `horizontal-21p6-insert`: reusable seven-pocket insert for nominal 21.6 mm bobbins; two index bars and Full underside identity.
- `bobbin-fit-gauge`: two shallow pockets proving the declared diameter allowances before printing an insert.
- `foot-cell-gauge`: 30/35/40 mm open cells for checking representative sacrificial feet.
- `full-watermark-coupon`: canonical generated intended-process identity coupon.

Source of truth: `config/model-parameters.json`. All geometry is original parametric CAD. Third-party bobbins and presser feet are purchased/use interfaces and are not included.
