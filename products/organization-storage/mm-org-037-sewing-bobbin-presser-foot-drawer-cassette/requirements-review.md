# Requirements review

- Portfolio source: `SKU-107`, sewing bobbin and presser-foot drawer cassette.
- Pilot envelope: at most 220 x 220 x 45 mm and one Kobra 3 Max plate.
- Store ten mixed domestic presser feet in open non-retaining cells; make no brand compatibility claim.
- Store seven bobbins face-up in a replaceable insert. Provide 20.5 mm CB and 21.6 mm horizontal-hook variants with 0.8 mm diametral allowance.
- Provide a two-size bobbin gauge and 30/35/40 mm foot-cell gauge before the full cassette.
- Every manufacturing mesh must be one connected, watertight component and print base-down without generated support.
- Preserve at least 2.4 mm cassette base/walls, 2.0 mm foot dividers and a 2.0 mm residual wall behind the 0.4 mm identity recess.
- Intended reference process: PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, drawer temperature at or below 40 C.
- Physical fit, snag, retrieval, drawer clearance, mark legibility, appearance, safety and commercial release remain human gates.
