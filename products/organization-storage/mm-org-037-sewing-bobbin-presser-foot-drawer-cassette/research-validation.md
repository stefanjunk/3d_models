# Bounded demand and competition gate

Decision: **PASS — proceed to a controlled digital print candidate.** Demand is plausible, but pricing and fit claims remain hypotheses until listing and physical tests exist.

Evidence checked on 2026-08-28:

- Prym lists multiple household bobbin geometries, including 20.5 x 11.5 mm, 21.2 x 9.2 mm and 21.6 x 10.4 mm horizontal-hook variants; this supports replaceable size-specific inserts rather than a universal-fit claim: https://de.handicraft.com/products/nahmaschinenspulen-fur-horizontalgreifer
- Prym's CB bobbin is independently listed at 20.5 x 11.7 mm, confirming a second common geometry close enough to confuse but different enough to qualify: https://www.naehwelt-flach.de/prym-naehmaschinenspulen-fuer-cb-greifer-metall-5-st.html
- A dedicated 28-bobbin Etsy case shows 110 current item reviews and a 4.9 rating, supporting paid demand for orderly, non-rattling storage: https://www.etsy.com/listing/97704428/locking-bobbin-box-set-sewing-machine
- Etsy currently has active presser-foot storage and machine-foot organizer market pages, including branded and 3D-printed fit-specific products: https://www.etsy.com/market/presser_feet_storage
- A current Janome accessory box uses a removable presser-foot tray; one of its two reviews asks for more vertical organization and reports the box smaller than expected: https://www.premierstitching.com/janome-sewing-machine-accessory-storage-box-organizer/
- Sewists report losing bobbins, searching for feet, and resorting to mixed generic boxes or drawer prints. These are problem signals, not market-size estimates: https://www.reddit.com/r/sewing/comments/mcvuw3/ and https://www.reddit.com/r/quilting/comments/rvisff/
- Recent 3D-printed brand-specific foot holders confirm additive-manufacturing competition and a compatibility/IP hazard: https://www.etsy.com/listing/4304600466/bernina-830-foot-organizer-for-red-box

Selected differentiation: one shallow drawer cassette combines ten deliberately generic open foot cells with a removable seven-bobbin insert. Two original insert variants use published factual diameters, not copied accessory-box geometry. The separate gauges bound fit before the full print. No compatibility with a particular sewing-machine brand or foot system is claimed.

Commercial uncertainty: low-cost injection-molded cases are strong substitutes, and a mixed cassette may be valuable only to users who keep a drawer beside the machine. Validate willingness to pay, drawer envelope, foot-cell mix, thread snagging and retrieval before release.
