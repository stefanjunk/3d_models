# Physical test plan

1. Print the gauge plate with the exact PLA/nozzle/layer/bed process intended for the product.
2. Test at least three known 20.5 mm CB bobbins and three known 21.6 mm horizontal bobbins in the labeled pockets. Record insertion, lift-out, rocking, flange abrasion and thread snagging.
3. Place representative low-value presser feet in the 30, 35 and 40 mm cells. Select only a size that allows two-finger retrieval without contact damage.
4. Print the selected kit. Verify the insert drops into the rear bay without force, can be lifted, and does not bind after 20 insert/removal cycles.
5. Load all seven pockets and ten cells. Run 100 drawer open/close cycles; record migration, rattling, thread escape, cracking and edge damage.
6. Verify the drawer closes with at least 5 mm vertical clearance above the tallest stored item and stays at or below 40 C.
7. Inspect all required Full identity lines on the coupon, cassette and chosen insert. Any merged or missing character fails the mark gate.
