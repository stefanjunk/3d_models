# MM-ORG-038 MomentPair 2

Controlled parametric implementation of portfolio research item `SKU-180`.

MomentPair 2 is a `150 x 52 x 22 mm` clip-free desk display for one photo and one milestone card. The one-piece base uses a `120 mm` rear slot and a shorter, right-offset `50 mm` front slot so the card pair remains visible without a rotating metal clip or purchased hardware. Both slots share a rigid tapered-groove contract: `2.8 mm` at the opening, `0.2 mm` at the floor, and `8°` back tilt. Their depths are `10 mm` and `8 mm`.

Start with `PRINT-GUIDE.md` and print the exact-depth gauge before the full base. The gauge reproduces both final slot depths and must be tested with the actual photo and card stock. The intended `0.25–1.2 mm` stock range is a design target, not a physical compatibility claim.

Revision `0.1.0-draft.1` is a supportless digital print candidate. The base, gauge, and watermark coupon are one-component watertight meshes; both 3MF packages pass strict inspection; exact Anycubic Slicer Next 1.3.9.4 jobs are warning-free; and the one-piece rail-and-bridge layout saves `38.29%` CAD volume against an equally marked solid-envelope proxy. The unscaled R2 Full metriMade identity is recessed on the finished underside.

Customer photos, names, dates, and card artwork are not retained in the controlled files. The neutral `YOUR MOMENT` placeholder is the only supplied front text. Card fit and marking, loaded stability, 100-swap durability, hidden-content margin, Full-mark legibility, final preview, appearance, safety, privacy workflow, and commercial release remain human gates.
