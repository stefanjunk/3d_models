# Concept review

Selected concept: **MomentPair 2**, a two-rail desktop display.

- A full-width rear rail holds the photo and provides the primary stability and watermark land.
- A shorter front rail holds a smaller milestone card offset to the right, allowing both cards to remain visible.
- Two low bridges connect the rails into one printable body without filling the entire footprint.
- Both grooves narrow continuously, avoiding a moving clip while accepting a bounded thickness family.
- A short gauge reproduces both final groove depths before the full base is printed.
- Recessed placeholder text demonstrates local personalization without embedding real customer data.

Rejected: rotating wire clip (current review reports picture rotation), full solid block (unnecessary CAD volume), snap flexure (fatigue/edge risk), and a universal-card claim.

Status: selected for parametric implementation; physical grip and stability remain unproven.
