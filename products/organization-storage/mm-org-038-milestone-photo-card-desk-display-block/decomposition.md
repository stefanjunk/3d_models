# Decomposition

| Component | Source of truth | Interface | Distribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dual-slot display base | `config/model-parameters.json` + `cad/build.py` | Tapered rear/front groove contract | Primary product |
| Two-depth slot gauge | Same groove generator and parameter file | Exact rear/front cross-sections | First-print coupon |
| Full identity coupon | Generated `MM-WM-001-R2` profile | Intended PLA process | Physical qualification coupon |
| Customer cards/photos | Customer-owned external item | Measured thickness and safe lower margin | Not included or retained |

Registration datum: base center at XY origin, bed at Z=0, front toward negative Y. Both card faces lean toward positive Y with height. The rear rail underside owns the product identity; coupons are covered by the marked product and their own exact identity coupon.
