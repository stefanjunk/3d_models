# Demand and competition gate

Date: 2026-08-28. Portfolio source: `SKU-180`, planning score `85.0`.

Decision: **PASS for one bounded digital prototype**. Demand is supported, but the offer is crowded and price-sensitive. The differentiated hypothesis is a compact clip-free dual display with one rear photo groove, one smaller offset milestone-card groove, a shared tapered thickness contract, and privacy-minimizing local personalization.

Qualified signals:

1. Personalization Mall's graduation photo block reports `4.7/5` from 18 reviews and explicitly targets desk/shelf use; a review also calls out unexpectedly small size, supporting exact dimensions in the offer: https://www.personalizationmall.com/Personalized-Photo-Clip-Holder-Block-Graduation-Gift-p25971.prod
2. Smiling Tree's wooden photo-holder blocks show 134 reviews and report bulk use for more than 100 graduates plus employee-anniversary desk displays: https://smilingtreegifts.com/products/clip-photo-block-holder
3. A current Etsy personalized wedding/photo block reports `4.9/5` from 25 item reviews, 408 favorites, replaceable photos, and explicit name/date inputs: https://www.etsy.com/listing/715696300/personalised-wedding-photo-block
4. Personal Creations' graduation photo keepsake block reports `4.8/5` from 382 reviews, confirming milestone-photo gift demand at a higher price point: https://www.personalcreations.com/product/graduation-photo-keepsake-block-30198911
5. A German-facing Etsy shop lists personalized retirement photo desk displays from `69.90 EUR` and showed more than 20 shoppers with an item in cart at capture time, supporting a German milestone-gift segment: https://www.etsy.com/de/shop/UnserMomentWerk
6. A current Etsy photo-holder review says a rotating metal clip did not keep the picture in place. The pilot therefore uses no wire or purchased clip: https://www.etsy.com/listing/4352230501/customizable-rustic-wood-block-photo
7. Current slotted holders cluster around `1.8–3.0 mm` top grooves, `10–14 mm` depth, and a slight angle. The pilot uses a tapered `2.8 to 0.2 mm` groove and supplies an exact-profile gauge rather than claiming universal grip: https://www.refinedhandmade.co.nz/product/slotted-card-stand-pine/ and https://www.timberyardstudio.com/product/photo-holder-card-stand/
8. A German-facing personalized stand explicitly warns that a `14 mm` groove hides the bottom of the image. This pilot limits the rear groove to `10 mm` and front groove to `8 mm`, and documents the hidden edge: https://www.etsy.com/de/listing/1412076996/vatertagsgeschenk-benutzerdefinierte

Hypotheses still open: German product-level search volume, acquisition cost, conversion, the value of two simultaneous cards versus one, willingness to pay for FDM rather than wood/acrylic, card-edge marking, actual thickness/grip, stability with large cards, text quality, and repeat purchase. No customer photo, child-photo data, copyrighted card art, name, or date is included or retained.
