# Physical test plan

1. Print the two-depth groove gauge and Full identity coupon with the exact intended process.
2. Test representative `0.25–1.2 mm` cards. Record thickness method, insertion depth, grip, removal force by repeatable method if available, surface marking, and the preferred groove.
3. Perform 100 insert/remove cycles with the thinnest and thickest accepted cards. Fail on cracking, visible cutting, permanent widening, or loss of stable grip.
4. Print the unchanged full base only after the gauge passes. Test one intended photo and one milestone card simultaneously.
5. Tap the desk in four directions and open/close the nearby drawer or desk surface 100 times. Fail on tip, walk, card ejection, or interference.
6. Inspect the lower 10 mm / 8 mm hidden card margins and confirm no important photo/card content is obscured.
7. Inspect desk-contact edges, recessed placeholder text, and Full identity for sharpness and legibility.
8. Complete final slicer layer/seam/support/bed review, appearance, safety, privacy/proof workflow, rights, and commercial review.

Record printer unit/firmware, actual filament product/color/batch/conditioning, nozzle material/wear, bed surface, environment, instruments, uncertainty, photos, and failures. No physical pass is asserted in this draft.
