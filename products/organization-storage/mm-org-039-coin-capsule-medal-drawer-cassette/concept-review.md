# Concept review

Selected concept: `CollectorGrid 6`, a one-piece open-lattice host with six cells and loose measured adapters.

Rejected concepts:

- Tight vertical rows: higher capacity but reproduce the reported extraction and finger-access failure.
- Bare-coin wells: compact, but unqualified PLA contact creates abrasion, chemistry and collectible-value risk.
- Universal foam or flex clips: add uncertain material ageing, retention and purchased-part variables.
- One monolithic pocket size: forces a new host for every capsule family.

The selected host keeps the drawer interface invariant. Two identical support rails per cell raise capsules near the top, front-edge notches permit fingertip access, and loose adapters localize the measured capsule interface. The design remains a digital hypothesis until actual protective capsules pass the gauge, abrasion and cycle plan.
