# Decomposition

- `host`: authoritative drawer envelope, six cell datums, lattice floor, support rails, access notches and Full underside identity.
- `square-50 adapter`: reusable drop-in ring with `50.8 x 50.8 mm` measured opening, label bay and Micro underside identity.
- `round-46 adapter`: reusable drop-in ring with `46.8 mm` measured opening, label bay and Micro underside identity.
- `interface gauge`: reproduces the actual host large-square cell, both adapter openings and the paper-label bay.
- `identity coupons`: generated Full and Micro R2 physical qualification artifacts.
- `purchased objects`: protective coin/medal capsules and paper labels; no coin, medal, foam, felt or adhesive is included or modeled as validated.

The host/cell coordinate frame is centered on the host footprint with `+Z` upward. Adapters own capsule fit; the host owns only the adapter envelope and direct large-square cell.
