# Optimization plan

- Baseline: equally marked host with a continuous `2.4 mm` floor, unchanged walls, dividers, supports and interfaces.
- Candidate: remove the six nonfunctional floor fields; retain an outer rim, divider roots, two full support rails per cell and the Full identity land.
- Do not thin walls, rails, adapter rings, label floors or identity residual walls to gain savings.
- Accept only if the selected host remains one watertight component, all protected dimensions pass and CAD volume is at least 20% below the baseline.
