# Research validation

Decision: proceed with a measured-interface digital prototype. Demand and market fit remain hypotheses until a German conversion test and physical collector trial exist.

- Leuchtturm currently sells a broad round/square capsule system and states that its square capsules use a common `50 x 50 x 6.25 mm` external format for coin diameters `14–41 mm`: https://www.leuchtturm.de/quadrum-muenzkapseln.html
- Its current accessory catalog lists boxes, trays, cases, sheets and archives for the `50 x 50 mm` square format, demonstrating an active storage ecosystem rather than a one-off item: https://www.leuchtturm.com/coins-bars-banknotes/coin-capsules/accessories-for-square-coin-capsules-quadrum/
- A current German marketplace listing offers a 20-cell capsule tray at `22.49 EUR`; the dated review signal is small but positive: https://www.kaufland.de/product/496490383/
- A reviewed 50-capsule box shows the core extraction failure: users describe fit as too snug and removal difficult, especially between adjacent capsules or for limited finger dexterity: https://onfireguy.com/products/guardhouse-double-row-coin-capsule-box-for-50-xl-i-capsules
- Another reviewed capsule box reports insufficient finger spacing, ambiguous holder-size identification, and inconsistent fit among superficially similar capsules: https://www.ebay.com/p/11011036071
- A 46 mm generic capsule/storage-box offer demonstrates a currently sold large round target, but its fit and material claims are not treated as authoritative: https://beta.daytonhoopla.com/products/84-pieces-46-mm-coin-capsules-with-foam-gasket-and-plastic-s/223422828/

Product response: a six-cell open-lattice drawer cassette with top-open access notches; direct large-square cells; measured, drop-in 50 mm square and 46 mm round adapter variants; paper-label bays; and an exact three-interface gauge. Compatibility is stated by measured outer envelope only. Bare collectibles are excluded because PLA is not qualified as an archival or non-abrasive coin-contact material.
