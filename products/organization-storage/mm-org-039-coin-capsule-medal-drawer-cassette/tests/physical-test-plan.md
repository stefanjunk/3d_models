# Physical test plan

1. Print the exact interface/label gauge and Full plus Micro identity coupons with the intended process.
2. Measure actual capsule outer width/diameter and height. Pass only if the selected capsule enters the matching gauge under gravity/light fingertip pressure and leaves without levering.
3. Insert a sacrificial or low-value protective capsule 100 times; inspect both capsule and adapter under strong oblique light for scratches, haze, stress marks or edge bites.
4. Test a `34 x 7.5 mm` paper label in the bay for insertion, retrieval, tearing, curling and transfer marks. Do not let paper touch a bare collectible.
5. Print one selected adapter and test host-cell clearance before printing the remaining five. It must seat and lift without force, rocking or wedging.
6. Load six encapsulated inert weights matching intended masses. Run 100 drawer cycles and 20 gentle fingertip removal/replacement cycles per position.
7. Verify no capsule is trapped between neighbors, every front notch permits removal, the host stays flat, rails and walls remain intact, and drawer closure clears the loaded height.
8. Verify Full host and Micro adapter marks are complete/readable. Physical print, fit/function, appearance, safety and commercial release remain human approvals.
