# Purpose — MM-ORG-040 Field Notes Original Kraft memo-book station

MM-ORG-040 is a prospective, digital-first desk station for one closed Field Notes Original Kraft memo book with the published 89 × 140 mm planform. The intended product registers the book against two loose, non-clamping desk datums so it can be placed repeatedly for adult indoor writing or display use.

This revision is intake and preflight only. It contains no concept image, CAD, source geometry, mesh, manufacturing export, G-code, physical-test result, compatibility release, or commercial-release approval.

The design scope excludes archival protection, impact protection, security, child-directed use, wall or vehicle mounting, and any claim for another Field Notes edition. Thickness is deliberately not a fit interface; cover bow, corner wear, edition variation, direct measurement, physical fit, marking, and stability remain open verification items.
