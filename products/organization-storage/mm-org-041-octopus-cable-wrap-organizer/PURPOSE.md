# Purpose — MM-ORG-041 Octopus cable wrap organizer

## Intended use

Hold and separate several **slack low-voltage USB and audio desk cables** in open
retention channels formed by the curled tentacles of a decorative octopus body
standing on a dry indoor desk or shelf. The product is a desk organizer with a
generated decorative envelope, not a figurine and not an electrical accessory.

## Who it is for

Adults organizing their own desk: desk-setup enthusiasts, tech workers, gamers
and home-office users. One-handed cable routing without tools.

## What it is not

- Not a measured animal model. The body is an **own AI-generated proposal**; no
  animal anatomy was measured and no scanned or third-party original was used.
- Not a toy. Explicitly not for children under 3 years; no small-parts,
  mouthing, or child-safety scope.
- Not an electrical accessory. No strain relief, minimum-bend-radius protection,
  insulation, EMC or power-delivery-heat function. Mains cables and damaged
  cables are out of scope.
- Not load-bearing, not for wall, vehicle, outdoor, wet or food-contact use.
- Not commercially releasable while the product-local image rights, physical
  qualification, disclosure, outgoing-licence, market and human approval gates
  remain open. New geometry-only Step1X runs are `WARN`, not `PASS`; see
  `commercial-clearance/05-clearance/RIGHTS-CLEARANCE.md`.

## Why additive manufacturing

The decorative envelope is generated while the working retention channels are
cut parametrically. That per-order pairing of a free-form appearance shell with
exact functional geometry is what mass production cannot vary.

## Current state

Digital candidate at revision 0.2.0. The active run-005 chain was regenerated on
the cleaned, geometry-only fork, then registered, flat-based and cut with six
CAD-owned cable channels. The selected 100k-face manufacturing candidate is one
watertight component and passes exact-profile slicing. **No physical print, no
measured cable, no retention test and no stability result exists.** Cable outer
diameters are a declared design range, not evidence. No fit or compatibility
wording may be used yet.

Revision 0.1.0 and its run-002 artifacts are retained as historical evidence.
They predate the decoder cleanup and include a textured output, so they remain
blocked and are not part of the active commercial derivation.
