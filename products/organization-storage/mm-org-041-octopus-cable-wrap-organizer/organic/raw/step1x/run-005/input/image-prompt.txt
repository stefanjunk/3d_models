Sacrificial preform plate for MM-ORG-041 Octopus cable wrap organizer.
Step1X-3D input. One image only. Read alongside preflight/interfaces/IF-INT-GEO-CON-BODY-001.json.

PROMPT
------
Create a closed, thick, single-piece preform for a stylized cartoon octopus
desk object that sits on a flat bottom.

Primary form: one rounded dome-like head/body mass, low and wide, sitting on a
broad flat base. Eight thick tentacles emerge low on the body and curl outward
and slightly upward in a roughly radial arrangement, each tentacle tapering only
gently so it stays solid and chunky along its whole length. Four of the
tentacles curl so their inner faces form generous open C-shaped hollows facing
outward; keep those hollows as smooth solid stock, wide and shallow.

Keep generous solid stock inside every tentacle curl where cable channels will
later be cut. Do not depict, model or suggest the channels, slots, grooves,
pinch lips, notches or any retention feature; they will be cut parametrically.

Protected visible surfaces: the head dome and the outer tentacle surfaces.
Keep them smooth, matte and free of fine texture.

Avoid thin sheets, membranes between tentacles, floating pieces, suckers,
undercut spirals, sharp tips and deep texture noise. Every tentacle tip must
end as a rounded blunt mass, never a thin point.

The hidden back should be deliberately simple and compatible with later
engineering. The bottom must read as one flat continuous seating plane.

Canonical three-quarter view at eye level or slightly above, object centered and
fully visible with about 8% clear margin, minimal near-orthographic perspective,
front = the side where two tentacle curls face the viewer, up = the head dome.
Matte neutral clay material, broad diffuse studio lighting, readable concavities.

No text, logo, eyes detail, fastener details, cables, wires, scenery, stand,
floor, ruler, arrow, annotation, cast shadow or extra object.
Isolated on a fully transparent background; clean alpha edge, no halo or
checkerboard. Square output, 1024 x 1024.

CONSTRAINTS THAT MUST NOT ENTER THE IMAGE
-----------------------------------------
No metric dimensions, no clearances, no wall thicknesses, no flat "datum"
claims. Those live in the interface contracts and are implemented in CAD.

WHY A PREFORM AND NOT A WHOLE OBJECT
------------------------------------
The cable channels are the product's function and its weakest evidence link.
They stay CAD-owned. Step1X supplies massing and surface character only.
