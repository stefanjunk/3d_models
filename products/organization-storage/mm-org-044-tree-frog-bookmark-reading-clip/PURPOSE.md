# MM-ORG-044 — Tree Frog bookmark reading clip

This product is an adult reading accessory: an original, stylised tree frog peeks over a book edge while a CAD-owned spring blade marks the page.

The Step1X stage owns only the appearance-led frog body. The page-contacting blade, backer, edge radii, thickness and retention geometry remain parametric CAD features. The 0.8 mm blade mentioned in the research portfolio is an unqualified concept value and is not approved for manufacture.

Out of scope are child-toy use, famous characters or likenesses, archival-paper claims, safety-critical retention and any commercial offer before physical page-marking, retention and cycle tests pass.
