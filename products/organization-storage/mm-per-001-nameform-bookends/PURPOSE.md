# Purpose — Nameform Split Pair Bookends

A coordinated pair of distinct left and right bookends. Books stand between the two ends along X. Each part has a vertical side blade beside the books, a thin inward foot beneath the books, and a front-facing letter-dominant facade without a rectangular wing. The left and right text halves read as one name from the front; neither text half is mirrored. A recessed rear web only joins the glyphs; the side blade and foot retain the book load independently of the decorative glyphs.
