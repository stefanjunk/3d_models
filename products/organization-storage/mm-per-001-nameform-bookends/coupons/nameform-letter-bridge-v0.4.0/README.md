# Full-height FA wood/bridge coupon — NameForm 0.4.0

This is the mandatory transfer coupon for the approved letter-only NameForm
architecture. It is not a miniature plaque: `F` and `A` use the same 122 mm cap
height, font, 6 mm body, rear connector, 0.45 mm texture grid, and upright print
direction as the product.

Print the textured candidate STL with the recorded Kobra 3 Max 0.4 mm / 0.12 mm
profile. Do not scale it in the slicer. The textured front faces `-Y` in model
coordinates; place the supplied bed datum at `z=0` and use no generated support.

The artifact remains `DRAFT` until the physical evaluation records the exact
filament and confirms wood visibility, open counter/gap, and connector handling.
