# NameForm reinforced FA bridge coupon v0.4.1

This `FA` coupon is the process-matched physical gate for the reinforced
letter-to-letter connection. It uses the same 12 mm glyph depth, connector at
`y=10..14 mm`, 2 mm true depth overlap, 12 mm local bridge band, and unchanged
candidate-C front relief as the complete MARITA draft.

The STL is one watertight body (120,874 triangles), and the exact Kobra 3 Max
0.4 mm / 0.12 mm / Anycubic PETG slice passes with 1,016 layers, a 4 h 39 min
estimate, and 23.043 m filament. The native slicer reports a floating
cantilever; inspect the entire layer preview before printing.

No upload or print start was performed. Record the physical result in
`physical-evaluation.md` before accepting the complete pair.
