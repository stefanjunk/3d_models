# MARITA print handoff — DRAFT

Print the two STLs separately and upright on their existing `z=0` datum. Keep
the textured glyph fronts facing `-Y`. Use the exact Kobra 3 Max 0.4 mm machine,
0.12 mm Standard process, and Anycubic PETG profiles recorded by the run02 slice
reports. Generated support remains disabled to match the candidate-C evidence.

Before printing, inspect every layer in the slicer UI. Confirm the first layer,
letter feet, A counters, I/T crossbars, local rear bridges, seam placement, and
the `RITA` floating-cantilever warning. Stop if a bridge or glyph island begins
without an acceptable underlying path.

After printing, record the exact filament product, color, batch, conditioning,
machine/firmware state, and deviations from the hashed profiles in
`physical-evaluation.md`. Do not treat the digital PASS as appearance,
connector-strength, load, or release approval.
