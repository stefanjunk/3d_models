# MARITA reinforced print handoff — DRAFT

Prefer the smaller reinforced FA coupon before committing to the full pair.
Print upright on the existing `z=0` datum with the textured glyph front facing
`-Y`. Use the exact recorded Kobra 3 Max 0.4 mm machine, 0.12 mm Standard
process, and Anycubic PETG profiles. Generated support remains disabled to
match the accepted candidate-C process.

Before any print, inspect every layer in the slicer UI. Confirm the first
layer, letter feet, A/R counters, I/T crossbars, 12 mm local rear bridge bands,
seam placement, and each native floating-cantilever warning. Stop if a glyph,
crossbar, or connector island begins without an acceptable underlying path.

After printing the FA coupon, record the exact filament product, color, batch,
conditioning, machine/firmware state, and profile deviations in its physical
evaluation record. Accept the complete pair only after the coupon keeps the
wood appearance and survives the defined handling/deflection checks.

The G-code in this run is evidence, not an instruction to upload or start the
printer. The models are unwatermarked DRAFT artifacts and not a release.
