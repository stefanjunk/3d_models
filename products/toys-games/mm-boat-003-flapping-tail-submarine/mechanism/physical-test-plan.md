# Physical test plan for reused principles

1. Print library samples 001/002/003 and 077/078/079 unchanged in the final
   PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer profile.
2. Record insertion force, required deburring and free play. Select the real
   hinge variant; do not assume sample 002 wins.
3. Print the product hinge coupon and one nose-to-segment joint. Cycle it 100,
   1,000 and 10,000 times in air; measure play and inspect pin/lug cracking.
4. Repeat 1,000 cycles after 24 h water immersion and after drying.
5. Print a capsule mouth and cap coupon with the final O-ring. Record closing
   torque and leakage at 0.2 m and 1.0 m water depth for 30 minutes.
6. Run 100 open/close cycles, then repeat the leakage test and inspect the
   bayonet stops, O-ring and groove whitening/wear.
7. Operate the motor for 30 minutes submerged. Check cap rotation, gland leak,
   shaft-sleeve slip, temperature and crank-slot wear.
8. Promote an interface to `qualified-local` only with printer, material lot,
   nozzle, profile, geometry commit and measured results recorded.
