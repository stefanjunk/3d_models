# Purpose — Trailcam B2 Balance Fpv Rover

A two-wheel, single-axis FPV camera rover that actively balances its body as an inverted pendulum. Two independently driven coaxial wheels provide pitch stabilization, forward/reverse motion and differential yaw steering. A protected front camera, independent control/video links and serviceable electronics remain visually and functionally related to TrailCam CF10.
