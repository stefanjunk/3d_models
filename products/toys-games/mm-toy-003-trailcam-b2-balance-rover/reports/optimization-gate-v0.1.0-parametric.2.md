# Optimization gate — 0.1.0-parametric.2

Status: **PENDING / no print-time or material optimization applied**

The geometry change is requirement-driven mass redistribution, not a claimed
lightweighting result. The revised source raises the battery and electronics
tiers and extends their protective frame so the approved whole-system COM band
passes. It does not select a print-time/material optimum.

Exact print time, filament and support comparisons remain unavailable because
the complete Anycubic machine, process and filament JSON profiles are not yet
frozen. The DRAFT meshes retain direct CadQuery tessellation; no lossy mesh
simplification was applied.

Protected invariants remain the common axle datum, motor/bracket seats,
fastener ligaments, ±12 mm battery trim, IMU datum, wheel/cable/tool keep-outs,
landing load paths, camera field and the now-passing whole-system mass/COM
contract. Optimization may resume only with an exact-slicer baseline and must
re-run the mass, geometry, interface, controller-correlation and physical-test
gates.
