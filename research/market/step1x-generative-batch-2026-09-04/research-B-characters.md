## SECTION 2 — IP RULE

- SAFE: self-authored generic fantasy classes and folkloric archetypes (wizard, goblin, gargoyle, knight, ninja, valkyrie, treant, mushroom sprite, astronaut, mech) are sellable, because the platform rules only forbid infringing third-party rights and falsely claiming someone else's model as "Original" — they do not restrict generic archetypes; such archetype models already rank on their own without any named character (e.g. MakerWorld's "Croakus the Frog Wizard" 2.6k downloads, "Fast 5 Goblin Supportless Miniature Pack" 3.7k, "Cathedral Gargoyle Monitor Buddy" 3.5k). [S68][S62]
- FORBIDDEN: named characters, franchise names and franchise trade dress — the visible top ranks of "chibi" supply on both MakerWorld and Cults3D are licensed IP (Pokémon, Marvel, One Piece, Stranger Things, Mario, Final Fantasy, Undertale), i.e. exactly the inventory that rightsholders target; MakerWorld prohibits "Infringement of copyrights, trademarks, patents and/or other intellectual property rights". [S62][S63][S68]
- FORBIDDEN: "compatible with", "proxy for", or system-name positioning even on original sculpts. Games Workshop operates a stated "zero tolerance policy" covering counterfeit models, imitation models and models that are "illegally re-cast or scanned", and it drove a 3D-model creator earning roughly $9,712/month on Patreon out of business via 12 Copyright Claims Board filings over 14 works — with no ruling ever issued, so the deterrent works regardless of legal merit. Note that MakerWorld itself hosts listings using such wording (e.g. "Colossus Heavy Mech Walker - TTRPG (40k Proxy)", 539 downloads) — platform tolerance is not legal safety. [S69][S62]
- FORBIDDEN: commercial "fan art". Games Workshop's guidelines permit fan artwork only if it is non-commercial, carries no advertising revenue, is distributed only as free digital files, is marked unofficial and uses no logos — so no paid-file or printed-goods fan art is available under that carve-out. Cults3D listings that sell fan-made character models explicitly shift the risk to the buyer, confirming those files are not cleared for commercial use. [S69][S63]
- LICENCE HYGIENE: do not build metriMade SKUs on downloaded third-party models. Popular archetype references are frequently CC BY-NC-ND with a separate paid merchant licence required (e.g. Cults3D's "Baby Dragon Dice Guardian", 5.2k downloads, is CC BY-NC-ND), and MakerWorld will take down any "remix or re-upload that violates the license of its source models" and does not accept scaling/cropping/recolouring as a remix. [S63][S68]
- AI RULES (found): MakerWorld requires that "When uploading models or posts that generated by AI, please ensure that you clearly identify and flag the AI generated content" — so metriMade's AI-assisted sculpts must be flagged at upload. Cults3D's NoAI policy prohibits reproducing or exploiting Cults-hosted content "through any artificial intelligence service" without author permission — so no Cults models may be used as AI training or image-to-3D input. Market-side, MakerWorld creators already use "(No AI)" as a title badge on top-performing originals (e.g. "Meccha Chameleon Characters - All OG Poses (No AI)", 54.2k downloads / 17.7k boosts), so disclosure is a positioning question, not only compliance. Etsy's own AI and IP policy texts are UNVERIFIED (etsy.com blocked automated access). [S68][S63][S62]
